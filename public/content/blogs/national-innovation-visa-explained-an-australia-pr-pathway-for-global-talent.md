## National Innovation Visa (Subclass 858): Eligibility, Benefits & Australia PR Guide

Australia continues to attract highly talented individuals who can contribute to its economy, research sector, innovation ecosystem, and global reputation. One of the most significant pathways available for exceptional individuals is the National Innovation Visa (Subclass 858).

Unlike many traditional migration pathways, this visa is not based on occupation lists, points tests, or employer sponsorship. Instead, it focuses on recognising internationally distinguished individuals who can make a significant contribution to Australia.

So, if you are a researcher, entrepreneur, investor, creative professional, athlete, or highly skilled expert with recognised achievements, this visa may offer a direct pathway to Australia PR.

## What Is the National Innovation Visa (Subclass 858)?

The [National Innovation Visa (NIV)](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/individual/employer-sponsored-visa/) is a permanent residency visa designed for exceptionally talented individuals who have an internationally recognised record of outstanding achievement.

The visa replaced the former Global Talent Independent (GTI) Program and now operates under a more targeted framework focused on attracting individuals who can strengthen Australia's future industries and national priorities.

Successful applicants receive Australian permanent residency from the outset.

## Key Benefits of the National Innovation Visa

**Some of the major advantages of this visa include:**

* Direct permanent residency

* No points test

* No occupation list restriction

* No age limit (although age may be considered where relevant)

* Ability to include eligible family members

* Freedom to live and work anywhere in Australia

* Access to Medicare and other permanent resident benefits

## How Does the Application Process Work?

The National Innovation Visa follows an invitation-based process.

### **Step 1: Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI)**

Applicants must first submit an EOI outlining their achievements, qualifications, recognition, and contribution to their field.

### **Step 2: Receive an Invitation**

The Department of Home Affairs assesses EOIs and may issue invitations to candidates who demonstrate exceptional merit and alignment with Australia's priorities.

### **Step 3: Lodge the Visa Application**

Only invited candidates can proceed with the visa application.

### **Step 4: Visa Assessment and Decision**

The Department assesses eligibility, achievements, nomination requirements, health, character, and other legislative criteria before making a final decision.

## Is a Nominator Required?

Yes. A nomination is mandatory for the National Innovation Visa (Subclass 858).

At the time of application, you must submit a completed Nomination for National Innovation Visa (Form 1000). This form confirms your internationally recognised achievements and must be endorsed by a nominator with a strong national reputation in the same field.

Your nominator must be one of the following:

* An Australian citizen

* An Australian permanent resident

* An eligible New Zealand citizen

* An Australian organisation

## FAQs

### **1. What is the National Innovation Visa in Australia?**

It is a permanent residency visa for highly talented individuals who can contribute to Australia’s economy through achievements in areas like research, business, tech, sports, and creative industries.

### **2. Can I apply for the National Innovation Visa directly?**

No. You must first submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) and receive an invitation from the Department of Home Affairs before you can apply.

### **3. Who is eligible for the National Innovation Visa?**

It is for individuals with exceptional or emerging talent, including researchers, entrepreneurs, investors, athletes, and creative professionals with strong proven achievements.

### **4. What are the main benefits of the National Innovation Visa?**

It offers direct permanent residency, no points test, no occupation list restriction, and allows eligible family members to be included in the application.

### **5. Is a nomination required for the National Innovation Visa?**

Yes. A completed Form 1000 nomination is required from an eligible Australian citizen, permanent resident, eligible New Zealand citizen, or an Australian organisation with a national reputation in your field.
