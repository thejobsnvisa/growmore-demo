Not every pathway to Australia PR follows the standard skilled migration route that requires competitive points to receive invitations. There are special employer-sponsored migration pathways like the Designated Area Migration Agreements (DAMAs) that offer eligible skilled workers greater flexibility to get Australia PR through regional employers, occupation concessions, and minimal visa requirements.

The Far North Queensland (FNQ) DAMA is one of these regional agreements that is designed to help employers in Far North Queensland fill ongoing skill shortages by sponsoring overseas workers for eligible occupations through Australia's employer-sponsored migration program.

In this guide, you will learn how the FNQ DAMA works, what the benefits are, what concessions are available, and how it can become a practical pathway to [Australia permanent residency (PR)](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/pr-pathway-in-australia-right-now-2026/) for skilled workers.

## What Is the FNQ DAMA?

The Far North Queensland Designated Area Migration Agreement (FNQ DAMA) is a regional labour agreement between the Australian Government and the Cairns Chamber of Commerce, which acts as the Designated Area Representative (DAR) for the region.

The agreement helps address ongoing workforce shortages across Far North Queensland by allowing eligible regional employers to hire skilled overseas workers when suitable Australian workers are not available.

## What are the Benefits of the FNQ DAMA?

One of the biggest advantages of the FNQ DAMA is that it provides flexibility and concessions that are often not available through standard skilled migration pathways. It includes:

### **1. Access to a Larger Occupation List**

FNQ DAMA includes a region-specific occupation list with significantly more occupations than many standard [skilled migration programs](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/individual/general-skilled-visa/), without any extra restrictions.

### **2. Opportunities for Skilled and Semi-Skilled Workers**

Some migration pathways mainly focus on [ANZSCO Skill](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/anzsco-codes-for-australia-pr-everything-skilled-workers-need-to-know/) Levels 1–3, while the FNQ DAMA includes occupations across Skill Levels 1–5. This creates migration opportunities for workers in a broader range of industries, including several semi-skilled occupations seeking employer-sponsored jobs in Australia.

### **3. Unique Local Occupations**

The agreement can include occupations that are specific to the Far North Queensland region, even if they are not formally classified under ANZSCO.

### **4. Higher Age Limits**

Under the FNQ DAMA, eligible applicants access age concessions of 50 or 55 years based on their occupation.

### **5. English Language Concessions**

Certain occupations may also qualify for reduced English language requirements.

### **6. Salary Concessions**

For eligible Skill Level 3–5 occupations, employers may access salary concessions where the market salary is below the standard income threshold.

### **7. Work Experience Concessions**

The FNQ DAMA also offers concessions relating to work experience requirements for eligible occupations.

### **8. Additional Advantages**

Apart from these main advantages, it also offers flexible labour market testing requirements, priority processing in many cases, labour agreements valid for up to five years, and the ability to add additional occupations and positions during the agreement period.

## Visa Pathways Available Under the FNQ DAMA

**Eligible employers can nominate overseas workers through several employer-sponsored visa pathways, including:**

- Skills in Demand (SID) Visa ([Subclass 482](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/subclass-482-visa-consultant-in-australia/))
- [Employer Nomination Scheme (Subclass 186)](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/ens-186-direct-entry-the-smartest-route-to-secure-australia-pr/)
- [Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional Visa (Subclass 494)](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/employers/standard-business-sponsorship/)

For eligible workers sponsored under the Subclass 494 pathway, permanent residency may later be available through the Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional) Visa ([Subclass 191](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/difference-between-subclass-491-and-subclass-190/)) after meeting the legislative requirements.

## Who Can Access the FNQ DAMA?

The FNQ DAMA is employer driven. Before an overseas worker can apply for a visa, an employer must first obtain access to the agreement.

The designated region includes local government areas such as Cairns, Douglas, Mareeba, Tablelands, Cassowary Coast, Cook, Croydon, Etheridge, Torres, Northern Peninsula Area and the Weipa Town Authority.

## How Does the FNQ DAMA Process Work?

Unlike standard employer sponsorship, the FNQ DAMA follows a structured four-step process.

### **Step 1: Employer Endorsement**

Employers first apply for endorsement through the Cairns Chamber of Commerce, which is the Designated Area Representative for the FNQ DAMA. In this application, the employer can cover one or multiple occupations and positions.

Endorsement requests are generally assessed within 10 business days if the application is complete.

### **Step 2: Labour Agreement**

Once endorsed, employers submit a Labour Agreement request through the Department of Home Affairs' ImmiAccount system. A Labour Agreement is generally valid for five years and can be updated if additional occupations or positions are required.

Importantly, employers do not need to identify overseas workers at this stage.

### **Step 3: Nomination**

Once the Labour Agreement is approved, employers can nominate overseas workers for the approved occupations. Here, workers may be either onshore or offshore at the time of nomination.

Immediate family members may also be included in the visa application where eligible.

### **Step 4: Visa Application**

After the nomination, the overseas worker submits the relevant visa application. Depending on the visa stream, temporary or permanent visas will be granted.

## FNQ DAMA Endorsement Fees

The Cairns Chamber of Commerce charges endorsement fees for employers who are seeking access to the agreement.

**Current fees include:**

- $885 per position for Cairns Chamber members
- $985 per position for non-members
- $275 per position to roll over unused positions from an existing agreement
- $275 per occupation for certain minor variations to existing agreements

These endorsement fees are separate from Australian Government visa application charges, which vary depending on the visa subclass.

## FAQs

### **1. What is the FNQ DAMA?**

The FNQ DAMA is a regional labour agreement that allows eligible employers in Far North Queensland to sponsor overseas skilled and semi-skilled workers for approved occupations.

### **2. Can the FNQ DAMA lead to permanent residency?**

It depends on the visa pathway and your eligibility. Eligible workers may have a pathway to Australian permanent residency after meeting the requirements of their nominated visa.

### **3. Is employer sponsorship required for the FNQ DAMA?**

Yes. The FNQ DAMA is an employer-sponsored program, so you must have sponsorship from an eligible employer.

### **4. Who is eligible for the FNQ DAMA?**

Eligible overseas workers nominated by approved employers for occupations on the FNQ DAMA occupation list can apply, provided they meet the relevant visa requirements.

### **5. What occupations are available under the FNQ DAMA?**

The FNQ DAMA includes a broad range of skilled and semi-skilled occupations across multiple industries, with the list updated based on regional workforce needs.
