## Myth 1: You Need a Job Offer to Apply for Australia PR

### **Reality**

A job offer is not mandatory for all migration pathways. If you are applying through an employer-sponsored pathway, a job offer from an approved sponsor is generally required.

However, many skilled migration pathways let applicants to apply without a job offer. [Subclass 189 Skilled Independent Visa](https://www.growmore.one/news/is-your-eoi-ready-subclass-189-invitation-round-confirmed-for-4-june-2026/),[Subclass 190 Skilled Nominated Visa](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/australian-visa-processing-times-update-february-2026-vs-april-2026/), and [Subclass 491 Skilled Work Regional Visa](https://www.growmore.one/news/is-your-eoi-ready-subclass-189-invitation-round-confirmed-for-4-june-2026/) are some of the visas that allow eligible applicants to apply without a job offer.

Here, eligibility depends on factors such as your occupation, skills assessment, points score, English proficiency, and state nomination (where applicable).

## Myth 2: 65 Points Guarantees an Invitation

### **Reality**

A score of 65 points is the minimum eligibility requirement for many skilled visas. However, invitations are generally issued to the most competitive candidates within each occupation and invitation round.

So, the invitation depends on your occupation demand, state nomination requirements, invitation round quotas, and individual profile strength.

## Myth 3: Your Occupation Must Appear on One Specific Occupation List

### **Reality**

Many applicants assume they are not eligible for Australia PR because their occupation does not appear on a particular occupation list. While occupation lists play an important role in skilled migration, they are not the only factor that determines eligibility.

Australia offers several alternative migration pathways, including Labour Agreements such as Company-Specific Labour Agreements, Industry Labour Agreements, and [Designated Area Migration Agreements (DAMAs)](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/dama). These pathways can provide opportunities for skilled workers whose occupations may not be available through standard skilled migration programs.

As a result, being absent from one occupation list does not automatically make you ineligible for Australia PR.

## Myth 4: A Positive Skills Assessment Means PR Is Guaranteed

### **Reality**

A skills assessment is an important milestone, but it is only one step in the migration process. A positive skills assessment does not directly give you any visa approval.

Applicants may still need to satisfy other criteria, such as points requirements, English language requirements, health requirements, character requirements, state nomination criteria, and visa-specific eligibility requirements.

## Myth 5: Every Occupation Follows the Same PR Process

### **Reality**

Different occupations are assessed by different authorities and may have completely different requirements.

**For example:**

* Engineers may deal with Engineers Australia.  
* IT professionals may undergo ACS assessments.  
* Accountants may be assessed by accounting authorities.  
* Healthcare occupations often have additional registration requirements.

## Myth 6: IELTS Is the Only English Test Accepted for Australia PR

### **Reality**

Many applicants believe IELTS is the only option available. But in reality, Australia accepts several English language tests for migration purposes, including:

* C1 Advanced  
* CELPIP General  
* LANGUAGECERT Academic  
* MET  
* PTE Academic  
* TOEFL iBT  
* OET (for eligible occupations)

Choosing the test that best suits your strengths may help maximise your points score.

## Myth 7: Australia Is Reducing Opportunities for Skilled Migrants

### **Reality**

Australia still relies heavily on skilled migrants to fill workforce shortages across many industries.

While migration policies and selection criteria continue to evolve, skilled migration remains a major part of Australia's long-term plans. The focus is not on reducing opportunities but on attracting applicants with skills that are in demand.

Candidates with strong occupations, good English, relevant work experience, and competitive profiles continue to receive invitations and state nominations.

## FAQs

### **1. Can I get Australia PR without a job offer?**

Yes. Many skilled migration visas, including Subclass 189, 190, and 491, do not require a job offer. Eligibility is generally based on your occupation, skills assessment, English proficiency, and points score.

### **2. Is Australia PR difficult to obtain?**

Australia PR can be competitive, but many skilled workers successfully migrate each year. Success depends on factors such as occupation, points score, English proficiency, work experience, and choosing the right pathway.

### **3. What if my occupation is not on a particular occupation list?**

You may still have options through employer-sponsored visas, Labour Agreements, or DAMA pathways. Being absent from one list does not automatically make you ineligible.

### **4. Does a positive skills assessment guarantee Australia PR?**

No. A skills assessment is only one requirement. You must also meet other visa criteria, including English, health, character, and nomination requirements where applicable.

### **5. Is Australia still inviting skilled migrants?**

Yes. Australia continues to rely on skilled migration to address workforce shortages, and invitations are regularly issued across various occupations and visa pathways.
