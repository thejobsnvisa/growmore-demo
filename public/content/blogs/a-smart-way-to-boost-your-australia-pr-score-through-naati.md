## What Is NAATI?

The National Accreditation Authority for Translators and Interpreters (NAATI) is Australia's national standards and certifying authority for translators and interpreters.

For migration purposes, NAATI offers the Credentialed Community Language (CCL) test, which allows eligible applicants to claim additional points under Australia's [General Skilled Migration (GSM) program](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/gsm-general-skilled-migration).

Successfully passing the CCL test provides 5 additional PR points and strengthens your overall EOI ranking during invitation rounds.

## Why Are These 5 Points Important?

[Australia's skilled migration system](https://www.growmore.one/points-calculator/) operates on a points-based model. While 65 points is the minimum eligibility requirement, in reality, receiving an invitation often requires a significantly higher score due to competition.

This is where NAATI becomes strategically important.

By passing the NAATI CCL test, eligible applicants can claim an additional 5 points towards their Australia PR score.

For example, an applicant with 75 points may move to 80 points through NAATI, which can significantly improve their ranking in invitation rounds.

## What Is the NAATI CCL Test?

The Credentialed Community Language (CCL) test is designed to assess your ability to translate conversations between English and another language.

Unlike professional translator qualifications, this test is designed specifically for migration purposes, not professional certification.

During the examination, candidates listen to recorded dialogues and interpret them between English and their chosen language. The focus is on practical communication rather than advanced translation expertise.

## How Are the NAATI CCL Tests Assessed?

* The NAATI CCL test is assessed by trained and impartial examiners using a mark deduction system.

* Each dialogue is worth 45 marks, making a total of 90 marks for the test.

* Candidates begin with a full score of 90, and marks are deducted based on interpretation errors made during the test.

**To pass the NAATI CCL test, candidates must score:**

* At least 63 marks overall  
* A minimum of 29 marks in each dialogue  

## How to Prepare for the NAATI CCL Test?

Many candidates underestimate the test because they are fluent in two languages. However, the real challenge is accurate interpretation under time pressure.

**Here are key preparation strategies:**

* Practice interpreting both directions (English ↔ target language)  
* Focus on meaning, not word-for-word translation  
* Improve listening speed with real-life dialogue practice  
* Learn common vocabulary used in everyday Australian contexts  
* Practice with timed mock tests to simulate exam conditions  
* Avoid adding or removing information while interpreting  

## How Long Does It Take to Get NAATI CCL Results?

NAATI CCL results are typically released within a few weeks after the test date.

However, processing times may vary depending on exam volume.

Once you pass the test, you can immediately use your credential to claim 5 points for [Australia PR](https://www.growmore.one/) under the General Skilled Migration (GSM) points system.

## FAQs

### 1. What is the NAATI CCL test?

It is a language interpretation test that allows eligible candidates to gain 5 extra points for Australia PR by translating conversations between English and another language.

### 2. How many PR points can NAATI add?

Successful candidates receive 5 additional points under Australia’s General Skilled Migration points system.

### 3. Who can take the NAATI CCL test?

Anyone applying for a points-tested visa who is fluent in English and an approved community language can apply. No formal qualification is required.

### 4. Is the NAATI CCL test difficult?

It is not about advanced language skills, but about accurate interpretation. Candidates often find it challenging without proper practice and mock tests.

### 5. How long is the NAATI result valid for PR purposes?

Once you pass, the result can be used immediately for PR applications, as long as it is valid at the time of your EOI and invitation.
