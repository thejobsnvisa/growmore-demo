The Northern Territory continues to experience demand for skilled workers across industries such as healthcare, construction, hospitality, agriculture, and community services. To address these workforce shortages, the Australian and Northern Territory Governments have established the **Northern Territory Designated Area Migration Agreement (NT DAMA)**. 

It is a regional employer-sponsored migration program based on the Territory's labour market needs.Through this DAMA, eligible employers can sponsor overseas workers in occupations that may not be available under standard visa programs and benefit from certain concessions. 

So, if you are a skilled worker looking for employer sponsorship or exploring potential pathways to [Australia PR](https://www.growmore.one/), understanding **how the NT DAMA works is important**.

## What Is the Northern Territory DAMA?

The Northern Territory Designated Area Migration Agreement (**NT DAMA**) is an employer-sponsored migration program that covers the entire Northern Territory. Under this program, an approved NT employer can sponsor eligible skilled and semi-skilled overseas workers when they cannot fill a position with local workers.

## How Skilled Workers and Employers Can Use the NT DAMA?

Through this program, employers can access several visa pathways, including the [Subclass 482 Skills in Demand visa](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/how-to-apply-for-482-visa-australia-step-by-step-sid-visa-guide), the [Subclass 494 Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/employers/standard-business-sponsorship/), and the permanent Subclass 186 Employer Nomination Scheme visa to sponsor eligible overseas workers. It also covers some occupations that may not be available under standard employer-sponsored programs and offers certain concessions for eligible applicants.

However, workers cannot apply under the NT DAMA on their own. They can explore current employment opportunities through The Territory Jobs Portal before seeking sponsorship under the program.

## How NT DAMA Creates Pathways to Australia PR

### **Pathway 1: Skills in Demand (Subclass 482) Visa**

The Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand visa) is the first step for overseas workers entering Australia under the NT DAMA. This temporary employer-sponsored visa allows eligible workers to live and work in the Northern Territory for an approved employer. 

During this period, workers can gain Australian work experience and, if they become eligible, they may transition to employer-sponsored permanent residency pathways such as the Subclass 186 visa by meeting the relevant requirements.

### **Pathway 2: Employer Nomination Scheme (Subclass 186)**

The Subclass 186 Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS) visa offers a direct permanent residency pathway for eligible workers sponsored by an approved employer. Under the NT DAMA, eligible temporary visa holders who have worked in the Northern Territory in an approved occupation may be sponsored for permanent residency through the Subclass 186 visa. 

They just need to meet all occupation, qualification, experience, age, English language, and visa requirements, which includes many applicable concessions available under the NT DAMA.

### **Pathway 3: Subclass 494 to Subclass 191**

Workers sponsored under the [Subclass 494 Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/employers/standard-business-sponsorship/) may have a pathway to permanent residency through the [Subclass 191 Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional) visa](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/difference-between-subclass-491-and-subclass-190/).

This pathway allows eligible workers who live and work in regional Australia to apply for permanent residency once they meet the required visa conditions.

## What Concessions Are Available Under the NT DAMA?

One of the key reasons the NT DAMA attracts both employers and skilled workers is the range of concessions available for eligible occupations, including:

### **1. Higher Age Limits**
&nbsp;
While permanent employer-sponsored visas such as the Subclass 186 and Subclass 494 normally have an age limit of under 45, the NT DAMA can allow applicants in many occupations to be sponsored up to 55 years of age, or 50 years of age for some Skill Level 5 occupations.

### **2. Lower English Requirements**
&nbsp;
Some occupations qualify for reduced English requirements. Eligible applicants may be able to apply with an IELTS 4.5 overall (minimum 4.0 in each band) for Subclass 482 and Subclass 494 visas, or IELTS 5.0 overall (minimum 4.0 in each band) for the Subclass 186 visa.

### **3. Salary Concessions**
&nbsp;
In some cases, employers can access salary concessions where the Northern Territory market salary rate is below the standard income threshold, helping businesses fill critical workforce gaps while still meeting minimum salary requirements.

### **4. Access to Unique NT Occupations**
&nbsp;
The NT DAMA also includes several Northern Territory-specific occupations that are not available under the standard ANZSCO occupation framework, creating additional sponsorship opportunities for eligible workers.

## Could the NT DAMA Be Your Pathway to Australia PR?

## FAQs

### **1. Can the NT DAMA lead to Australia PR?**

Yes. Eligible workers may access permanent residency pathways through visas such as the Subclass 186 or the Subclass 191 visa.

### **2. Can I apply for the NT DAMA on my own?**

No. You must first secure a job with an approved Northern Territory employer who can sponsor you under the NT DAMA.

### **3. How many occupations are available under the NT DAMA?**

The NT DAMA currently includes around 325 occupations across a range of industries, including healthcare, construction, hospitality, and ICT.

### **4. Does the NT DAMA offer any concessions?**

Yes. Depending on the occupation, concessions may be available for age, English language requirements, and salary thresholds.

### **5. Which visas are available under the NT DAMA?**

The main visa pathways include the Subclass 482 Skills in Demand visa, Subclass 494 Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional visa, and Subclass 186 Employer Nomination Scheme visa.
