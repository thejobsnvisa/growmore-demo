If your visa has expired, been refused, or cancelled, your immigration status can quickly become uncertain. In some cases, leaving Australia immediately may not be possible due to ongoing immigration matters, detention, or pending visa decisions.

In such situations, the Bridging Visa E (BVE) may allow you to remain lawfully in Australia for a limited period while your immigration issues are being resolved. It is a temporary visa with no direct pathway to [permanent residency (PR)](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/australia-permanent-resident-visa-options-2026/).

## **What Is a Bridging Visa E (BVE)?**

A [Bridging Visa](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/what-is-a-bridging-visa-in-australia-types-purpose-common-myths/) E is a temporary visa that allows certain non-citizens to stay lawfully in Australia while they manage or resolve serious immigration matters.

**It is commonly used for individuals who are:**

* Resolving unlawful status in Australia
* Applying for or awaiting a visa decision
* Seeking merits review or judicial review
* Preparing to depart Australia
* Involved in ministerial intervention cases
* In immigration detention in specific circumstances

A BVE does not provide permanent residency or long-term legal status in Australia.

## **Types of Bridging Visa E (Subclass 050 & 051)**

**There are two main types of Bridging Visa E visas and both of them apply in different situations:**

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **Visa Subclass** | **Purpose** |
| Subclass 050 | For unlawful non-citizens, the current holder of a BVE 050 or the holder of a BVD 041 dealing with immigration matters |
| Subclass 051 | For certain eligible protection visa applicants in specific circumstances |

## **Bridging Visa E Subclass 050**

**Bridging Visa E (Subclass 050) is generally granted to people who:**

* Have become unlawful in Australia, or
* Were previously holding a Bridging Visa D (Subclass 041), or
* It has come to the attention of immigration authorities due to visa expiry or breach

This subclass mainly applies to individuals resolving their unlawful status in Australia.

## **Bridging Visa E Subclass 051**

Bridging Visa E (Subclass 051) is a more limited visa category designed specifically for certain eligible protection visa applicants.

**It generally applies to individuals who:**

* Are in immigration detention
* Have lodged a valid protection visa application that is not finally determined, OR are undergoing judicial review of a refusal decision
* Meet strict regulatory requirements related to eligibility, health, age, or legal criteria

## **How Long Can You Stay on a Bridging Visa E?**

A Bridging Visa E is not permanent.

**It remains valid:**

* Until a specific date, OR
* Until a specific event occurs (such as visa grant or departure), OR
* Until cancellation

## **Can a Bridging Visa E Be Extended?**

**A Bridging Visa E cannot be extended indefinitely. If your immigration matter remains unresolved, you may need to:**

* Apply for another BVE if eligible
* Explore alternative visa pathways
* Make departure arrangements

Each case is assessed based on individual immigration circumstances.

## **Common Misconceptions About Bridging Visa E**

### **Myth 1: It leads to permanent residency**

False. A BVE is only a temporary visa and does not provide any direct pathway to PR.

### **Myth 2: Everyone gets work rights**

False. Work rights depend on the visa conditions assigned at grant.

### **Myth 3: It can be extended indefinitely**

False. It is temporary and linked to specific immigration outcomes.

### **Myth 4: You can travel and return**

False. Leaving Australia ends the visa automatically.

## **FAQs**

###  **1. Can Bridging Visa E lead to permanent residency?**

No. It’s only a temporary visa and does not directly lead to PR.

###  **2. Do I automatically get work rights on a BVE?**

Not always. Work rights depend on the conditions given in your visa grant.

###  **3. How long can I stay on a Bridging Visa E?**

Until your immigration matter is decided, you depart Australia, or the visa is cancelled.

###  **4. Can I travel outside Australia on a BVE?**

No. If you leave Australia, your Bridging Visa E stops automatically.

###  **5. Can Bridging Visa E be extended?**

Not directly. You may need to apply for another BVE or explore other visa options depending on your case.
