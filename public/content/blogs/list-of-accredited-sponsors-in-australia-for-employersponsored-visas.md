## **What Is an Accredited Sponsor in Australia?**

An accredited sponsor is an Australian employer that holds Standard Business Sponsorship (SBS) approval and has met additional requirements set by the Australian Government.

All accredited sponsors must have demonstrated:

- A strong history of complying with Australian immigration laws.
- Genuine sponsorship practices.
- Good workplace and employment records.
- Ongoing business operations in Australia.
- Financial stability and compliance with sponsorship obligations.

By showing their full track record, accredited sponsors may receive priority processing benefits for certain employer-sponsored visa applications. So, as an overseas employee, getting a sponsorship from an accredited sponsor means getting the benefit of faster processing for your subclass 482/ subclass 186/ subclass 494 compared to standard business sponsorship.

## **Which Employer-Sponsored Visas Can Accredited Sponsors Support?**

Accredited Sponsors may sponsor eligible skilled workers under Australia's employer-sponsored visa program. Depending on the employer's requirements and your circumstances, this may include the following employer sponsored visas:

- Subclass 482 – Skills in Demand Visa
- Subclass 186 – Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS) Visa
- Subclass 494 – Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa

## **FAQs**

### **1\. What is an Accredited Sponsor in Australia?**

An Accredited Sponsor is an Australian employer that has met additional government requirements under the employer-sponsored visa program and is recognised as a low-risk sponsor.

### **2\. Which visas can Accredited Sponsors support?**

Eligible Accredited Sponsors may sponsor skilled workers under the Subclass 482, Subclass 186, and Subclass 494 visa pathways.

### **3\. Does being an Accredited Sponsor mean an employer is actively hiring overseas workers?**

No. Accredited Sponsor status does not guarantee that an employer is currently recruiting overseas workers.

### **4\. What are the benefits of being sponsored by an Accredited Sponsor?**

Accredited Sponsors may receive priority processing benefits for eligible employer-sponsored visa nominations.
