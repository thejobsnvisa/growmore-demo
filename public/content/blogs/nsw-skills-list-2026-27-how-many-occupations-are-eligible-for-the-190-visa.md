## How to Check Whether Your Occupation Qualifies for NSW Nomination 

Before making decisions about your Australian PR plans, follow these steps: 

**1.Identify your occupation's correct six-digit ANZSCO code.**

**2.Check whether the first four digits of that code match a unit group on the NSW Skills List.** 

**3.Verify that your specific occupation is eligible for the subclass 190 visa through the Department of Home Affairs.** 

**4.Review the skills assessment requirements for your occupation.** 

**5.Assess your points score, English proficiency and other applicable visa criteria.** 

**6.Review the latest NSW nomination requirements and program status before proceeding.** 

## What Should You Do If Your Occupation Is on the NSW Skills List? 

Finding your occupation within a listed unit group is a useful starting point, but it should not be the only factor in your migration planning. 

First, confirm that your occupation meets the relevant federal requirements. Then review your skills assessment, points score, English proficiency and eligibility for NSW nomination. Depending on your circumstances, it may also be worth exploring other Australian skilled migration pathways rather than relying exclusively on a NSW invitation.
