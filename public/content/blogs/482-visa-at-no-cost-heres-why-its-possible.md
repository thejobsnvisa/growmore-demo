Western Australia’s building and construction sector continues to rely on skilled workers for major infrastructure and development projects underway across the whole state. And to address workforce shortages required for this development, the State Government has introduced targeted migration initiatives that attract overseas talent and support businesses facing critical skill shortages.

The Construction Visa Subsidy Program (CVSP) is one such initiative that provides eligible applicants with financial support to help cover migration, visa, and relocation expenses.

So, if you're exploring a Subclass 482 visa opportunity in Western Australia's construction sector or planning to migrate as a skilled construction worker, the CVSP could help make the migration process more affordable and accessible.

## What is the Construction Visa Subsidy Program (CVSP)?

The Construction Visa Subsidy Program (CVSP) is a Western Australian Government initiative designed to attract skilled overseas workers to fill critical labour shortages in the state's building and construction sector.

Under the program, eligible applicants can receive financial assistance of up to AUD 10,000 to help cover expenses related to Western Australian migration.

**The subsidy can be used to offset costs such as:**

- Visa application fees
- Migration agent or migration representative fees
- Relocation expenses
- Costs associated with sponsoring overseas workers

**The program benefits both:**

- Western Australian employers sponsoring overseas workers.
- Independent skilled migrants applying through the WA State Nominated Migration Program.

## Who Can Apply for the Construction Visa Subsidy Program?

**The Construction Visa Subsidy Program is available under two streams:**

### **1. Employer Sponsored Stream**

Western Australian construction businesses may be eligible if they sponsor skilled overseas workers.

#### **Eligible Visa Subclasses**

**Employers sponsoring workers under the following visa subclasses may qualify:**

- Subclass 482 – Skills in Demand Visa (Core Skills stream)
- Subclass 494 – Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa
- Subclass 186 – Employer Nomination Scheme Visa

### **2. State Nominated Migration Program Stream**

Independent skilled migrants may also qualify for the subsidy.

#### **Eligible Visa Subclasses**

**Applicants must receive Western Australian State Nomination under:**

- Subclass 190 – Skilled Nominated Visa
- Subclass 491 – Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa
- Skilled Migrant Eligibility Requirements

## How Much Financial Support Can the CVSP Provide?

The Construction Visa Subsidy Program provides milestone-based payments.

### **For Western Australian Employers**

Eligible employers can receive up to AUD 10,000 per sponsored skilled migrant.

| Milestone | Payment |
|-----------|---------:|
| Milestone 1 | AUD 2,000 |
| Milestone 2 | AUD 4,000 |
| Milestone 3 | AUD 4,000 |
| **Total** | **AUD 10,000** |

### **For Offshore Skilled Migrants**

If you apply for your visa while living outside Australia, you may receive up to AUD 10,000.

| Milestone | Payment |
|-----------|---------:|
| Milestone 1 | AUD 5,000 |
| Milestone 2 | AUD 2,500 |
| Milestone 3 | AUD 2,500 |
| **Total** | **AUD 10,000** |

The higher first payment is designed to assist with relocation and migration-related expenses.

### **For Onshore Skilled Migrants**

Applicants already in Australia may receive reduced payments because relocation costs are generally lower.

| Milestone | Payment |
|-----------|---------:|
| Milestone 1 | AUD 2,500 |
| Milestone 2 | AUD 1,250 |
| Milestone 3 | AUD 1,250 |
| **Total** | **AUD 5,000** |

## How to Apply through Employer Sponsored Stream

- Submit Milestone 1 Claim: Complete the claim form, agree to the program terms, and confirm your intention to sponsor skilled migrants.
- Find a Skilled Migrant: Identify an eligible overseas worker for the role.
- Become an Approved Sponsor: Apply to the Department of Home Affairs to sponsor and nominate the worker.
- Lodge the Visa Application: The skilled migrant you are sponsoring applies for the relevant visa.
- Submit Milestone 2 Claim: After the visa is granted, provide the required visa and sponsorship documents to claim milestone payments.
- Submit Milestone 3 Claim: Once the worker starts employment, submit their employment contract and first payslip to receive the final payment.

## How to Apply through State Nominated Migration Program Stream

- Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI): Apply for Western Australian State Nomination through SkillSelect.
- Find a WA Employer: Secure employment with an eligible WA construction employer.
- Register Your Visa Approval: Provide your visa and arrival details to Migration Services.
- Submit Milestone 1 Claim: Once you start work, submit your employment contract and first payslip.
- Submit Milestone 2 Claim: After six months of employment, lodge your claim with the required documents.
- Submit Milestone 3 Claim: After completing 12 months of employment, submit your final claim and supporting evidence.

## FAQs

### **1. What is the Construction Visa Subsidy Program (CVSP)?**

The CVSP is a Western Australian Government initiative that provides financial support to eligible construction workers and employers to help cover migration, visa, and relocation costs.

### 2. **How much financial support can I receive under the CVSP?**

Eligible applicants can receive up to AUD 10,000, depending on their visa pathway and whether they are applying from inside or outside Australia.

### 3. **Which Australian visas are covered under the CVSP?**

The program supports eligible applicants under Subclass 482, 186, 494, 190, and 491 visas.

### **4. Can offshore skilled workers apply for the CVSP?**

Yes. Eligible offshore skilled migrants may receive financial assistance to help with their move to Western Australia.

### **5. Does the CVSP help with Australia PR?**

The CVSP is not a PR visa program, but it supports visa pathways that may lead to Australian permanent residency.
