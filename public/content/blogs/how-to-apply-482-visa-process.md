## What is the 482 Visa?

The Skills in Demand Visa Australia (Subclass 482), or simply the [482 Visa Australia](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/how-to-apply-for-482-visa-australia-step-by-step-sid-visa-guide/), was introduced to replace the previous Temporary Skill Shortage (TSS) Visa. It enables approved Australian employers to hire skilled overseas workers to fill genuine labour shortages.

As this is an [employer-sponsored visa](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/individual/employer-sponsored-visa/), applicants need sponsorship from an eligible Australian employer before applying for the visa. Visa application fees for the 482 visa start from AUD 4,015 across all three streams.

- Work in your nominated occupation
- Include eligible family members in your application
- Travel freely in and out of Australia during the visa validity
- Gain valuable Australian work experience
- Become eligible for Australia permanent residency through suitable pathways like subclass 186 by meeting the applicable requirements

## Types of 482 Visa Streams

The [Skills in Demand Visa](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/a-complete-guide-for-skills-in-demand-visa-subclass-482/) (Subclass 482) offers three streams based on the applicant's occupation and the employer's sponsorship arrangements.

### **1. Core Skills Stream**

The Core Skills Stream is for skilled workers nominated by an approved Australian employer whose occupation is listed on the Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL).

### **2. Specialist Skills Stream**

The Specialist Skills Stream is for highly skilled professionals whose occupation falls under an eligible ANZSCO Major Group 1, 2, 4, 5, or 6, and whose nominated salary meets the Specialist Skills Income Threshold (SSIT).

### **3. Labour Agreement Stream**

The Labour Agreement Stream is for [skilled workers sponsored by employers who have a valid Labour Agreement with the Australian Government.

## 482 Visa Eligibility Checklist

Before you apply 482 visas, you should first understand the basic 482 visa eligibility requirements. **Generally, applicants must have:**

- A genuine job offer from an approved Australian employer
- Skills and qualifications relevant to the nominated occupation
- At least 12 months of relevant work experience closely related to the nominated occupation
- Required English language proficiency
- Health and character clearance (including medical examinations and police clearance certificates, where required)
- Any mandatory professional registration or licensing, where applicable

## 482 Visa Salary Requirements (Updated July 2026)

To be eligible for the 482 Visa, your employer must offer a salary that meets both the applicable income threshold for your visa stream and the Australian Market Salary Rate (AMSR).

| Visa Stream | Minimum Salary Requirement |

| Specialist Skills Stream | AUD146,576 or above (Specialist Skills Income Threshold - SSIT) |
| Labour Agreement Stream | Salary depends on the approved Labour Agreement |

## How to Apply for a 482 Visa

**The 482 visa application process involves the following steps:**

### **Step 1: Check Your Eligibility**

Ensure you meet the occupation, work experience, English, health, and character requirements.

### **Step 2: Find an Approved Sponsor**

Secure a job offer from an Australian employer who is an approved sponsor.

### **Step 3: Employer Lodges the Nomination**

Your employer nominates your position and submits the required sponsorship details.

### **Step 4: Lodge Your Visa Application**

Submit your online application with the required documents and pay the visa application fee.

### **Step 5: Application Assessment**

The Department of Home Affairs assesses your application and may request additional documents, health examinations, or police clearances.

### **Step 6: Receive Your Visa Decision**

If you meet all eligibility requirements, your 482 Visa will be granted.

## FAQs

### **1. Can family members be included in a 482 Visa?**

Yes. You can include eligible family members, such as your spouse or de facto partner and dependent children in your 482 Visa Australia application. If not included initially, they may apply later as subsequent entrants, subject to eligibility.

### **2. Do I need a job offer to apply for a 482 visa?**

Yes. You must have a confirmed job offer from an approved Australian employer because the Skills in Demand Visa Australia is an employer-sponsored visa.

### **3. Can a 482 Visa lead to PR?**

Yes. The Skills in Demand Visa Australia (Subclass 482) can provide a pathway to permanent residency. Many applicants transition to employer-sponsored permanent visas like the Subclass 186 Employer Nomination Scheme, depending on eligibility.

### **4. Can I change employer on a 482 visa?**

Yes, but you cannot start working immediately. Your new employer must be an approved sponsor and must lodge a new nomination before you can legally work under your 482 visa Australia.

### **5. What is the processing time for a 482 Visa?**

Processing time for the 482 visa for Australia varies depending on the stream, occupation, sponsorship status, and application completeness. It can range from a few weeks to several months.
