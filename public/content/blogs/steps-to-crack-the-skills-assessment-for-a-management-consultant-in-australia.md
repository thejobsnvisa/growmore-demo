Are you dreaming of kickstarting your career as a management consultant in Australia? If so, one of the first and important things to ensure is that you complete the positive skills assessment. It proves that your qualifications and experience meet the particular Australian standard.

Primarily, this skills assessment of a management consultant with ANZSCO 224711 is conducted by VETASSESS based on the particular criteria, documentation standards, and evaluation process.

So, whether you are an experienced consultant working independently or within an organization, this guide will provide you with detailed step-by-step information to complete this skills assessment process without any confusion and increase your chances of a positive outcome.

**Step 1:** **Understand the Role of a Management Consultant (ANZSCO 224711)**

Before you dive into documents and applications, take a step back and understand whether your occupation is actually eligible as a Management Consultant or not.

According to ANZSCO 224711, a Management Consultant is someone who assists organisations in achieving greater efficiency and solving organisational problems.

**Here are some not accepted roles under this occupation:**

*   HR Consultant
*   ICT Business Analyst
*   Tax/Finance Advisors
*   Marketing/PR/Recruitment Consultants
*   Managers & Management Accountant

So, if your work history falls under these occupations, then you need to explore other ANZSCO code options.

**Step 2: Meet the Eligibility Requirements**

As a management consultant in the VETASSESS Group B occupation, make sure that you meet the related eligibility requirements.

##### **Educational Requirement:**

**Accepted qualification levels include:**

*   AQF bachelor’s degree
*   AQF master’s degree
*   AQF Doctoral Degree

**In some cases, additional qualifications in a relevant field may also be considered, such as:**

*   AQF Diploma
*   AQF Advanced Diploma
*   AQF associate degree
*   AQF Graduate Diploma

##### **Work Experience Requirement**

**Your experience must be:**

*   Paid (at least 20 hours/week)
*   At a professional level
*   Directly related to management consulting tasks

**Step 3: Prepare Your Documents Like a Pro**

Once you meet all the requirements, start preparing the documents and keep them up to date.

##### **Core Documents:**

*   Degree certificate & transcripts (must show level and field of study)
*   Passport (photo page)
*   Detailed CV (aligned with ANZSCO 224711 tasks)
*   Recent photo

##### **Work Experience Evidence:**

*   Reference letters (on official letterhead, signed, stating roles, tasks, duration, hours/week)
*   Organisation chart (showing your role, reporting lines, team)
*   Payslips, contracts, tax returns (especially if self-employed)

**Note:** If your employer can’t provide an org chart, write a statutory declaration with essential information and explain why this information cannot be provided.

**Step 4: Apply Online via the VETASSESS Portal**

Once your documents are ready, go to the [VETASSESS official website](https://www.vetassess.com.au/).

1.  Create an account
2.  Select “Management Consultant – ANZSCO 224711”
3.  Upload documents
4.  Pay the fee (Around AUD 1,096)
5.  Submit the application and wait for the result.

After you receive a positive skills assessment outcome, you may become eligible to apply for various skilled migration visas in Australia, such as the Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent), [Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated)](https://www.growmore.one/difference-between-subclass-491-and-subclass-190/), or [Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional) visas](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/queensland-skilled-migration-program-2025-26-state-nomination-guide/), based on your circumstances and state nomination.

**Tips to Get a Positive Skill Assessment**

*   Instead of only saying what your job was, explain what you did, what problems you solved, and what results you got in your role as a consultant.

*   Highlight achievements, strategies implemented, and impact delivered in each role.

*   If you are not sure what to do, you can check the helpful resources from the Institute of Management Consultants. They work with VETASSESS to set the rules and give guidance.
