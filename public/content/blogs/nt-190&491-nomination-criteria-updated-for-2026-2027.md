The Northern Territory has released its **updated nomination criteria for 2026–27**, with applications opening **21 August 2026** for all onshore and selected offshore streams.
Here is the simplified breakdown.

## Basic Requirements – All Applicants

Applicants must:

* Be under 45 at the time of nomination.
* Hold a valid skills assessment for their nominated occupation.
* Have at least Competent English (unless a higher level is required by the assessing/licensing/registration body).
* Have at least 65 points.
* Meet the relevant NT nomination stream requirements.
* Demonstrate a genuine commitment to the Northern Territory.
* If nominated, commit to living and working in the NT in their skilled or closely related occupation for at least 3 years after visa grant and comply with NT reporting/survey obligations.

**Important:** You will not be eligible if you or any migrating dependant:

* currently lives interstate; or
* is on a student visa and has not completed the studies connected with that visa.

Applicants previously nominated by NT for a **subclass 489 or 491 cannot receive NT subclass 190 nomination**.

MigrationNT **does not prioritise applications based on points score.**

## ONSHORE APPLICANTS

For subclass **190**, applicants must generally demonstrate a **stronger connection to the NT than any other Australian state or territory**, with long-term residence and commitment to the NT.

If you apply for 190 but do not qualify, MigrationNT may consider you for **491** instead.

 
## 1. NT Graduates – Subclass 190

You must have completed **2 academic years / 4 semesters of full-time study in the NT** through a single tertiary course or nested courses.

You must then meet one of these pathways:

### Pathway A – 6 Months After Graduation

Live continuously in the NT for at least **6 months after completing your qualification**, while holding a **485 visa or associated bridging visa,** AND:

* be currently employed or have a recent job offer in your nominated or closely related skilled occupation; OR
* **in exceptional circumstances**, have worked for at least **3months immediately before applying in regional/remote NT outside Greater Darwin**, even where the employment is not in your nominated or closely related occupation; OR
* in exceptional circumstances, be currently employed or have a recent job offer in a designated critical sector, even where the employment is not in your nominated or closely related occupation.

Critical sectors include:
**Energy, defence, agriculture, mining, health, aged/disabled care, education, tourism and construction.**

**Important:** The exceptional circumstances provision does **not waive or reduce the 6-month NT residence requirement**. It relates to whether certain regional/remote or critical-sector employment may be accepted despite the employment not being in the applicant's nominated or closely related skilled occupation.

### Pathway B – 12 Months After Graduation

Live continuously in the NT for at least **12 months after completing your qualification** while holding a 485 or associated bridging visa AND demonstrate **genuine and sustained efforts to obtain relevant skilled employment in the NT.**

Evidence can include job applications, relevant employment, professional association membership and positive industry feedback.

The **12-month residence requirement is not subject to the exceptional circumstances employment provisions** described above.

### Graduates Over 35 – Not Eligible for 485

Applicants aged over 35 who cannot obtain a 485 may still be considered for 190 after completing the required 2 academic years of NT study if they:

* have relevant employment/recent job offer in their nominated or closely related skilled occupation; OR
* in exceptional circumstances, have at least 3 months' employment immediately before applying in regional/remote NT outside Greater Darwin, even where the employment is not in the nominated or closely related occupation; OR
* in exceptional circumstances, have current employment/recent job offer in a designated critical sector, even where the employment is not in the nominated or closely related occupation.

The exceptional circumstances provisions relate to the employment requirement and do not provide an exemption from the required 2 academic years of NT study.

## 3. NT Residents – Subclass 190

You must:

* Have lived continuously in the NT for at least 2 years immediately before applying and continue living there.
* Have been in full-time NT employment for at least 2 consecutive years immediately before applying.
* The employment must be available for at least another 12 months.
* Employment must normally be in your nominated or closely related skilled occupation.

**Exceptional Circumstances**

In exceptional circumstances, employment may still be considered where it is in a regional or remote area outside Greater Darwin, even if the employment is not in the applicant's nominated or closely related skilled occupation.

**Important:** This exception relates to the type/location of employment only. It does not waive or reduce the requirement to have lived in the NT for 2 consecutive years or the requirement for 2 years of full-time NT employment.

## 4. NT Residents – Subclass 491

You must:

* Have lived continuously in the NT for at least 12 months immediately before applying and continue living there.
* Have worked full-time in the NT for at least 6 consecutive months immediately before applying.
* The employment must be available for at least another 12 months.

Employment must be:

* in your nominated or closely related skilled occupation; OR
* in exceptional circumstances, in a regional or remote area outside Greater Darwin, even if the employment is not in your nominated or closely related skilled occupation; OR
* in exceptional circumstances, in one of the designated critical sectors, even if the employment is not in your nominated or closely related skilled occupation.

Critical sectors include:

**Energy, defence, agriculture, mining, health, aged or disabled care, education, tourism and construction.**

**Important:** “Exceptional circumstances” does **not provide an exemption from the 12-month NT residence requirement or the 6-month full-time employment requirement**. It relates to whether regional/remote or critical-sector employment may be accepted even though the employment is not in the applicant's nominated or closely related skilled occupation.

## What Does “Exceptional Circumstances” Mean?

A key point in the new NT criteria is that **exceptional circumstances should not be confused with an exemption from the required NT residence period.**
Where the criteria refer to exceptional circumstances, they relate to the **employment being relied upon for nomination.**

In practical terms, MigrationNT may consider certain employment:

* in a regional or remote NT location outside Greater Darwin; or
* where expressly permitted, in a designated critical sector,

even though that employment is **not in the applicant's nominated occupation or a closely related skilled occupation.**

The applicant must still meet the applicable **residence, study and/or minimum employment-duration requirements** specified for their particular nomination stream.

Exceptional circumstances are considered by MigrationNT and **should not be treated as an automatic exemption simply because an applicant works in a regional area or critical sector.**

## OFFSHORE APPLICANTS

Offshore applicants will generally only be considered for subclass 491.  
They must:

* Have at least 2 years of post-qualification work experience in their nominated occupation within the last 5 years.
* Demonstrate genuine long-term commitment to living and working in the NT.
* Declare sufficient financial capacity to settle in the NT.
* Qualify under one of the offshore streams below.

## 5. NT Priority Occupation Stream

🚫 **Currently CLOSED to new applications.**  
NT has a significant backlog from the previous program year.

When available, applicants must:

* Have a full skills assessment for an occupation on the NT Offshore Migration Occupation List (NTOMOL); and
* meet any additional work-experience requirement specified for that occupation.

## 6. NT Job Offer Stream

* You must have a verifiable NT job offer in your nominated occupation.
* The employer/business or organisation must have been actively operating in the NT for at least 12 months.
* Applicants under this stream can nominate any occupation on the national eligible occupation list.


## 7. NT Family Stream

You must have an eligible family member who:

* Is usually resident in the NT and has lived there for at least 12 months.
* Is an Australian citizen, Australian permanent resident, eligible New Zealand citizen, or holds an eligible 491, 494, 489 or 309 visa, or an eligible bridging visa associated with a 887 or 191 application.
* Is your eligible relative, or your migrating partner's eligible relative.
* Provides a statutory declaration confirming their willingness and ability to assist you and your migrating dependants with settlement, including accommodation and employment.

### Eligible relatives include:
* Parent, child/step-child, brother/sister (including adoptive/step siblings), aunt/uncle (including adoptive/step), nephew/niece (including adoptive/step), first cousin or grandparent.

### Applicants must also:
* Provide a detailed statement or family tree proving the relationship; and
* show sustained efforts to obtain NT employment, including NT labour-market research, job applications and/or contact with NT employers.

Applicants under the Family stream can nominate any occupation on the national eligible occupation list.


## Financial Capacity

Applicants are responsible for their own settlement costs. NT provides the following guidance for net assets:

| Family Composition | Minimum Net Assets |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Single applicant** | AU$35,000 |
| **Applicant + spouse** | AU$50,000 |
| **Applicant + spouse + 1 child** | AU$60,000 |
| **Applicant + spouse + 2 children** | AU$65,000 |

 Meeting the eligibility criteria does not guarantee nomination. Each application is assessed on its individual merits and nomination remains at the discretion of the NT Government.
