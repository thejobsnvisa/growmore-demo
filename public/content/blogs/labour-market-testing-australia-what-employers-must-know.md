If, as an Australian employer, you wish to nominate an overseas employee before submitting the nomination application, you may need to complete one important step, which is Labour Market Testing (LMT). This step proves to the Department of Home Affairs that you genuinely tried to find a suitable Australian worker for the role before hiring an overseas candidate.

LMT is a mandatory requirement for many employer-sponsored visa nominations and must be completed correctly. The timing of your advertisements and the information they contain matter more than many employers realise.

So, let’s understand Labour Market Testing and the requirements employers must meet before lodging a nomination application.

## **What is Labour Market Testing in Australia?**

Labour Market Testing (LMT) is a requirement mainly associated with Australian employer-sponsored visas. Before an employer can sponsor an overseas worker for certain visa nominations, they must show that they genuinely tried to hire an Australian citizen or permanent resident but were unable to find a suitably qualified and experienced candidate.

In most cases, this involves advertising the position in Australia and keeping evidence of those advertisements.

By this, the government ensures that Australian workers are given priority for available jobs before employers look overseas to fill genuine skill shortages.

## **Which Australian Visas Require Labour Market Testing?**

**Labour Market Testing requirements generally apply when nominating workers under:**

- Skills in Demand (Subclass 482) Visa
- Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Subclass 494) Visa
- Certain Labour Agreement streams

## **Labour Market Testing Requirements for Subclass 482 and Subclass 494 Visas**

The applicable requirements will depend on the nomination stream.

**For Subclass 482 Core Skills and Specialist Skills Streams and Subclass 494 Employer Sponsored Stream**

### **Advertising Requirements**

**For most nominations:**

- The position must have been advertised for at least four weeks.
- The advertisement must have been published within the four-month period immediately before lodging the nomination application.

If an Australian citizen or permanent resident lost their job in the same role within 4 months before you submit the nomination application, it may impact your application.

## **What Must the Advertisement Include?**

**The advertisement must:**

- Be published in Australia.
- Be written in English.
- Include the title or description of the position. (multiple positions in one advertisement is acceptable)
- Include the skills or experience required for the position.
- Include the name of the approved sponsor or recruitment agency being used by the sponsor.
- Include the salary for the position if the annual earnings are lower than AUD 96,400.

## **Where Can Employers Advertise?**

**At least two valid advertisements may be published through:**

- Prominent or professional recruitment websites with national reach throughout Australia.
- Industry-specific recruitment websites that are significantly used within the relevant industry.
- National print media such as newspapers or magazines marketed throughout Australia.
- National radio with national reach.
- The business's website if the sponsor is an accredited sponsor.
- LinkedIn's online recruitment platform.

## **What Advertising Methods Are Not Accepted?**

**The following are not acceptable methods of Labour Market Testing advertising:**

- General classified websites.
- Advertisements made solely through social media notifications, such as Twitter or Instagram.
- LinkedIn vacancies restricted only to LinkedIn profile members.

### **Advertisement Period Requirements**

**Employers should also note that:**

- Advertisements are expected to run for at least four weeks.
- Applications or expressions of interest must be accepted for at least four weeks.
- The position may be advertised in the same medium on two separate occasions.
- The job can be advertised on two different platforms at the same time or at different times.
- The ads can run for two or more overlapping periods, as long as the total advertising time is at least 4 weeks.
- The job can be advertised by the employer or by an approved third party, such as a related company or recruitment agency.

### **Evidence Requirements**

**At the time of lodging the nomination application, sponsors must provide:**

- Copies of all advertisements that meet the Labour Market Testing requirements.
- Evidence of a total of two valid advertisements.

### **For Subclass 482 and Subclass 494 Labour Agreement Streams**

Where specified in the Labour Agreement, employers must provide proof of Labour Market Testing when nominating an applicant.

From 11 December 2023, Labour Market Testing requirements for businesses located in Category 2 locations have changed to align with the Labour Market Testing changes made to the standard program.

**For Category 2 locations:**

- Workforce Australia advertising is no longer required.
- Employers are required to conduct two valid advertisements instead of three.
- Labour Market Testing must have occurred up to 12 months before lodging the nomination application.
- LMT Australia must be conducted in accordance with the standard program requirements.

### **Category 1 and Category 3 Locations**

There are no changes to Labour Market Testing requirements for Labour Agreements covering Category 1 and Category 3 locations.

### **Minister of Religion Labour Agreement**

Labour Market Testing is not required where a Minister of Religion Labour Agreement is being sought.

### **Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement**

For the purposes of the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement, Labour Market Testing Australia requirements may be met through a Memorandum of Understanding with the relevant industry union or unions.

## **Things to Keep in Mind at the Time of Labour Market Testing (LMT)**

**Before completing Labour Market Testing, employers should carefully ensure that:**

- The advertisement meets all required requirements, including the position details, required skills or experience, sponsor or recruitment agency details, and salary information where applicable.
- The advertisements are published through acceptable platforms and remain available for the required period.
- All Labour Market Testing evidence, including copies of advertisements, is properly maintained and ready to provide with the nomination application.
- The Labour Market Testing process is completed according to the requirements of the specific nomination stream.
- Any applicable exemptions or alternative requirements are confirmed before relying on them.

Yes, LMT is required for many employer-sponsored visa nominations to show that no suitable Australian worker was available for the role.

Generally, employers must advertise the position on at least two suitable platforms and meet the Department of Home Affairs’ advertising requirements.

Usually, the position must be advertised for at least four weeks before the nomination application.

Employers must provide LMT evidence, including advertisements and recruitment records, with the nomination application.
