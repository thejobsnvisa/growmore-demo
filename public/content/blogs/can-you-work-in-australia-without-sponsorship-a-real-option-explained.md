Australia is one of the most popular destinations for skilled professionals looking to build an international career. **However, one of the biggest questions many people ask is:**

**"Can I work in Australia if I don't have an employer to sponsor me or enough points for a points-tested visa?"**

The answer is yes, but only through one specific Australian work visa: the Subclass 400 Temporary Work (Short Stay Specialist) Visa. It is designed for highly specialised, short-term work and comes with specific eligibility requirements.

So, let's explore how it works and whether it is the right option for you.

## What Is the Subclass 400 Visa?

The Subclass 400 Temporary Work (Short Stay Specialist) Visa is a short-term Australian work visa designed for people who have highly specialised skills, knowledge, or experience that are not generally available in Australia. One of the key benefits of the Subclass 400 visa is that it does not require employer sponsorship.

In most cases, the Subclass 400 Visa is granted for up to 3 months. However, if there is a strong business need, it may be granted for up to 6 months. Applicants who want to stay longer than three months must provide a compelling business case as part of their application.

It's important to note that this is a temporary work visa. You must apply for it from outside Australia, and it cannot be extended once granted. If you plan to remain in Australia after your visa expires or eventually [apply for Australia PR](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/how-do-i-apply-for-australia-pr-a-step-by-step-expert-guide/), you will need to explore another suitable visa pathway based on your qualifications and eligibility.

## Who Can Apply for the Subclass 400 Visa?

The Subclass 400 Visa is designed for professionals who have highly specialised skills, knowledge, or experience that are needed in Australia for a short period.

**To be eligible, you should generally meet the following requirements:**

**1. Have specialised expertise that can benefit an Australian business and is not readily available in the local Australian labour market.**

**2. Provide evidence of your skills and experience, such as your resume, relevant qualifications or licences, along with a letter from an overseas employer that outlines your current role and responsibilities.**

**3.Ability to financially support yourself and any accompanying family members during your stay in Australia.**

**4.Meet Australia's health and character requirements.**

**5. Be a genuine temporary visitor, with the intention of staying only for the approved period.**

**6. Have no outstanding debts to the Australian Government or have a formal arrangement in place to repay them.**

Your immigration history will also be considered. Previous visa refusals or cancellations may influence your eligibility for the [Subclass 400 visa](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/individual/temporary-work-short-stay-visa/).

## Is an English Test Required?

One reason people search for this visa is that they want to avoid IELTS or other English language tests. Unlike many [skilled migration visas](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/individual/general-skilled-visa/), the Subclass 400 Visa does not have a mandatory English language test requirement.

However, applicants must still be able to communicate effectively to perform the specialised work they have been invited to undertake.

## How to Apply for the Subclass 400 Visa

### **Step 1: Check Your Eligibility**

Before applying, confirm that you meet the visa requirements and have a genuine short-term, highly specialised work opportunity in Australia.

### **Step 2: Prepare Your Documents**

**Gather the documents required to support your application, including:**

**1. A valid passport and proof of identity**

**2. A letter of invitation, employment contract, or job offer from the Australian organisation**

**3. Evidence of your specialised skills, qualifications, or work experience**

**4.Financial documents showing you can support yourself during your stay**

**5.Any additional health or character documents, if requested**

### **Step 3: Apply Online**

Submit your application through ImmiAccount while you are outside Australia. Upload the required documents and pay the visa application fee of **AUD 430 (except in limited circumstances) to lodge your application**.

### **Step 4: Wait for a Decision**

After applying, you can track your application through ImmiAccount. If needed, the Department of Home Affairs may ask for additional documents, health examinations, or biometrics.

### **Step 5: Receive Your Visa Outcome**

You will receive the visa decision by email. If your application is successful, you'll be informed of your visa grant details and conditions. If it is refused, the Department will explain the reason for the decision.

No. The Subclass 400 Visa is a temporary visa and does not lead directly to Australia PR.

### **2. Can I work in Australia without employer sponsorship?**

Yes. The Subclass 400 Visa allows eligible professionals to work in Australia without traditional employer sponsorship for short-term, highly specialised work.

### **3. How long can I stay in Australia on the Subclass 400 Visa?**

Most Subclass 400 Visas are granted for up to 3 months, although some may be approved for up to 6 months with a strong business case.

### **4. Do I need IELTS for the Subclass 400 Visa?**

No. There is no mandatory IELTS or English test requirement for this visa.
