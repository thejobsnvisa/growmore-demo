## What Is Australia’s Working Holiday Maker (WHM) Program?

Australia’s reciprocal Working Holiday Maker program has been operating since 1975 and was designed to strengthen cultural and social connections between Australia and participating countries.

**The program allows eligible young adults to:**

* Stay in Australia for up to 12 months  
* Work temporarily during their stay  
* Study for a short period  
* Travel and explore different parts of Australia  

**Today, the WHM program includes more than 40 partner countries and jurisdictions across two visa subclasses:**

* Working Holiday Visa (Subclass 417)  
* Work and Holiday Visa (Subclass 462)  

An interesting part that many people overlook is that the program works both ways. Australia’s reciprocal agreements also allow eligible Australian citizens to apply for working holiday visas in many of those same partner countries.

## Important Application Conditions Many Applicants Miss

One important thing applicants should understand when applying under the WHM program is the visa location requirement. **If an applicant lodges a WHM visa application from outside Australia:**

* The applicant must remain outside Australia until a decision is made on the visa application.

If you currently hold a Working Holiday Maker visa (subclass 417 or 462) and another visa is granted to you, like eVisitor (subclass 651) or Transit visa (subclass 771), your WHM visa will automatically end. As a result, you may lose your work rights in Australia, and it could also affect your eligibility for another WHM visa in the future.

## Can a Working Holiday Visa Lead to an Australia PR?

The answer is that Working Holiday visas do not directly offer Australia permanent residency. However, applicants should understand that a WHM visa does not guarantee employer sponsorship or permanent residency opportunities.

* Employer sponsorship  
* Regional migration opportunities

## FAQs

### **1. What is Australia’s Working Holiday Maker Program?**

It allows eligible young travellers to live, work, and travel in Australia for up to 12 months.

### **2. What is the difference between Subclass 417 and Subclass 462 visas?**

Both offer similar benefits, but eligibility depends on your nationality, and some Subclass 462 applicants may need to meet extra English or education requirements.

### **3. Do Indian applicants need to go through a ballot process?**

Yes. Applicants from India, China, and Vietnam must first be selected through the pre-application ballot system before applying for Subclass 462.

### **4. Can a Working Holiday visa help with Australia PR later?**

Not directly, but many applicants use this time to gain Australian work experience and explore future PR pathways.

### **5. Can getting another visa affect my WHM visa?**

Yes. If another visa is granted while holding a WHM visa, your current WHM visa may automatically end.
