**What Is an Employer-Sponsored Visa?**

An employer-sponsored visa in Australia is a type of visa that allows skilled workers from overseas to live and work in Australia by getting sponsorship from an Australian employer. It can be temporary or permanent, and some of them also offer a clear pathway to Australian permanent residency.

##### **Skills in Demand visa (Subclass 482)**

*   **What it is:** Skills in Demand is a temporary visa that lets you live and work in Australia for up to 4 years.
*   **Who can apply for:** Skilled workers with a job offer from an approved sponsor.
*   **Pathway to PR:** Yes, through the Medium-Term stream or via regional options.
*   **Requirement:** To apply for this visa, yourequire relevant skills and English proficiency.

##### **Employer Nomination Scheme (Subclass 186)**

*   **What it is:** This is a type of permanent visa for skilled workers who are nominated by an Australian employer to live and work in Australia.
*   **Who can apply for:** If you are already on an eligible temporary visa or an overseas skilled professional with experience, then you can apply for this visa type.  
    
*   **Streams:**
    
    *   **Direct Entry:** For skilled workers who are not already working with the sponsor
    
    *   **Transition:** For workers who have more than two years on a 457 or 482 visa
    
    *   **Labour Agreement:** For workers employed under a formal labour agreement

##### **Skilled Employer-Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa (Subclass 494)**

*   **What it is:** A 494 Visa Australia is a 5-year provisional visa for skilled workers in regional Australia to fill local labour shortages.
*   **Who can apply for:** Skilled workers whose age is under 45 and nominated by an approved sponsor in designated regional areas.
*   **Pathway to PR:** Under the employer-sponsored stream, a pathway to apply for permanent residence can commence after 3 years.

**Basic Process to Apply for Employer-Sponsored Visa**

*   **Get a Job Offer From a Sponsoring Employer:**The first thing you will need to do to apply for an employer-sponsored visa is to find an employer that can sponsor foreign workers and offer you a position.  
    
*   **Fulfill All Visa Requirements:** You will need to make sure you meet all of the visa requirements, including relevant work experience, level of English language ability, and others.

*   **Lodge the visa application:** After your employer has approved your nomination, you can then lodge your visa application and submit the required documentation.
