## What Qualification is Needed for Speech Pathologist PR in Australia?

Your qualification is one of the first things you need to check before planning your migration pathway.

For SPA's current Overseas Qualified Competency Assessment (OSQCA), your Speech Pathology qualification must be comparable to the Australian standard. SPA states that the minimum qualification accepted in Australia is a **Bachelor's degree**, which is classified as AQF Level 7.

Your qualification must also:

* Be specific to Speech Pathology practice
* Include supervised clinical experience
* Be comparable with the Australian Qualification Framework definition of AQF 7
* Be supported by academic transcripts and relevant documentation

Having a health-related qualification alone is not enough. For example, experience as an allied health assistant, teacher, or another related professional does not automatically qualify you as a Speech Pathologist for the Australian assessment process.

## Speech Pathologist Skills Assessment in Australia

For an overseas-qualified Speech Pathologist, the skills assessment is a major step in the Australian PR process for most visas. Speech Pathology Australia is the relevant assessing authority where you can get your skills assessment done.

SPA currently provides two pathways for overseas-trained Speech Pathologists:

* Mutual Recognition Agreement (MRA)
* Overseas Qualified Competency Assessment (OSQCA)

The pathway you use depends on where you obtained your qualification, your professional membership and whether you meet the relevant SPA criteria.

| Who can apply?                                                                                                     | Skills assessment pathway                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Qualified in the US, UK, Ireland, New Zealand or Canada and meet the relevant professional membership requirements | Mutual Recognition Agreement (MRA)               |
| Qualified outside these MRA countries                                                                              | Overseas Qualified Competency Assessment (OSQCA) |
| Canadian-qualified applicants who are currently unable to use the SAC MRA pathway                                  | OSQCA                                            |

## English Requirement for Speech Pathologists

English is an important requirement because Speech Pathologists in Australia work extensively in English for communication, assessment and treatment. Here, Home Affairs' general visa English requirement is not necessarily the same.

The current SPA requirements are:

* **IELTS Academic:** Overall band score of 8.0, with no individual subtest below 7.5; or
* **OET:** Overall score of 1800, with no individual element below 400.

## Visa Options for Speech Pathologists

You can broadly explore two main migration pathways: **skilled migration** and **employer-sponsored migration**.

The right pathway depends on your points, skills assessment, English, work experience, age, state nomination opportunities and whether you have an Australian employer willing to sponsor you.

### 1. Skilled Migration Pathway

The skilled migration pathway is generally suitable for Speech Pathologists who want to migrate based on their own skills and points, rather than relying on an employer sponsor.

This includes visa subclasses like:

* **Subclass 189** – Skilled Independent Visa
* **Subclass 190** – Skilled Nominated Visa
* **Subclass 491** – Skilled Work Regional Visa

### 2. Employer-Sponsored Pathway

The employer-sponsored pathway is generally suitable for Speech Pathologists who have an eligible Australian employer willing to sponsor them.

This includes visa subclasses like:

* **Subclass 482** – Skills in Demand Visa
* **Subclass 186** – Employer Nomination Scheme
* **Subclass 494** – Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional Visa

## What Is the Australia Visa Process for Speech Pathologists?

Once you understand your qualification, SPA skills assessment and available visa options, the next step is to follow the process that applies based on your chosen pathway.

### Skilled Migration Pathway

If you are considering Subclass 189, 190 or 491, the process generally involves:

1. Confirm your occupation is **Speech Pathologist (ANZSCO 252712)** and your qualification meets SPA requirements.
2. Meet the applicable English requirements for SPA and your intended visa.
3. Complete the relevant SPA skills assessment through MRA or OSQCA.
4. Obtain **Certified Practising Speech Pathologist (CPSP)** credentialing with SPA.
5. Submit an **Expression of Interest (EOI)** through SkillSelect with your eligible points.
6. Apply for state or territory nomination if targeting Subclass 190 or 491.
7. Receive an invitation and lodge your visa application by meeting all requirements, including health, character and other applicable requirements.

### Employer-Sponsored Pathway

If you are considering Subclass 482, 186 or 494, the process generally involves:

1. Secure an eligible job offer from an Australian employer willing to sponsor you.
2. Confirm that the nominated occupation, position and employer meet the requirements of the relevant visa.
3. Meet the applicable English, work experience and other visa requirements.
4. Complete the required SPA skills assessment, where applicable, and meet any relevant professional credentialing or registration requirements.
5. The employer completes the required sponsorship and nomination process.
6. Lodge your visa application with the required supporting documents.
