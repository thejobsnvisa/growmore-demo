Step-by-Step: How the Australia PR Process Works for Indians

**Here is a clear, simplified roadmap your consultant will walk you through:**

1.  **Initial Assessment & Points Calculation —** Your consultant evaluates your age, English test scores (IELTS/PTE/OET), qualifications, and work experience to [calculate your current and potential points score](https://www.growmore.one/points-calculator/) under SkillSelect.
2.  **Choose the Right Visa Subclass —** Based on your profile, occupation, and target state, your consultant recommends the ideal pathway — Subclass 189, 190, 491, or another route like the Global Talent Visa or [Employer Sponsored visa](https://www.growmore.one/top-employer-sponsored-visas-everything-you-need-to-know/).
3.  **Skills Assessment —** You apply to the relevant assessing body (e.g., ACS for IT, Engineers Australia for engineers, ANMAC for nurses). Your consultant prepares a compelling assessment application that meets the authority’s criteria.
4.  **English Proficiency Test —** You sit IELTS, PTE, or OET. A Proficient score (7+ IELTS equivalent) adds 10 points; a Superior score (8+ IELTS equivalent) adds 20 points. Your consultant advises on which test to take and what score to target.
5.  **Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) —** Your consultant lodges your EOI on SkillSelect with a maximised, accurate points claim. You then enter an invitation pool and wait for an Invitation to Apply (ITA).
6.  **Receive Invitation to Apply (ITA) —** Once invited, you have 60 days to lodge your full visa application. This is where thorough document preparation — done in advance by your consultant — pays off completely.
7.  **Visa Grant & Move to Australia 🎉 —** After a successful health examination, police clearances, and character checks, your PR visa is granted. You are now free to live, work, and study anywhere in Australia.

Popular Australia PR Visa Pathways for Indian Professionals

Different pathways suit different profiles. **Here is a quick breakdown:**

### Subclass 189 – Skilled Independent Visa

No sponsorship needed. You are selected purely based on your points score. Highly competitive — recent invitations have gone to applicants with 90+ points. Best for top-ranked occupations with consistently high invitation scores.

### Subclass 190 – Skilled Nominated Visa

Requires nomination from an Australian state or territory. Adds 5 bonus points. Easier to obtain an ITA if your occupation is in demand in a specific state. Great for IT professionals, engineers, healthcare workers, and accountants.

### Subclass 491 – Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa

Adds 15 bonus points and has lower invitation thresholds. Requires you to live and work in a regional area for 3 years [before applying for PR](https://www.growmore.one/australia-pr-visa-consultant/) (Subclass 191). Excellent strategy for applicants who just miss the mark for 189/190.

### Global Talent Visa

For exceptional talent in targeted sectors like AI, fintech, cybersecurity, agri-food, and medtech. No points test required. A strong consultant with global talent expertise can help assess your eligibility.
