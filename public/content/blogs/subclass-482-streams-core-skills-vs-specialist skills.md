# Subclass 482 Streams: Core Skills vs Specialist Skills 

If you are considering applying for a Subclass 482 visa to work in Australia, there is one important thing you should be aware of: **“all 482 visas are not the same”**.  

The Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482) mainly has two streams: **Core Skills** and **Specialist Skills**, and both of them are designed for different circumstances and come with different requirements. Your occupation and proposed salary can play an important role in determining which stream is relevant to your employer-sponsored pathway. 

So, let’s break down the key differences between the two streams to understand which pathway may be relevant to your circumstances. 


## What Are the Subclass 482 Streams? 

The Skills in Demand visa (Subclass 482) has two main streams for employer-sponsored visa applicants: 
* **Core Skills Stream**
* **Specialist Skills Stream**

However, there is also a **Labour Agreement pathway** for employers who are sponsoring workers under an approved labour agreement. In this situation, specific concessions or arrangements may apply based on the terms of the agreement and the occupation being sponsored. 

## What Is the Core Skills Stream? 

The Core Skills Stream is designed for skilled workers whose nominated occupation is included on Australia’s current Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL). However, your nominated position must genuinely match the occupation, skills, qualifications, and relevant work experience of the role you are being nominated for. 

**The Core Skills Stream may be relevant where:** 

* The nominated occupation is on the CSOL
* The nominated position genuinely matches the occupation 
* The salary meets the applicable Core Skills Income Threshold, which is **AUD 79,423** from 1 July 2026
* The salary is also in compliance with the applicable Annual Market Salary Rate (AMSR) requirements
* An applicant has a minimum of 1 year of relevant work experience
* The applicant meets English requirements or is eligible for exemption
* Any required skills assessment, registration, or licensing requirements are satisfied
* The employer and nomination meet the relevant sponsorship requirements 

---

## What Is the Specialist Skills Stream? 

The Specialist Skills Stream may apply where the nominated occupation falls within eligible ANZSCO Major Groups 1, 2, 4, 5, or 6, subject to the applicable requirements. Unlike the Core Skills Stream, the occupation does not need to be included on the Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL).  

**The Specialist Skills Stream may be relevant where:** 

* The nominated occupation falls within eligible ANZSCO Major Groups 1, 2, 4, 5, or 6
* The nominated position is the actual occupation 
* The salary meets the applicable Specialist Skills Income Threshold, which is **AUD 146,576** from 1st July 2026
* The applicant has at least 1 year of relevant work experience
* The applicant meets English requirements or qualifies for an exemption
* Any required skills assessment, registration, or licensing requirements are satisfied
* The employer and nomination meet the relevant sponsorship requirements 

## Core Skills vs Specialist Skills: At a Glance 

| Factor | Core Skills Stream | Specialist Skills Stream |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Occupation requirement** | Must be on the Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL) | Must be an eligible ANZSCO occupation in Major Groups 1, 2, 4, 5 or 6 |
| **Salary threshold** | AUD 79,423 from 1 July 2026 | AUD 146,576 from 1 July 2026 |
| **Market salary requirement** | Applies | Applies |
| **Relevant work experience** | At least 1 year | At least 1 year |
| **Skills assessment** | Where required for the occupation | Where required for the occupation |
| **English** | Required unless exempt | Required unless exempt |
| **Employer nomination** | Required | Required |
| **Maximum stay** | Up to 4 years | Up to 4 years |


## How Do You Determine Which Stream Applies? 

The applicable 482 visa stream depends mainly on the nominated occupation, position, and proposed salary. 

When assessing your options, you should first confirm that the nominated occupation genuinely matches your position and the duties you will perform. If the occupation is on the current CSOL, you may be considered under the Core Skills Stream.  

> Explore the CSOL list here: [https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/Documents/core-sol.pdf](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/Documents/core-sol.pdf)

If it falls within ANZSCO Major Groups 1, 2, 4, 5, or 6, then the Specialist Skills Stream may be available, subject to the other requirements. 


## What Happens If Your Occupation Is Not on the CSOL? 

If your occupation is not on the CSOL, then you can apply for a 482 visa under another stream, such as the Specialist Skills stream. 

You can also explore the Labour Agreement stream if your employer has an approved labour agreement for a specific occupation. Apart from this, there are also other employer-sponsored visa pathways that may be available based on your occupation, position, salary, and circumstances.

## FAQs 

**1. What are the two streams of the Subclass 482 visa?**  
The Skills in Demand visa (Subclass 482) has two main streams: the Core Skills Stream and the Specialist Skills Stream. A Labour Agreement pathway may also apply in specific circumstances. 

**2. What is the difference between the Core Skills and Specialist Skills streams?**  
The Core Skills Stream generally requires the nominated occupation to be on the CSOL, while the Specialist Skills Stream covers eligible occupations in specified ANZSCO Major Groups and has a higher salary threshold. 

**3. What is the salary threshold for the Subclass 482 Specialist Skills Stream?**  
From 1 July 2026, the Specialist Skills Income Threshold is AUD 146,576. The applicable Annual Market Salary Rate requirements must also be met. 

**4. Can I apply for a Subclass 482 visa if my occupation is not on the CSOL?**  
Potentially, yes. An occupation not on the CSOL may still qualify under the Specialist Skills Stream if it meets the relevant occupation, salary, and other Subclass 482 requirements. 

**5. How do I choose between the Core Skills and Specialist Skills streams?**  
The relevant Subclass 482 stream depends on your nominated occupation, position, salary, and other eligibility requirements. The occupation must genuinely match the position and duties being sponsored.
