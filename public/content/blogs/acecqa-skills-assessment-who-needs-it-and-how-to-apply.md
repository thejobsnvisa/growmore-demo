If you are planning to migrate to Australia as an Early Childhood Teacher, Child Care Centre Manager or another eligible childcare professional, an ACECQA Skills Assessment may be an important step in your skilled migration journey. It checks whether your early childhood education and care qualifications meet Australian standards.

However, not every childcare professional needs an ACECQA Skills Assessment. Your nominated occupation, qualifications, work experience, and the pathway you are applying for; all these factors can determine whether you require this assessment or not.

In this guide, we explain which occupations ACECQA assesses, the key eligibility requirements, how to apply and which documents you may need to provide.

## What Is an ACECQA Skills Assessment?

The Australian Children's Education and Care Quality Authority (ACECQA) is an approved assessing authority that conducts migration skills assessments for specified early childhood education and care occupations.

An ACECQA Migration Skills Assessment evaluates whether your qualifications in children's education and care meet Australian standards and the requirements for your nominated occupation.

This assessment is relevant for applicants pursuing skilled migration pathways where a skills assessment is required.

## Which Australian Visa Pathways May Require an ACECQA Skills Assessment?

Whether you need an ACECQA Skills Assessment depends on your nominated occupation, visa subclass and the requirements in force when you apply.

Potentially relevant skilled migration pathways include:

* **Subclass 189 Skilled Independent visa:** A points-tested permanent visa for eligible skilled applicants who meet the applicable criteria.
* **Subclass 190 Skilled Nominated visa:** A permanent skilled visa requiring nomination by an Australian state or territory, subject to the relevant criteria.
* **Subclass 491 Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa:** A provisional skilled visa with eligible state or territory nomination or family sponsorship pathways.
* **Employer-sponsored visa pathways:** Depending on the visa subclass, occupation, stream and applicable rules, a skills assessment may be required.

## How to Apply for an ACECQA Skills Assessment

You can apply for an ACECQA Migration Skills Assessment through its online portal by following these steps:

### 1. Identify your occupation

Confirm whether you are applying as an Early Childhood Teacher, Child Care Centre Manager or another eligible childcare professional.

### 2. Check your qualifications

Compare your qualification with ACECQA's requirements for your occupation. Overseas qualifications may need an equivalency assessment.

### 3. Gather your evidence

Prepare your certificates, academic transcripts and English-language results. If your occupation requires work experience, include evidence of your duties and employment history.

### 4. Submit your application

Complete the relevant application through ACECQA's online portal, upload your documents and pay the applicable fee.

### 5. Follow up on your application

Respond to any requests for additional evidence and review your assessment outcome once it is issued.

## Documents Required for an ACECQA Skills Assessment

The required documents mainly depend on your specific nominated occupation. However, these include:

* **Valid passport:** A copy of the relevant identity pages.
* **Qualification certificate or parchment:** Official evidence that you completed the qualification.
* **Academic transcripts:** Official records covering all years of study.
* **Change-of-name evidence:** Required if your identity or qualification documents show different names.
* **Applicant Declaration and Consent Form:** Completed as required by ACECQA.
* **Authorised Agent Nomination and Declaration forms:** Where you have appointed an authorised agent.
* **Australian teacher registration or accreditation:** Where applicable.
