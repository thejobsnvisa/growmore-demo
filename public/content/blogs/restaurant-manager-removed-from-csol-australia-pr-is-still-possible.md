## Visa Pathways Available for Restaurant Managers in Australia

### Pathway 1: Australia PR Through Company Specific Labour Agreement

A Company Specific Labour Agreement is one of the alternative employer-sponsored pathways that may still be available for Restaurant Managers in Australia.

Under this arrangement, an Australian employer can apply for a Labour Agreement with the Australian Government when they can demonstrate genuine workforce shortages and difficulty finding suitable local workers.

Once it is approved, the employer may be able to nominate overseas workers for occupations that are not normally available under standard employer-sponsored visa programs. This makes it a valuable option for eligible Restaurant Managers seeking a pathway to Australia PR.

### Pathway 2: Australia PR Through DAMA

Another possible pathway for Restaurant Managers is through a Designated Area Migration Agreement (DAMA). DAMAs are agreements between the Australian Government and designated regional areas that allow employers to sponsor overseas workers for occupations that may not be available under standard employer-sponsored visa programs.

Through this pathway, you may get many concessions related to age, English language requirements, salary thresholds, and skills or work experience requirements that are not available under the standard visa program.

### Pathway 3: Australia PR Through Industry Labour Agreements

As a Restaurant Manager, you may also have an alternative pathway through the Restaurant (Premium Dining) Industry Labour Agreement.

This Industry Labour Agreement is designed for Australian premium quality dining restaurants that are experiencing genuine skill shortages and are unable to fill positions locally. It allows eligible employers to sponsor skilled overseas workers in occupations including Restaurant Manager, Cook, Chef, Café Manager, and Trade Waiter.

The Restaurant (Premium Dining) Industry Labour Agreement has fixed terms and conditions set by the Australian Government, which cannot be negotiated or changed. Employers must meet the prescribed eligibility requirements before they can access this labour agreement.

### Pathway 4: Australia PR Through Subclass 494 Visa

If you have at least three years of work experience as a Restaurant Manager and have secured a job offer from an employer located in a designated regional area of Australia, the [Subclass 494 Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/employers/standard-business-sponsorship/) (Provisional) visa also provides a pathway towards Australia PR.

This visa allows you to live and work in regional Australia for up to five years, but after holding the Subclass 494 visa and meeting the relevant residence, income and visa requirements, eligible applicants may become eligible to apply for the [Subclass 191](https://www.growmore.one/news/how-australia-counts-regional-visa-places-critical-updates-for-applicants/) Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional) visa.

## How Our Client Secured an Alternative Australia PR Pathway

One of our clients approached us for a Subclass 482 visa when the occupation of Restaurant Manager was available under CSOL. We had already started the process, including the required recruitment activities for employer sponsorship.

In the meantime, when we had posted the company’s advertisement for SBS approval, the occupation was suddenly removed from CSOL, which made the standard Subclass 482 pathway unavailable for our client.

So, our registered migration agent explored alternative Australia PR pathways.

In this case, DAMA pathway was not an option because the employer's restaurant was not located within an eligible DAMA region.

After carefully assessing both the employer's circumstances and the client's profile, we identified a suitable pathway: **Company Specific Labour Agreement.**

So, we applied for the company’s Company Specific Labour Agreement, and based on its approval, the client can now apply for a Subclass 482 visa. After meeting the relevant requirements, the client may become eligible for permanent residency through the Subclass 186 Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream.

## FAQs

### **1. Can Restaurant Managers still get Australia PR after being removed from the CSOL?**

Yes. There are still alternative pathways available, depending on your work experience, employer, and location.

### **2. Do I need to work in regional Australia to get sponsored?**

Not necessarily. While some pathways are only available in regional Australia, others may be available through eligible employers in metropolitan areas.

### **3. Is there an age limit for Australia PR pathways?**

It varies by visa type. Some pathways may even offer age concessions if you meet the relevant requirements.

### **4. Do I need a skills assessment to apply for Australia PR?**

No, not always. Whether you need a skills assessment depends on the visa pathway you choose. Most employer-sponsored visa pathways do not require a skills assessment, although some occupations and visa streams may have specific requirements.

### **5. What if my occupation is removed from an occupation list?**

It does not always mean your migration journey has ended. There may still be alternative visa pathways available depending on your occupation, experience, and personal circumstances. Consulting a registered migration agent can help you understand your options and identify the most suitable pathway.
