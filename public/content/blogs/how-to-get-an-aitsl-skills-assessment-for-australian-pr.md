If you’re considering a move to Australia as a teacher, you have probably begun to look into your qualifications, English score and visa options. But there is one important step that every skilled migrant needs to complete: the AITSL skills assessment. 

It helps determine your years of study and classroom experience and checks whether your teaching qualifications meet the requirements for your nominated occupation in Australia. 

So, what does AITSL actually assess, what paperwork is required and how do you ensure your application is correctly prepared? 

Let’s break down the entire process step by step. 

## What Is an AITSL Skills Assessment? 

An AITSL skills assessment is an assessment to check whether your qualifications and other required evidence meet the criteria for a nominated school teacher occupation for Australia's skilled migration program.

No doubt, an AITSL skills assessment for Australia PR is mandatory. Still, you will also need to gain registration with the relevant state or territory teacher regulatory authority to work as a teacher in Australia. 

**School teacher occupations assessed by AITSL**

For Australian skilled migration, AITSL provides skills assessments for the following school teacher occupations: 

| ANZSCO Code | Occupation | Skill Level |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| 241213 | Primary School Teacher | 1 |
| 241311 | Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher | 1 |
| 241411 | Secondary School Teacher | 1 |
| 241511 | Special Needs Teacher | 1 |
| 241512 | Teacher of the Hearing Impaired | 1 |
| 241513 | Teacher of the Sight Impaired | 1 |
| 241599 | Special Education Teachers nec | 1 |

Take note that Early Childhood Teacher is no longer assessed by AITSL for migration purposes. Since 7 December 2024, ACECQA has been the assessing authority for that occupation. 

**Note: Here, skill level 1 means these occupations generally require a bachelor’s degree or higher qualification, with relevant experience sometimes accepted in place of formal education.** 

## AITSL Skills Assessment Requirements 

Before submitting an application, you need to check the criteria for your specific occupation. AITSL provides separate occupation checklists because the evidence required can vary depending on the nominated teaching occupation. 

**1. To Have Relevant Qualifications** 

Your qualifications are a key part of the AITSL skills assessment. 

For teaching qualifications, AITSL generally requires a qualification comparable to at least an Australian Bachelor degree (AQF Level 7). For non-teaching qualifications, a qualification comparable to an Australian Advanced Diploma or Associate Degree (AQF Level 6 or higher) may be considered. 

AITSL does not assess short courses of less than one-year, non-academic qualifications, professional development programs or incomplete qualifications. 

**2.To Have a Relevant Initial Teacher Education Qualification**

Your initial teacher education (ITE) qualification must be relevant to your nominated teaching occupation. 

AITSL requires at least one year of ITE study relevant to the nominated occupation. 

**3.To Meet the English Language Requirement** 

You must meet AITSL’s English language requirement by Academic IELTS or Eligible higher education study in an approved English-speaking country. 

For Academic IELTS, you need at least 7.0 in Reading and Writing and 8.0 in Speaking and Listening. Effective July 13, 2026, the validity of the English result for assessment has been extended from two years to three years. 

**4. To Provide Evidence of Supervised Teaching Practice**

You need to provide evidence of supervised teaching practice completed as part of your initial teacher education qualification. 
The evidence should be issued by your university and confirm the duration of teaching practice, age range of students taught and school setting. 

**5.To Provide Valid Identity Documents**

You have to present a passport that is valid and up to date, and other identity documents as necessary.  

This includes a birth certificate, national ID card, driver’s license or other government ID. If you are going to submit any documents that are not in English, you must submit an English translation of those documents. 

## How to Apply for an AITSL Skills Assessment 
Once you know you meet the requirements and have your documents ready, you can follow these steps to apply for an AITSL Skills Assessment. 

**Step 1: Choose Your Occupation**

Select the school teacher occupation that best matches your qualifications and check its specific AITSL requirements. 

**Step 2: Check the Application Guide** 

Read the latest AITSL application guide before you start so you know exactly what evidence is required. 

**Step 3: Prepare Your Documents**

Gather your identity documents, qualifications, transcripts, supervised teaching practice evidence, English test results and other required documents. 

**Step 4: Apply Online** 

Create your application through the AITSL Applicant Portal and complete the required details. 

**Step 5: Upload Your Evidence** 

Upload clear, complete documents in the required format. Check everything carefully before submitting. 

**Step 6: Pay the Fee** 

The current AITSL skills assessment fee is AUD 1,154. Payment can be made through the Applicant Portal using Visa or MasterCard. 

**Step 7: Receive an Outcome**

After submitting your application, wait for an outcome, which generally comes within four weeks if your application is fully assessment ready. 

## Common AITSL Skills Assessment Mistakes 
Any small error in your skills assessment application can cost your Australia PR chances, so it is important to take care of these mistakes: 

**1. Choosing an occupation without checking the checklist** 

Your occupation should be supported by your qualifications and evidence. 

**2. Submitting an unsuitable English test**

Don't assume that any English test score will satisfy AITSL. Check the specific English language criterion before booking or submitting your test. 

**3. Uncertified or incorrectly translated documents** 

Submitting documents without proper certification or using a translation service that does not meet the required standards. 

**4. Providing evidence that does not meet AITSL’s requirements**

A document may seem relevant but still not meet AITSL’s requirements. This is especially important for supervised teaching practice, where specific evidence from the awarding institution is required.

## FAQs 

**1.What is an AITSL skills assessment?** 

An AITSL skills assessment checks whether your teaching qualifications and required evidence meet the criteria for your nominated school teacher occupation for Australian skilled migration. 

**2.Who needs an AITSL skills assessment for Australian PR?**  

Teachers who nominate an AITSL-assessed school teacher occupation for Australian skilled migration generally need a positive AITSL skills assessment. 

**3.What documents are needed for an AITSL skills assessment?**  

Common documents include identity documents, qualifications, academic transcripts, English evidence and supervised teaching practice evidence. 

**4.Can I apply for an AITSL skills assessment without teaching experience?** 

Teaching experience is not the only factor AITSL considers. Your teaching qualifications, initial teacher education and supervised teaching practice must meet the relevant assessment requirements. 

**5.Does an AITSL skills assessment give me teacher registration in Australia?** 

No. An AITSL skills assessment is for migration purposes. You must separately meet the requirements of the relevant state or territory teacher registration authority to work as a teacher in Australia.
