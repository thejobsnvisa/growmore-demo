You may have come to Australia on a Subclass 482 visa with a goal in mind to build a stable future and eventually become a permanent resident. The Subclass 186 visa is one pathway that can help you turn this goal into reality. Its Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream provides a pathway for eligible 482 visa holders to move from temporary sponsored employment to permanent residency.

However, moving from 482 to 186 TRT does not simply mean completing two years on your 482 visa. You need to meet specific requirements related to your sponsored employment, occupation, employer nomination, age, English, licensing and other visa criteria.

If you are currently working in Australia on a 482 visa and planning to settle permanently through the 186 TRT pathway, let's understand the requirements and process in this guide.

## What are the 186 TRT Eligibility Requirements?

To qualify for the Subclass 186 TRT stream, you must satisfy a number of requirements.

### 1. You Must Hold an Eligible Visa

You must currently be the primary holder of a Subclass 457 visa, a Subclass 482 visa, or an eligible bridging visa connected with certain 457, 482, 186 or 187 applications.

### 2. You Generally Need Two Years of Eligible Sponsored Employment

You must have at least two years of eligible sponsored employment in Australia within the three years before applying. The employment must be full-time, undertaken while holding a subclass 457, 482 or eligible bridging visa, and in an occupation you were approved to work in.

If you held multiple 457 or 482 visas, eligible employment may be counted across approved occupations. If you changed employers, employment may also count from the date an approved nomination was lodged, provided you started working for that employer before the nomination was approved.

So, do not simply count two years from your 482 grant date. Your actual employment history needs to be checked against the TRT rules, particularly if you have changed employers, occupations or had gaps in employment.

### 3. Your Employer Must Nominate You

The Subclass 186 is also an employer-sponsored visa. So, your employer must nominate you for a permanent position, and the nomination must be approved within 6 months before you apply for the 186 visa.

There is also an important employer requirement:

Your nominating employer must generally be the employer who last sponsored you to work full-time on your Subclass 457 or 482 visa. Your employer must be an Australian business that is actively and lawfully operating.

### 4. Meet the Skills Requirement

The fact that a skills assessment is generally not required upfront for the TRT stream does not mean that the skills requirement becomes irrelevant. You must have the skills and qualifications required to perform the tasks of the nominated occupation.

### 5. Meet the English & Age Requirement

You generally need Competent English and must be under 45 years of age when applying for the 186 TRT stream. Age exemptions may apply in specific circumstances.

### 6. Licensing, Registration or Professional Membership

If your occupation requires mandatory licensing, registration or membership in Australia, you must satisfy the applicable requirement for the state or territory where you intend to work.

### 7. Health and Character Requirements

You and your relevant family members must meet Australia's health and character requirements. Depending on your circumstances, this may include medical examinations, police certificates or additional information.

It is best to check these requirements early rather than leave them until the last minute.

### 8. Immigration History Can Matter

You generally should not have had a visa cancelled or an application refused, unless a specific exception applies.

## Step-by-Step Process to Move from 482 to 186 TRT Pathway

### Step 1: Hold a Subclass 482 Visa

Work in Australia while meeting your visa and sponsorship conditions.

### Step 2: Complete Eligible Employment

Generally, complete two years of eligible full-time sponsored employment within the three years before applying.

### Step 3: Check Your Eligibility

Review your employment, occupation, employer, English, age, skills and other requirements.

### Step 4: Employer Nomination

Your employer nominates you for a permanent position.

### Step 5: Lodge the 186 Visa

After nomination approval, lodge your Subclass 186 application within the required timeframe. Provide any additional documents if requested by the Department.

### Step 6: Visa Decision

If all requirements are met, your Subclass 186 visa is granted, and you become a permanent resident.

## Why Is the 186 TRT Pathway Popular?

Compared to the 186 Direct Entry stream, the 186 TRT stream is preferable because you do not need to start your application from scratch. Here are two practical advantages that you should definitely understand.

### 1. A Skills Assessment Is Generally Not Required Upfront

One of the advantages of the TRT stream is that applicants generally do not need to obtain a skills assessment like other migration pathways. However, this does not mean the Department can never ask about your skills. They may ask you to obtain a skills assessment later on if required.

### 2. The Genuine Position Requirement Can Be Easier to Evidence

A genuine position is one of the main requirements for the 186 visa, but TRT applicants may have an advantage when it comes to demonstrating this.

If you have already worked for the same sponsoring employer in the nominated role for around two years, your employment contract, payslips, position details and actual duties can help establish that the role is genuine and ongoing.

While in the Direct Entry pathway, this evidence may not be as readily available as it may be when starting a new sponsored role.

## FAQs

### 1. Can I move from a 482 visa to a 186 TRT visa?

Yes. Eligible 482 visa holders may transition to the Subclass 186 Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream if they meet all requirements.

### 2. How long do I need to work on a 482 visa before applying for 186 TRT?

Generally, you need at least two years of eligible full-time sponsored employment in Australia within the three years before applying.

### 3. Is a skills assessment required for the 186 TRT visa?

Generally, a skills assessment is not required for the 186 TRT stream, although specific circumstances may require further evidence of your skills.

### 4. Do I need my employer to sponsor me for 186 TRT?

Yes. Your employer must nominate you for a permanent position and meet the relevant nomination requirements.

### 5. What are the main requirements for 482 to 186 TRT?

Key requirements generally include eligible sponsored employment, employer nomination, Competent English, age requirements, relevant skills, licensing or registration, and health and character criteria.
