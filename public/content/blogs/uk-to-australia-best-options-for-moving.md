**Top Pathways for UK Citizens Moving to Australia** 
-----------------------------------------------------

Here are some top pathway options through which you can easily come to Australia. 

### **Option 1: Employer-Sponsored Visas (Subclass 482 and 186**) 

If you have an offer of employment from an Australian employer, then the Employer-Sponsored Visa can be one of the quicker pathways to permanent residency. You can consider the following options: 

*   **Subclass 482 (Temporary Skill Shortage Visa):** This [Skills in Demand Visa](https://www.growmore.one/a-complete-guide-for-skills-in-demand-visa-subclass-482/) allows you to work for an Australian employer for up to four years. It is ideal for skilled workers, especially for those who are in high-demand sectors like construction and healthcare. 

Currently, the 482 visa is particularly popular in industries such as construction, healthcare, and IT, where there is high demand for skilled workers. 

*   **Subclass 186 (Employer Nomination Scheme Visa)**: If you have worked for an employer in Australia for a certain period, you can become eligible for permanent residency through employer sponsorship. 

### **Option: 2 General Skilled Migration (GSM)** 

You can go for the General Skilled Migration (GSM) program if you have qualifications and work experience in an in-demand profession. This pathway uses a points-based system, which includes factors such as age, education, work experience, and English proficiency. Here are the visa subclasses options for skilled workers: 

*   **Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent Visa)**: For skilled workers not sponsored by an employer or family member. 

*   **Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated Visa)**: For skilled workers nominated by an Australian state or territory. 

*   **Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa):**  For skilled workers willing to live and work in regional areas. 

However, the time for the GSM process is comparatively higher than that of Employer-Sponsored Visas. 

### **Option 3: Working Holiday Visa Australia (Subclass 417)** 

This visa allows UK citizens under 35 years of age to work and stay in Australia for up to one year. It is an excellent option for those who want to explore the country while working to support themselves. You can also apply for other visas, such as employer-sponsored visas or general skilled migration, while you are on this visa. 

### **Option 4: Family-Sponsored Visas** 

If you have close family members in Australia, you may be eligible for family-sponsored migration. Common family visa types for this include: 

*   **Partner Visa (Subclass 820/801):** If you are married to, or in a de facto relationship with, an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or eligible New Zealand citizen, then you can apply for these subclasses. 

*   **Parent Visa (Subclass 103):** If your child is an Australian citizen or permanent resident, you may be able to apply for a parent visa. 

*   **Child Visa (Subclass 101):** This visa is for dependent children of Australian citizens or permanent residents. 

### **Option 5: Student Visa (Subclass 500)** 

If you are looking to pursue further studies in Australia, the Student Visa allows you to study at an Australian educational institution. This visa also provides the right to work part-time during your studies.  

If you pursue a master’s in research in Australia, then it will give you extended TR and full-time work rights during your research study. Furthermore, if you have done this study from a regional area, then you will also get the benefits of living 2 extra years on your TR. 

### **Option 6: Visitor Visa (Subclass 600)** 

If you’re not immediately looking to work or study but want to visit Australia to explore or attend family events, a Visitor Visa might be a good option. However, please note that you cannot convert a Visitor Visa directly into a Student Visa. 

### **Option 7: MATES Visa (for Indian Passport Holders)** 

If you hold an Indian passport and are living in the UK, you may be eligible for the MATES Visa. It offers a pathway for skilled workers to migrate to Australia and targets specific skills that are in demand in Australia. 

### Conclusion 

When planning your move from the UK to Australia, it’s important to choose the right pathway based on your individual circumstances. If you are eligible, then give the first priority to **Subclass 186, Subclass 189, Subclass 190, or Subclass 491**.

If you’re not eligible for these, then consider the Subclass 494 (Regional) or Subclass 482 (Temporary Skill Shortage), which are employer-sponsored options. Those not qualifying for any of the above can choose from Student Visas, MATES Visas, or Visitor Visas.
