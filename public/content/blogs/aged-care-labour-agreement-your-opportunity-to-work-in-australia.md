## **What is the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement?**

The Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement (ACILA) is a specialised labour agreement introduced by the Australian Government to help approved aged care providers address workforce shortages by sponsoring overseas workers in direct care occupations.

This agreement offers several concessions regarding qualifications, wage criteria, and work experience requirements for eligible occupations that may not be available under standard employer-sponsored visa pathways.

For many overseas workers, it offers a practical pathway to gain Australian work experience and eventually become eligible for permanent residency.

## **How Does the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement Work?**

Before an overseas worker can be sponsored, an aged care provider must first obtain access to the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement. The process involves three key steps:

- Negotiating a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the relevant union
- Requesting access to the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement
- Submitting the nomination and visa application

Once an employer receives approval, they may be able to sponsor eligible overseas workers under the relevant employer-sponsored visa pathway.

### **Step 1: Negotiate a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)**

To access the labour agreement, employers must first enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the relevant union.

The recognised unions include:

- Australian Nursing and Midwifery Federation (ANMF)
- Health Services Union (HSU)
- United Workers Union (UWU)

The appropriate union will depend on factors such as the occupation being sponsored, whether services are provided in residential or home care settings, and the state or territory in which the employer operates.

During this stage, labour market testing requirements are also addressed. Employers are generally expected to demonstrate that reasonable efforts have been made to recruit Australian workers before seeking overseas talent.

### **Step 2: Request Access to the Labour Agreement**

After establishing an MoU, employers can submit their request for access to the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement.

The Department will assess whether the employer satisfies the relevant requirements and whether the agreement has been genuinely established before granting approval.

### **Step 3: Nomination and Visa Application**

Once access has been approved, employers can nominate eligible overseas workers under the relevant visa pathway.

This generally involves:

- Nomination by employer &
- Visa application by the overseas worker

After both stages are successfully approved, overseas workers can begin their employment in Australia's aged care sector.

## **FAQs**

**1\. What is the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement?**

The Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement allows approved aged care providers to sponsor eligible overseas workers for specific direct care occupations.

**2\. Which occupations are eligible under the Aged Care Labour Agreement?**

Eligible occupations include Aged or Disabled Carer, Nursing Support Worker, and Personal Care Assistant.

**3\. Can the Aged Care Labour Agreement lead to permanent residency?**

Yes, eligible workers may access a pathway to permanent residency through the Employer Nomination Scheme (Subclass 186) visa.

**4\. Is a skills assessment required for the Aged Care Labour Agreement?**

A skills assessment may be required for overseas qualifications or when relying on work experience instead of formal qualifications.

**5\. Which visas are available under the Aged Care Labour Agreement?**

Eligible workers may be sponsored through the Skills in Demand Visa (Subclass 482) and Employer Nomination Scheme (Subclass 186).
