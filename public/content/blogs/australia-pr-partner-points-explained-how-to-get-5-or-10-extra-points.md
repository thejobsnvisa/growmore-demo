If you are aiming for Australian Permanent Residency (PR) through the skilled migration program, your points score is everything. And at the time of point calculation, many applicants get confused about how their partner’s profile can also increase their Australia PR chances.

In reality, a partner can add either 5 or 10 extra points based on their English ability, skills assessment, occupation, and visa eligibility under the Australia PR Points Test for Subclass 189, 190, or 491 visas. These additional points can significantly improve your overall ranking and often become the deciding factor in whether you receive an invitation or remain in the skilled migration pool for a longer period.

## Understanding How Partner Points Work in Australia PR

Australia’s skilled migration system is based on a Points Test, where applicants are ranked based on age, education, work experience, English ability, and other factors.

Here, what many applicants overlook is that partner skills and eligibility can directly contribute to your total score under [Subclass 189](https://www.growmore.one/news/is-your-eoi-ready-subclass-189-invitation-round-confirmed-for-4-june-2026/), 190, and 491 visas.

This is not a minor add-on. In many occupations where invitation cut-offs are high, partner points become a deciding factor between getting selected and missing out entirely.

## How Many Extra Points Can You Get from Your Partner?

**Under the official points system, your partner's situation can contribute in three possible ways:**

### 10 Points for Skilled Partner

You can claim 10 points if your partner is included in your visa application and meets all required criteria at the time of invitation.

**Your partner must:**

- By applying for the same visa subclass as you
- Not be an Australian citizen or permanent resident
- Be under 45 years of age at the time of invitation
- Have Competent English
- Have a positive skills assessment from the relevant assessing authority
- Nominate an occupation listed on the relevant skilled occupation list
- Ensure their occupation aligns with your visa pathway requirements

When all these conditions are met, you can claim the maximum 10 partner points under the skilled migration points test.

### 5 Points for Partner with Competent English

You can claim 5 points if your partner is included in your visa application and meets the English requirement.

**To be eligible, your partner must:**

- By applying for the same visa subclass as you
- Not be an Australian citizen or Australian permanent resident
- Demonstrate Competent English as per the Department of Home Affairs requirements

If these conditions are met, you can claim 5 points under the partner skills category, even if your partner does not meet the full skilled partner criteria.

### 10 Points for Single Applicant When Partner is PR/Citizen

You may also receive 10 points if you are single and your partner is an Australian citizen or permanent resident.

However, take note that the Department assesses your relationship status carefully at the time of invitation and during processing and if they find any inconsistency, it can impact your eligibility.

## What You Should NOT Do in Partner Points Calculation

- Use ineligible occupation assessment: You can’t claim points if your partner’s skills assessment is for an occupation not on the relevant skilled list.
- Submit incomplete documents: Points can’t be claimed without all requirements being met together, including skills assessment, Competent English, age criteria, and an eligible occupation.
- Provide inconsistent partner details after invitation: Changing or misreporting partner information after receiving an invitation can lead to refusal or cancellation.

## FAQs

### **1. How many points can a partner add in Australia PR?**

A partner can add 5 or 10 points in the Australia PR points test, depending on their English level, skills assessment, and visa eligibility.

### **2. When do you get 10 partner points in Australia PR?**

You get 10 points if your partner is included in your visa, is under 45, has competent English, a skills assessment, and an occupation on the skilled list.

### **3. Can I get partner points without a skills assessment?**

Yes, but only 5 points. For 10 points, a valid skills assessment and eligible occupation are required.

### **4. What if my partner’s job is not on the skilled list?**

Then you can’t claim 10 points, even if they have experience or qualifications.

### **5. Can changing partner details affect PR chances?**

Yes. Any mismatch or change after invitation can lead to refusal or delays, so details must stay consistent.
