## What Is a Bridging Visa in Australia? Types, Purpose & Common Myths

**Bridging Visas in Australia: What Keeps You Lawful While Waiting?**

Your employer-sponsored visa application is lodged. The paperwork is done.

But the decision? That could take months.

So what keeps you lawful in Australia during the waiting period — especially when meeting critical requirements for visas like the 482 Temporary Skill Shortage or [186 Employer Nomination](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/employers/nomination-application/) Scheme?

**That’s where a Bridging Visa comes in**

As the name suggests, a bridging visa acts as a temporary “bridge”, allowing you to remain lawfully in Australia while the Department of Home Affairs processes your substantive visa application. Whether you’re transitioning from a student visa, [Visitor Visa Subclass 600](https://www.growmore.one/australian-visitor-visa-subclass-600-guide-expert-visa-agent-australia/), or another temporary visa, your bridging visa quietly becomes the legal foundation of your stay.

Yet despite its importance, bridging visas are widely misunderstood. From work rights and travel conditions to health insurance obligations and compliance risks, small mistakes can have serious consequences — including visa cancellation or refusal of your main application.

### **Who uses them?** 

Onshore applicants in Australia who lodge a valid new visa application _before_ their current substantive visa expires. This includes:

*   Skilled workers (e.g., pharmacy staff applying for 482 via employer nomination).
*   Partner visa applicants.
*   Students switching to work visas.

**Purpose and Benefits**

Bridging visas fill the gap between your expiring visa and a new application decision, keeping you lawful in Australia.

### **Purposes:**

*   Prevent unlawful status and 3-year re-entry bans.
*   Maintain compliance during the processing of employer-sponsored visas like 482/186.
*   Support key pathways (skilled, partner, protection claims).

### **Benefits:**

*   **Work Continuity**: Retain the same rights as prior visa (full-time if applicable).
*   **Family Stability**: Live together without separation risks.
*   **Business Advantage**: Sponsors keep talent during 6-18 month waits.

**Health Insurance for Bridging Visas**

Many people stop their health insurance when they switch to a bridging visa. They think, “I’m just waiting for my main visa decision, so I don’t need it anymore.”

However, Condition 8501 requires you to maintain continuous OVHC (Overseas Visitors Health Cover) with no gaps allowed. If your insurance lapses, it can lead to cancellation of your bridging visa or jeopardize your main visa application, such as a [482 employer-sponsored visa](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/individual/employer-sponsored-visa/).

**Common Myths About Bridging Visas**

**Here are the top 3 myths debunked simply:**

#### **Myth 1: Bridging visas = permanent residency**

_**Reality**_**:** Temporary only—ends when your real visa (482/186) is decided (grant/refusal/withdrawal**).**

#### **Myth 2: Unlimited work/travel rights**

_Reality_: Work matches prior visa; BVA = no travel (cancels if you leave). BVB needed for trips.

#### **Myth 3: No health insurance required**

**_Reality_:** Overseas Visitors Health Cover is mandatory—gaps can cancel your bridging visa.
