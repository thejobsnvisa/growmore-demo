Moving to regional Australia on a Subclass 491 is a big step, especially when your ultimate goal is Australia PR. You may have started a new job, settled into a new place and begun building a future in Australia. Naturally, the next question that comes to mind is: how can you apply for permanent residency?

Here, the Subclass 191 visa offers eligible Subclass 491 holders a pathway to permanent residency. However, it is not as simple as applying for another visa. You need to meet specific eligibility requirements, comply with your visa conditions and prepare the necessary documents before lodging your application.

So, let’s understand how one can move from Subclass 491 to Subclass 191 and achieve Australia PR.

## What Is the Subclass 191 Visa?

The Subclass 191 Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional) visa is a permanent visa pathway for eligible holders of certain regional provisional visas, including Subclass 491 and Subclass 494.

For a Subclass 491 holder, the important point is that the 191 pathway comes with requirements that must be satisfied during your provisional visa period.

In simple terms, holding a 491 visa does not automatically lead to permanent residency. You need to complete the required period, comply with your visa conditions and meet the Subclass 191 criteria when you apply.

## When Can a Subclass 491 Holder Apply for Subclass 191?

Generally, an eligible Subclass 491 holder can apply for the Subclass 191 visa after holding the eligible regional provisional visa for at least three years, provided all other requirements are met.

This three-year period is therefore an important milestone for 491 visa holders planning their Australia PR pathway.

But there is an important distinction here.

**Completing three years does not, by itself, guarantee eligibility for Subclass 191.**

You should also check your regional residence history, visa compliance, tax records and other applicable requirements before lodging the application.

## What Are the Main Subclass 191 Requirements?

If you are planning to move from Subclass 491 to Subclass 191, completing three years on your eligible visa is an important milestone. But there are several other requirements you need to meet before you can apply for permanent residency.

Here are the key Subclass 191 eligibility requirements to understand:

### 1. Hold an Eligible Visa for at Least Three Years

You must hold an eligible visa when you apply for Subclass 191. This includes the Subclass 491 Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa and Subclass 494 Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa.

You must have held an eligible visa for at least three years before applying for Subclass 191. If you are a Subclass 491 holder, check your visa grant date carefully before planning your application.

### 2. Meet the Income Requirement

There is no minimum income requirement for the Subclass 191 visa. However, you must provide Notices of Assessment issued by the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) for three income years out of the five years of your eligible visa.

So, you do not need to earn a specific amount each year, but you do need to have the required ATO Notices of Assessment. If you have worked as an employee, been self-employed or operated a business, make sure your tax records are properly maintained.

### 3. Comply With Your Visa Conditions

You must have complied with the conditions of your eligible visa while you were in Australia. This applies throughout the period you hold the eligible visa or visas.

If you changed your employment, moved to a different location, became self-employed or changed your work arrangements during your 491 visa period, make sure those changes complied with your visa conditions.

### 4. Meet the Health Requirement

You must meet Australia's health requirement to qualify for Subclass 191. This can also apply to family members applying for the visa with you.

In some circumstances, family members who are not applying for the visa may also need to meet the health requirement.

### 5. Meet the Character Requirement

You must meet Australia's character requirement. This also applies to family members aged 16 or older who apply for the visa with you.

Certain family members who are not applying for the visa may also need to meet the character requirement.

### 6. Sign the Australian Values Statement

If you are 16 or older, you must have read, or had explained to you, the *Life in Australia* booklet and sign an Australian Values Statement.

By signing the statement, you confirm that you will respect the Australian way of life and obey Australian laws.

### 7. Pay Back Any Australian Government Debt

If you or any family member owes money to the Australian Government, the debt must be paid back. An arrangement to repay the debt may also be accepted.

This requirement can apply even if the family member who owes the money is not applying for the Subclass 191 visa.

## Ready to Apply for Subclass 191?

Reaching three years on your Subclass 491 visa is an important milestone, but it is only one part of the Subclass 191 process.

Before applying, make sure you have checked your eligible visa history, ATO Notices of Assessment, compliance with your visa conditions, health and character requirements, Australian Values Statement, government debts and previous immigration history.
