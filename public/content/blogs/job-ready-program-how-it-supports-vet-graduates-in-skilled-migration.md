Completing a VET qualification in Australia can feel like a milestone, but as a student you may have a bigger goal in mind: building a future in Australia and eventually achieving Australia PR. After your studies are complete, you may start exploring migration options and wonder how your VET qualification and practical work experience can support your migration plans.

For VET graduates in certain trade occupations, the Job Ready Program can be an important part of this process. It allows eligible graduates to demonstrate their practical skills through employment and workplace assessment in Australia.

So, let us understand how this program works, what eligibility requirements you need to meet, and how this program fits into your skilled migration pathway towards Australian PR.

## What Is the Job Ready Program?

The Job Ready Program is a minimum 12-month employment-based skills assessment which is managed by Trades Recognition Australia.

The Job Ready Program is designed for international student graduates who have completed an Australian Vocational Education and Training course, are working in Australia and are seeking a pathway to a skilled visa.

The main purpose of JRP is to assess whether your practical skills and workplace experience meet the Australian standards for your nominated occupation.

During the program, TRA checks if you can perform your trade work safely, properly use the tools and equipment, follow workplace and industry standards, finish tasks to the required level and show the practical skills that are expected in your occupation.

If you successfully complete the program, TRA provides a Skills Assessment Outcome Letter. After that, this outcome can be used as part of an application for an Australian skilled visa.

## Who Is Eligible for the Job Ready Program?

To enter the program, you generally need to:

- Have a Provisional Skills Assessment (PSA) outcome from TRA that was issued within the previous three years.
- Be working in Australia in your nominated occupation.
- Have employment that can be approved by TRA for the program.
- Have a current passport.
- Provide evidence of your employment and required pay evidence.
- Ensure TRA is the appropriate assessing authority for your nominated occupation.

The JRP is not available for every trade occupation. For example, the current TRA guidelines do not allow Electrician (General), Electrician (Special Class), Plumber (General) and Air-conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic to join the program.

Therefore, before you plan your migration pathway around JRP, you should first confirm that your occupation and circumstances are eligible.

## How Does the Job Ready Program Work?

The JRP has three steps, and you must complete each step before progressing to the next.

### Step 1: Job Ready Employment

You begin by working in your nominated occupation in Australia and providing evidence of your employment and pay.

The JRP requires at least 12 months of full-time employment, or equivalent part-time employment. TRA considers 38 hours per week as full-time.

You may also claim up to three months of Prior Employment with your current employer if TRA accepts the required evidence. This claim must be made when you apply for JRE.

After six months of full-time employment, you must submit an Employment Confirmation Form and Skills Progress Report before progressing to the next stage.

### Step 2: Job Ready Workplace Assessment

The JRWA is a practical assessment conducted at your workplace by an approved RTO assessor.

The assessor checks whether you have developed the required technical and workplace skills, including your practical work, safety, communication and ability to perform routine and complex tasks.

If you are assessed as Not Yet Job Ready, you may need additional experience or training before another assessment.

### Step 3: Job Ready Final Assessment

After successfully meeting the previous requirements and completing the required 12 months of full-time employment, or equivalent part-time employment, you can apply for the JRFA.

TRA reviews your employment evidence and overall JRP completion. If successful, you receive an official Skills Assessment Outcome Letter.

## How Does JRP Fit Into an Australia PR Strategy?

For an eligible VET graduate, JRP can be an important bridge between Australian trade employment and skilled migration.

A simplified example would be:

1. Complete an eligible Australian VET qualification.
2. Obtain a successful Provisional Skills Assessment.
3. Start working in your nominated occupation in Australia.
4. Complete all three steps of Job Ready Program.
5. Receive your Skills Assessment Outcome Letter.
6. Use the assessment as part of an eligible skilled migration pathway, if you meet the remaining requirements.

## What Does JRP Cost?

Under the May 2026 TRA Job Ready Program Guidelines, the standard fees are:

| JRP Stage | Fee |
|---|---:|
| Job Ready Employment | AUD $490 |
| Job Ready Workplace Assessment | AUD $2,845 |
| Job Ready Final Assessment | AUD $75 |
| **Total** | **AUD $3,410** |

If you require another Job Ready Workplace Assessment, an additional AUD $2,845 fee applies.

TRA can change its fees, so applicants should check the current fees before submitting an application.

## Common Mistakes That Can Delay Your JRP

- **Working in an unrelated job:** These work hours may not be counted towards your JRP employment requirement.
- **Doing unpaid work:** Unpaid employment may not count towards the required employment period.
- **Incorrect ANZSCO occupation matching:** Your employment must align with your nominated occupation and may cause issues if it does not.
- **Incomplete documentation:** Missing or incorrect documents can delay the assessment process.
- **Missing employer evidence:** Incomplete employment verification or skills progress evidence may require further verification.
- **Delaying JRP registration:** Starting the JRP late can reduce the available time to complete the program while holding your current visa.

## Explore Your Australia Skilled Migration Options After Your VET Studies

If you are a VET graduate who meets the qualifications, the Job Ready Program can be part of a migration route that leads to visas like Subclass 189, Subclass 190 or Subclass 491. The exact visa you can get will depend on your experience, job title and other eligibility factors.

In some cases, employer-sponsored visas such as Subclass 482, Subclass 186 or Subclass 494 might also be a fit in your circumstances if you find a genuine employer who is ready to sponsor you.

However, JRP is not a mandatory requirement for every visa pathway, and completing it does not automatically make you eligible for any particular visa or Australia PR. Your occupation, skills assessment, work experience, English, points, employer requirements and other visa criteria need to be assessed before choosing a pathway.

## FAQs

### 1. What is the Job Ready Program in Australia?

The Job Ready Program is a TRA-managed skills assessment program that allows eligible VET graduates to demonstrate their practical skills through employment in Australia.

### 2. How long does the Job Ready Program take?

The program generally requires at least 12 months of full-time employment, or equivalent part-time employment, along with the required assessments.

### 3. Who can apply for the Job Ready Program?

Eligible applicants generally need an Australian VET qualification, a valid Provisional Skills Assessment, and employment in their nominated occupation in Australia.

### 4. Can JRP help with Australia PR?

JRP can support certain skilled migration pathways by providing a skills assessment outcome, but completing JRP does not guarantee Australia PR.

### 5. What visas can I apply for after JRP?

Depending on your occupation and eligibility, JRP may support skilled visa pathways such as Subclass 189, 190 or 491. Employer-sponsored options may also be available.
