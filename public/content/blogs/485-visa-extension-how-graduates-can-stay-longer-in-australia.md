The Subclass 485 visa is one of the most popular options for recent graduates, allowing eligible graduates to remain in Australia for an extended period. However, certain Temporary Graduate visa holders may have another option to stay longer in Australia, commonly referred to as the **“485 visa extension.”**

This “extension” does not simply add extra time to an existing 485 visa. Instead, eligible graduates must apply for a **new Temporary Graduate visa under the Second Post-Higher Education Work stream** and meet specific eligibility requirements.

In this guide, we explain how the 485 visa extension works, who may be eligible for a second Subclass 485 visa, who may not qualify, and how much additional time you could receive.

## What Is the 485 Visa Extension?

When people search for a **485 visa extension in Australia**, they are usually referring to the **Second Post-Higher Education Work stream** of the Temporary Graduate visa.

This stream is designed for certain Temporary Graduate visa holders who completed their qualifying studies at an education provider located in a **designated regional area**.

Depending on your study location and regional residence, this visa may allow you to remain in Australia for an additional **1 or 2 years**.

The visa application charge for the Second Post-Higher Education Work stream starts from **AUD 2,265**, with concessions available in limited circumstances.

## Who Can Get a 485 Visa Extension?

The Second Post-Higher Education Work stream is primarily designed for graduates whose Australian studies were completed at an education provider located in a designated regional area.

Generally, to qualify, you must:

* Be aged 35 or under when you apply, subject to specific age exceptions.
* Be in Australia when applying.
* Hold a Temporary Graduate visa in the Post-Higher Education Work, Post-Study Work or eligible Replacement stream.
* Have previously held a Temporary Graduate visa as the primary applicant in the relevant stream.
* Have obtained the qualification that led to your first 485 visa from a CRICOS-registered Australian education provider located in an eligible designated regional area.
* Have lived in a designated regional area for at least 2 years immediately before applying.
* Meet the applicable health and character requirements.
* Meet all other applicable visa criteria.

This means that simply moving to a regional area shortly before your current 485 visa expires does **not** automatically make you eligible.

Your previous study location and your regional residence history are important when determining whether you qualify for a second 485 visa.

## How Long Can You Stay on the 485 Visa Extension?

The 485 visa extension can provide an additional **one or two years** of stay, depending on the relevant regional categories and your circumstances.

The Department considers factors including:

* Where you completed the study that led to your first Temporary Graduate visa; and
* Where you lived while holding your first Temporary Graduate visa.

| Study and Residence Circumstances          |                                            Additional Stay |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------: |
| Eligible Category 2 regional circumstances | 1 year, with certain exceptions that may apply to Tasmania |
| Eligible Category 3 regional circumstances |                                                    2 years |
| Certain eligible Tasmania circumstances    |                                              Up to 2 years |

**Category 2 – Cities and Major Regional Centres** includes locations such as Perth, Adelaide, the Gold Coast, the Sunshine Coast, Canberra, Newcastle/Lake Macquarie, Wollongong/Illawarra, Geelong and Hobart.

**Category 3 – Regional Centres and Other Regional Areas** covers other locations classified by the Department as regional areas.

## Who Is Not Eligible for a 485 Visa Extension?

Not every 485 visa holder will qualify for a second 485 visa.

You may not be eligible if:

* Your qualifying study was completed at a non-regional campus.
* You have not lived in a designated regional area for the required period.
* You do not hold or have not previously held an eligible Temporary Graduate visa.
* You do not meet the applicable age, health, character or health insurance requirements.
* You do not meet other requirements for the Second Post-Higher Education Work stream.

Eligibility depends on your individual circumstances, including your qualification, education provider, study location, visa history and regional residence history.

## What Happens If You Do Not Qualify for a 485 Visa Extension?

If you are not eligible for a 485 visa extension, you do not necessarily need to leave Australia immediately.

Depending on your occupation, qualifications, work experience, points and individual circumstances, there may be other possible visa options available.

Potential pathways may include:

* **Skills in Demand visa (Subclass 482)**
* **Skilled Independent visa (Subclass 189)**
* **Skilled Nominated visa (Subclass 190)**
* **Skilled Work Regional visa (Subclass 491)**
* Other employer-sponsored or skilled migration pathways

The most suitable option depends on your individual circumstances and the visa criteria you can meet.

However, if you do not qualify for the 485 visa extension, other skilled or employer-sponsored pathways may still be available depending on your circumstances.
