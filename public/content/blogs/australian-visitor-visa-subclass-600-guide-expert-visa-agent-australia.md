**What Is the Australian Visitor Visa (Subclass 600)?**

The **Visitor Visa (Subclass 600)** is a **temporary visa** that allows individuals to visit Australia for:

*   Tourism and holidays
*   Visiting family or friends
*   Short-term business activities

Depending on your profile, the visa may be granted for 3, 6, or 12 months, with single or multiple entries.

(**This visa does not permit employment and is strictly for temporary stays.**)

### **Streams Under Australian Visitor Visa (Subclass 600)**

The **Subclass** 600 visa has **three distinct streams**;

**1\. Tourist Stream** **🧳**

**The Tourist Stream is ideal if you are travelling for:**

*   Holidays and sightseeing
*   Visiting friends or relatives
*   Leisure travel
*   Short-term recreational courses (up to 3 months)

**Key points:**

*   No sponsor required
*   Suitable for first-time and repeat travellers
*   Common validity: 3 or 6 months

Applicants with limited travel history often consult **Immigration Consultants** to present a strong and genuine tourist profile.

**2\. Family Sponsored Stream** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

This stream is for applicants visiting **close family members** in Australia with formal sponsorship.

**Eligible sponsors include:**

*   Australian citizens
*   Australian permanent residents
*   Eligible New Zealand citizens

#### **Important notes:**

*   A financial bond may be requested
*   Applications are assessed more strictly

**3\. Business Visitor Stream** 💼

**This stream allows short-term business-related travel, such as:**

*   Attending meetings or conferences
*   Business negotiations
*   Trade fairs or professional events

#### **Not permitted:**

*   Paid employment
*   Providing services to Australian clients

### **General Eligibility Criteria**

**All applicants, regardless of stream, must:**

*   Be a genuine temporary entrant
*   Show sufficient funds for the stay
*   Demonstrate strong ties to their home country
*   Meet health and character requirements
*   Intend to comply with visa conditions

Visa decisions are made by the Department of Home Affairs based on the overall strength and credibility of the application.

**Document Checklist for Visitor Visa (Subclass 600)**

Proper documentation is the foundation of a successful visa application.

#### **Mandatory Documents**

*   Valid passport (current and previous)
*   Passport-size photographs
*   Online visa application form
*   Cover letter explaining the purpose of travel
*   Proof of funds (bank statements, savings, fixed deposits, investments)

#### **Stream-Specific Documents**

*   **Tourist Stream:** Travel itinerary, hotel bookings
*   **Family Stream:** Invitation letter, sponsor documents, proof of relationship
*   **Business Stream:** Invitation from an Australian organisation, business profile

#### **Supporting Documents**

*   Employment letter or business registration
*   Leave approval (if employed)
*   Income tax returns
*   Property or asset documents
*   Previous travel history
