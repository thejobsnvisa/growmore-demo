If you completed your medical or health-related qualification overseas and now work in healthcare management, you may have a pathway to Australian permanent residency as a Primary Health Organisation Manager (ANZSCO 134213). 

If your career has moved beyond clinical practice into managing community health services, coordinating healthcare teams or overseeing the delivery of health programs, this occupation may be worth exploring. 

But there is also an important point for overseas-trained doctors: passing the Australian Medical Council (AMC) exams is not a mandatory requirement for a [VETASSESS skills assessment](https://www.vetassess.com.au/check-my-occupation/professional-occupations/primary-health-organisation-manager) under this occupation. Your qualifications and employment experience must meet the occupation-specific assessment criteria. 

The occupation has also featured in the [recent Subclass 189 invitation round](https://www.growmore.one/news/australia-releases-official-subclass-189-skillselect-data-20000-invitations-issued/) and Primary Health Organisation Managers received invitations at a minimum of 65 points. This makes it worth considering. However, an invitation score does not guarantee an invitation in future rounds because selection depends on the Department's invitation priorities and the applicant's circumstances. 

So, let's look at the qualification requirements, management experience and application process. 

## Can I Apply for PR Without Passing the AMC Exams? 

Yes. You may be eligible to apply for the Primary Health Organisation Manager occupation without passing the [AMC exams](https://www.amc.org.au/pathways) if your qualifications and employment history satisfy VETASSESS requirements. 

The main difference is skill assessment used for migration purposes only while the registration is required if you want to practice medicine in Australia. 

VETASSESS evaluates whether your academic qualifications and work experience are suitable for the nominated position. It does not assess whether you are qualified to practice as a registered practitioner. 

Therefore, if you wish to work as a Primary Health Organisation Manager in Australia, you must take additional steps to meet medical registration requirements set by the Australian Medical Council and the Medical Board of Australia. 

## Can My MBBS, Ayurveda, Homeopathy or Psychology Qualification Be Accepted? 

Your original qualification can play an important role in determining whether you meet the VETASSESS criteria. 

For [Primary Health Organisation Manager](https://www.vetassess.com.au/check-my-occupation/professional-occupations/primary-health-organisation-manager), VETASSESS recognises qualifications in fields such as: 

* Medicine and other professional health disciplines 
* Health administration 
* Health services management 

You should confirm that your qualification is at least equivalent to an Australian bachelor’s qualification or higher and closely associated with the nominated occupation. 

For example, an overseas MBBS qualification may satisfy the qualification requirement if VETASSESS considers its educational level and relevance appropriate. 

If you hold a qualification in Ayurveda, homeopathy, psychology or another health-related discipline, you should not assume that it will automatically be accepted. VETASSESS assesses the individual qualification, including its content, level and relevance. 

**One important point:** Having a medical or health-related degree alone is not enough. You must also demonstrate the required employment experience in healthcare management. 

## What Management Experience Does VETASSESS Accept? 

This is where many applicants need to look more closely at their employment history. 

VETASSESS is not simply looking for someone who has worked in the healthcare industry or held a senior clinical position. The assessment focuses on whether you have actually managed a primary health organisation delivering a broad range of out-of-hospital health services. 

For example, managing a multidisciplinary community health organisation offering nursing, allied health and community health programs across multiple locations may be relevant. 

Your role should involve responsibilities such as: 

* Overseeing healthcare services and program delivery. 
* Managing multidisciplinary teams and staff. 
* Planning and monitoring operational budgets. 
* Handling staffing and resource allocation. 
* Maintaining service delivery standards. 

However, there is an important limitation:  

Managing an individual healthcare practice is not considered suitable employment for this occupation under the VETASSESS criteria. This includes managing a GP clinic, medical centre or dental clinic. 

Likewise, working as a doctor, nurse or other healthcare professional does not automatically qualify. Your employment must demonstrate genuine organisational management responsibilities, not primarily clinical work. 

## How to Apply for an Australian Subclass 189 Visa as a Primary Health Organisation Manager 

If your qualifications and experience meet the occupation requirements, the process generally involves three steps. 

## Step 1: Obtain a Positive VETASSESS Skills Assessment 

You must obtain a positive skills assessment from [VETASSESS for Primary Health Organisation Manager (ANZSCO 134213)](https://www.vetassess.com.au/check-my-occupation/professional-occupations/primary-health-organisation-manager) before submitting your EOI. 

Generally, you need at least one year of highly relevant post-qualification employment within the last five years, at the required skill level and for at least 20 hours per week. 

Your evidence should clearly establish your management responsibilities and the organisation's health services. This may include detailed employment references and an organisational chart showing your position, reporting lines and team structure. 

## Step 2: Submit Your EOI Through SkillSelect 

Once you have a positive skills assessment, you can submit an [Expression of Interest (EOI)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/working-in-australia/skillselect/expression-of-interest) for the Subclass 189 visa. 

You must meet the key requirements, including: 

* Score at least 65 points. 
* Be under 45 years of age when invited. 
* Meet the Competent English requirement. 
* Hold a suitable skills assessment. 

Remember, [65 points](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/supporting/Pages/Work/189-points-table.aspx) is the minimum eligibility threshold, not a guarantee of an invitation. Your actual chances depend on invitation rounds and the points scores of other candidates. 

## Step 3: Lodge Your Visa Application After Invitation 

If you receive an invitation, you have 60 days to lodge your [Subclass 189 visa application](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-independent-189/points-tested). 

You will need supporting evidence for your qualifications, employment history, English proficiency and points claims, along with the required health and character documents. 

Make sure your visa application matches the information in your EOI. Any unsupported claims or inconsistencies can cause delays or affect the outcome. 

## Could Your Healthcare Management Experience Qualify for Australian PR? 

If you are an overseas-trained doctor or health professional who has moved into healthcare management, the Primary Health Organisation Manager occupation may be worth considering for your Australian skilled migration plans.
