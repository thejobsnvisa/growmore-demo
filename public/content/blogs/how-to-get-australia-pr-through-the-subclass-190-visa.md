## What is the Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190)? 

The Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190) is a permanent residence visa for skilled workers who are nominated by an Australian state or territory government. It is a points-tested visa designed for applicants whose occupations are in demand in Australia and who meet the eligibility requirements set by both the Australian Government and the nominating state or territory.  

You can apply for this visa from inside or outside of Australia after meeting the applicable visa requirements. 

## Benefits of the Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190) 

Many benefits come with the approval of a 190 visa Australia, which are as follows: 

* Permanent residency from the date of visa grant.
* Work and study rights anywhere in Australia. 
* 5 additional points in your State nomination Australia test score.
* Travel in and out of Australia for up to five years. (renewable) 
* Suitability for various occupations across multiple industries. 
* You can include your spouse and dependent children in the application. 
* Apply for Australian citizenship if you meet the eligibility requirements. 

## Who Can Apply for Subclass 190 Visa? 

Subclass 190 Visa Eligibility includes several requirements that applicants must meet before they can apply for the visa. The key eligibility criteria are listed below:

* **Have an Eligible Visa (If you are applying in Australia):** Must hold a substantive visa or an eligible Bridging Visa (Subclass 010, 020, or 030). 
* **Be Invited to Apply:** You must submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) through SkillSelect and receive an invitation to apply for the visa. 
* **Be Nominated by an Australian State or Territory:** You must be nominated by an Australian state or territory government agency. Each state and territory has its own nomination criteria. 
* **Have a Suitable Skills Assessment:** You must have a valid skills assessment for your nominated occupation at the time of invitation. Generally, it must have been obtained within the three years before receiving your invitation. 
* **Be Under 45 Years of Age:** You must be under 45 years old when you receive your invitation to apply. Turning 45 after receiving an invitation does not affect your eligibility. 
* **Score at Least 65 Points:** The Subclass 190 Visa is a points-tested visa. You must score a minimum of 65 points to submit your EOI. 
* **Meet the English Language Requirement:** You must demonstrate at least competent English at the time of invitation. 
* **Meet the Skills Requirement:** Your nominated occupation must be listed on the relevant skilled occupation list for the Subclass 190 Visa. 
* **Other Requirements:** You and any included family members must meet Australia's health and character requirements, and have no outstanding debts to the Australian Government. Plus, every applicant of age 18 or over must sign the Australian Values Statement that confirms your commitment to Australia's laws and values.

## Common Reasons Applicants Miss Out on State Nomination 

Many applicants are eligible to receive an invitation via subclass 190, but miss out on the chance just because of avoidable mistakes. These include: 

* Selecting the wrong occupation. 
* Claiming incorrect points. 
* Choosing unsuitable states. 
* Submitting incomplete documentation. 
* Applying without understanding state-specific requirements. 
* Ignoring state occupation priorities. 

Australian state nomination programs regularly change their criteria based on labour market needs. What works for one occupation or state may not work for another. A strategic approach is often more important than simply meeting the minimum eligibility requirements.

## FAQs 

**1. What is the Subclass 190 Visa?**  
The Subclass 190 Visa is a permanent residence visa for skilled workers nominated by an Australian state or territory government. 

**2. Can I apply for a Subclass 190 Visa without a job offer?**  
Yes. A job offer is not mandatory for the Subclass 190 Visa, although some states may have their own employment-related nomination requirements. 

**3. Does the Subclass 190 Visa give permanent residency?**  
Yes. The Subclass 190 Visa grants permanent residency in Australia once approved. 

**4. What is the difference between the Subclass 189 and Subclass 190 Visa?**  
The Subclass 189 Visa does not require state nomination, while the Subclass 190 Visa requires nomination from an Australian state or territory and provides an additional 5 points. 

**5. Can I move to another state after getting a Subclass 190 Visa?**  
Yes. However, you are generally expected to live and work in the state or territory that nominated you, as some states may require a 2-year commitment period as part of their nomination conditions.
