## 1\. Eligibility Check Up

She came to us with one big question: “**Am I eligible**?”. She shared all of her qualifications, like she has done a Master of Business Administration and has 5 years of experience as a Retail Manager in India. Along with that, she also had a Certificate IV in Retail Management.

She said that she had an employer in Queensland who is ready to sponsor her as a Retail Manager. So, our RMA has reviewed all her eligibility, documents, and made sure that her role (Retail Manager – ANZSCO 142111) for which she was applying matches all the job descriptions.

## 2\. SBS Approval For the Right Employer

After the complete review, the actual process for a [482 visa](https://www.growmore.one/want-to-work-as-a-chef-in-australia-know-how-to-get-a-482-visa/) started by making her employer an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS). We collaboratively worked with our client’s employer to:

* Gather and verify legal and business documents that prove the company is lawfully operating in Australia.

* Collect financial evidence, such as profit and loss statements, tax returns, and Business Activity Statements (BAS), to demonstrate its financial stability.

* Confirm the business’s size, scope, and operational legitimacy that justify the employer’s ability to sponsor overseas workers.

After that, we submitted the SBS application via the Department of Home Affairs’ online portal and provided all the required supporting documents. It was approved within a few days, and the employer received SBS status for a period of 5 years.

**This thing allowed an employer to nominate our client for the 482 Subclass Visa program.**

## 3\. Employer Nomination: Official Endorsement

After securing **SBS approval**, it was time to move to the next critical step—Employer Nomination. This is where things get real. The employer officially nominates our client for the Retail Manager role under the Subclass 482 visa, locking in the position and proving the job is genuine. No nomination, no visa—simple as that.

This step is often where people screw things up with weak paperwork or non-compliant roles. Whether you’re applying for management positions or exploring [Subclass 482 Aged Care Jobs](https://www.growmore.one/dreaming-of-aged-care-jobs-in-australia-start-with-subclass-482/), the nomination has to be rock-solid, properly justified, and 100% compliant. Do it right, and you move forward. Do it wrong, and the whole application can fall apart—fast.

We worked with the employer to define the Retail Manager role and ensure that it aligned with the Department of Home Affairs’ criteria. We helped the employer show the genuine need for a Retail Manager in its company who can manage operations, lead teams, and drive business growth. After that, we submitted the nomination application.

This nomination got approval, which means our client came one step closer to their dream job. Now the employer could offer a full-time, paid position to our client as a “Retail Manager”, which is also in line with Australian standards.

## 4\. Submitting a Visa Application

Finally, we moved on to the last part, which is the visa application submission. We guided our client through the whole visa application process, ensuring every detail was correct and complete. This included:

* Submitting the required documents, such as identity proof, employment history, qualifications, and any other supporting paperwork.

* Ensuring that the visa application complied with all Australian immigration laws.

After doing all this, we submitted her visa application for the 482 subclass on 3rd March 2025. Once the application was submitted, we worked closely with the Department of Home Affairs to monitor the status of the application, ensuring any questions or additional information requests by the government were handled promptly.
