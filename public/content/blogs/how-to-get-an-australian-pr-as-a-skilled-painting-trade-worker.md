Australia is one of the countries currently in need of a large number of skilled migrants. One of those highly skilled occupations that comes in the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL) is Skilled Painting Trade Worker. So, if you are a painter with all the essential skills and dreaming of securing permanent residency in Australia, then the Skilled Independent Visa (subclass 189) proves a fantastic pathway.

With the help of this visa, you can live and work anywhere in Australia permanently. Plus, you do not require any [type of employer nomination](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/employers/nomination-application/) to apply for this visa. So, let us know about the whole process to apply for the subclass 189, which will help you in taking the first step towards building your future as a Painting Trade Worker in Australia.

**Step 1: Confirm Your Occupation Eligibility**

*   First thing is to confirm that your occupation must be a paint trades worker with ANZSCO code 332211.
*   This occupation involves work related to applying paint, varnish, wallpaper, and other finishes to protect, maintain, and decorate the surfaces of buildings.

**Step 2: Obtain a Positive Skills Assessment from Trades Recognition Australia (TRA)**

*   Trades Recognition Australia (TRA) is the official assessing authority for Painting Trades Worker.
*   You must obtain a positive skills assessment before applying for the visa.
*   This is mainly a paper-based assessment, so no technical interview or practical test is required for painting trades.

### **Eligibility Criteria for TRA Skills Assessment:**

*   You can obtain the AQF Certificate III via Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) or formal training that is comparable.
*   Have 6 years of full-time employment experience as a painter and decorator, which is either pre- or post-qualifications.
*   Have 3 years of full-time employment experience plus a relevant formal qualification such as Certificate III in Painting and Decorating (AQF Level 3).

**Step 3: Meet Age and English Language Requirements**

*   You must be under 45 years of age at the time you receive your visa invitation.
*   Demonstrate competent English or higher through tests like IELTS, PTE, or TOEFL. (Make sure to aim for the updated score level for any particular test.)
*   Try to get higher English proficiency scores, and you will gain more points on the points test.

**Step 4: Aim to Achieve Maximum Points**

A minimum of 65 points is required to be eligible to apply for 189 visas, but maximum points will enhance your chances of getting an invitation.

Factors that will impact your score are as follows:

*   **Age:** Get the maximum points benefits related to age if you are between 25-32 years old.
*   **English:** Superior English level gains additional points
*   **Qualifications:** Relevant Australian and overseas qualifications give extra points.

  
You can count your point score via [https://www.growmore.one/points-calculator/](https://www.growmore.one/points-calculator/).

**Step 5: Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) via SkillSelect**

*   Submit your EOI online through Australia’s SkillSelect portal.
*   Provide accurate details of your skills assessment, English test, qualifications, work experience, and points claimed.
*   Wait for an invitation to apply.

#### **Key Tips:**

*   Keep all documents ready and consistent with your EOI claims.
*   Update your EOI if your circumstances change.

**Step 6: Receive Invitation and Submit Your Visa Application**

*   Upon receiving an invitation, lodge your visa application online within 60 days.
*   Provide:
    *   Skills assessment outcome
    *   English test results
    *   Employment and qualification evidence
    *   Health and character clearance documents
*   Pay visa application fees.

**Step 7: Visa Grant and Permanent Residency**

*   Once approved, you and your eligible family members receive Australian Permanent Residency.
*   Now, enjoy full rights to live, work, and study anywhere in Australia.
*   Access to Medicare and social benefits.
*   Eligible for Australian citizenship after meeting residency requirements.
