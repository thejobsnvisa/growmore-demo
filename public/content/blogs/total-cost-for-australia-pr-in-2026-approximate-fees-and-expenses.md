
If you're planning to apply for Australian Permanent Residency (PR), one question always comes to the top of your mind: **"How much will Australia PR cost?"** 

It's a fair question, as many applicants come across the government visa application charge and assume that's the only amount they'll need to pay. However, in reality, it is just one part of the overall cost of the visa application process. 

If you're applying through Australia's **General Skilled Migration (GSM)** program, there are several other mandatory expenses you should budget for, including a skills assessment, an English language test, medical examinations, and police clearance certificates. 

In this guide, we will break down the total approximate cost for Australia PR in 2026 for General Skilled Migration applicants so that you can plan your budget accordingly.

## Detailed Cost Breakdown

### 1. Australia PR Visa Application Fees

As of 2026, the Department of Home Affairs charges a base visa application fee of **AUD 6,135** for the primary applicant under the **Subclass 189**, **Subclass 190**, and **Subclass 491** visa programs. 

Additional charges apply if you include family members under the same visa application. For all three visa subclasses, the charge for each additional applicant aged 18 years or older is approximately AUD 3,070. For applicants under 18 years of age, the additional charge is AUD 1,540 for the Subclass 189 visa and AUD 1,535 for both the Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 visas. 

*The Australian Government changes visa charges periodically, so applicants should always verify the latest fees before submitting their application.*

### 2. Skills Assessment Fees

Before submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) for most skilled visas, applicants must obtain a positive skills assessment from the relevant assessing authority. The fee primarily depends on your nominated occupation and the relevant assessing authority. 

Some commonly used authorities and their approximate fee ranges include: 

* **VETASSESS** (AUD 1,096.00 – AUD 1,205.60)
* **Engineers Australia** ( AUD 315 – AUD 940 )
* **Australian Computer Society (ACS)** ( AUD 625 – AUD 1,498 )
* **CPA Australia** ( AUD 514 – AUD 565 )
* **Australian Nursing & Midwifery Accreditation Council (ANMAC)** ( AUD 395 – AUD 595 )
* **Trades Recognition Australia (TRA)** ( AUD 795 – AUD 5,320 }

*Certain occupations may require practical assessments, document verification, competency demonstrations, or additional assessment stages, which can increase the overall cost.*


### 3. English Language Test Fees

Most skilled migration applicants must demonstrate English language proficiency. 

Accepted tests include: 

*   IELTS
*   PTE Academic
*   TOEFL iBT
*   Cambridge C1 Advanced
*   OET (*for eligible occupations*)
*   CELPIP General
*   LANGUAGECERT Academic
*   MET

For this, the examination fee generally ranges between: 

**AUD 150 – AUD 400**

*However, the actual cost of an English language test depends on the country where you sit the exam, the test provider, and the test centre. As a result, fees may be higher or lower than the range above depending on your location*


### 4. State Nomination Fees (If Applicable)

Applicants applying under the **Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190)** or **Skilled Work Regional Visa (Subclass 491)** may need to pay a state or territory nomination application fee. 

Not every state or territory charges a nomination fee, but some do, and all have their own pricing structure. This typically ranges between: **AUD 0 – AUD 500**

*So, always check the latest nomination requirements of your chosen state or territory before applying. *


### 5. Medical Examination Costs

Every applicant included in the PR application must complete a health examination with an approved panel physician. Medical examinations generally include: 
*   General health assessment  
*   Chest X-ray  
*   Blood tests  
*   Urine tests (*where applicable*)  

The cost varies depending on the country where it is undertaken, the approved panel clinic, the applicant's age, and the specific tests required. Fees differ significantly between countries, but they generally range from **AUD 50 – AUD 350** per person when converted to Australian dollars.


### 6. Police Clearance Certificates

Applicants aged 16 years or older are generally required to provide police clearance certificates from Australia (*if applicable*) and every country where they have lived for the required period. The fee depends on the issuing authority. 

Most applicants spend approximately: **AUD 10 – AUD 100** per certificate. If you have lived in multiple countries, your overall expense may be higher.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How much does Australia PR cost in 2026?
The approximate cost for a single applicant under the General Skilled Migration (GSM) program generally ranges from **AUD 6,500 to AUD 13,500+** (excluding optional migration agent fees). The total cost depends on your visa subclass, occupation, family size, and individual circumstances. 

### 2. Do I need to pay the Australia PR visa application fee before submitting my Expression of Interest (EOI)?
No.There is no fee to submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) through SkillSelect. The Department of Home Affairs visa application charge is only payable after you receive an invitation to apply and lodge your visa application. 

### 3. Are Australia PR application fees refundable?
Generally, no. Department of Home Affairs visa application charges and most related expenses, such as skills assessments and English language tests, are non-refundable, even if your application is unsuccessful. 

### 4. Is it mandatory to apply for Australia PR through a Registered Migration Agent?
No. You can apply for Australia PR yourself. However, many applicants choose to work with a Registered Migration Agent (RMA) for professional guidance and assistance throughout the application process. 

### 5. Are Australia PR fees the same for all visa subclasses?
No. While the visa application charge is similar across the main GSM visa subclasses, the total cost varies. For example, Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 may include state nomination fees, while Subclass 189 does not. Other expenses also depend on your individual circumstances.
