Are you exploring ways to work in Australia but concerned that you may not meet every skilled migration requirement? The Designated Area Migration Agreement (DAMA) may be worth exploring as an alternative employer-sponsored pathway.

Several regional areas in Australia have DAMA arrangements that allow eligible employers to sponsor overseas workers for occupations facing local labour shortages. One such option is the NSW Orana DAMA, which may provide overseas workers with access to a wider range of eligible occupations and concessions on age, English language and salary requirements.

So, let’s explore how the NSW Orana DAMA works, the eligibility requirements, available visa concessions and potential pathways to Australia PR.

## What Is the NSW Orana DAMA?

The Regional Development Australia (RDA) Orana Designated Area Migration Agreement is a five-year employer-sponsored labour agreement between RDA Orana, the designated area representative, and the Australian Government.

It allows eligible employers operating in designated regional areas of NSW to access a broader range of occupations than some standard skilled migration pathways offer. Employers may also request specific concessions where these are available for their nominated occupations.

Currently, this DAMA includes 129 occupations across a range of industries.

## Which Regions Does Orana DAMA Cover?

The RDA Orana DAMA includes the following areas across regional NSW:

- **Orana:** Includes Dubbo, Narromine, Cobar, Bourke and other local government areas.
- **Central West:** Includes Orange, Bathurst, Parkes, Forbes and surrounding areas.
- **Riverina:** Includes Wagga Wagga, Griffith, Leeton and other designated local government areas.
- **Murray:** Includes Albury, Wentworth and other specified areas.
- **Southern Inland:** Includes Goulburn, Yass Valley, Queanbeyan-Palerang and other designated areas.

## Orana DAMA Concessions: What Flexibility May Be Available?

The Orana DAMA includes several types of concessions, but not every occupation qualifies for every concession.

### Age Concessions

Under the current Orana DAMA arrangements, age concessions for the relevant permanent residence pathway vary by ANZSCO skill level:

- **Skill Levels 1–4:** An age concession of up to 55 years may be available.
- **Skill Level 5:** An age concession of up to 50 years may be available.

### English Language Concessions

For occupations eligible for an English language concession, the Orana DAMA publishes the following overall test score equivalencies for tests taken on or after 7 August 2025:

| English Test | Published Overall Score |
|---|---:|
| IELTS Academic or General Training, as accepted for the relevant visa | 5.0 |
| PTE Academic | 31 |
| TOEFL iBT | 37 |
| Cambridge C1 Advanced | 161* |
| OET | 1020 |
| CELPIP General | 5 |
| LanguageCert Academic | 46 |
| MET | 44 |

These scores are not universally available to all Orana DAMA applicants. The occupation list identifies which occupations may access the English concession.

The requirements also vary by visa pathway. For the subclass 482 and subclass 494 pathways, the published concession generally uses the overall score without minimum scores in each component, while for the subclass 186 pathway, the published IELTS concession requires an overall score of 5.0 with no component below 4.0.

### Salary Concessions

For nomination applications lodged between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027, the standard Core Skills Income Threshold (CSIT) is AUD 79,423, excluding superannuation. However, the applicable income threshold and salary rules depend on the visa and nomination stream.

The Orana DAMA provides two types of salary threshold concessions for specified occupations.

**Type 1: Monetary Earnings Concession**

The employer must offer monetary earnings of at least 90% of the applicable threshold while meeting relevant award requirements and the annual market salary rate.

**Type 2: Monetary and Eligible Non-Monetary Earnings**

The employer must offer monetary earnings of at least 90% of the threshold, with eligible non-monetary benefits, such as food and board, covering up to 10%. The total package must meet the full threshold.

### Qualifications and Work Experience Concessions

Some Orana DAMA occupations have specific variations relating to qualifications, work experience or skills requirements.

Whether a concession is available depends on the nominated occupation, its ANZSCO skill level and the applicable visa pathway. If no relevant concession applies, the applicant must meet the standard requirements for the visa.

## Can Orana DAMA Lead to Australian Permanent Residency?

Orana DAMA can offer pathways to Australian permanent residency through employer-sponsored visas, depending on your occupation and eligibility.

### Pathway 1: Subclass 482 to Subclass 186

Eligible workers sponsored under Orana DAMA may be able to apply for the Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) after at least two years of relevant employment, based on the applicable visa and nomination requirements.

### Pathway 2: Subclass 494 to Subclass 191

Eligible subclass 494 visa holders may be able to apply for the Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional) visa (subclass 191) after meeting the applicable three-year and other visa requirements. A new employer nomination is not required for the subclass 191 application.

## How Does the Orana DAMA Process Work?

The process starts with the employer, not the individual worker.

### Step 1: Identify an Eligible Regional Employer

Find an employer operating in the Orana DAMA region who is willing to sponsor you.

### Step 2: Check the Occupation and Concessions

Confirm that your occupation is covered and check whether any concessions apply.

### Step 3: Seek Employer Endorsement

The employer applies for endorsement through the Orana DAMA Designated Area Representative (DAR).

### Step 4: Apply for the Relevant Labour Agreement

Once endorsed, the employer seeks a labour agreement with the Australian Government.

### Step 5: Lodge the Nomination

The employer nominates the position and worker under the relevant visa pathway.

### Step 6: Lodge the Visa Application

The worker applies for the relevant visa and must meet its eligibility requirements.

## How Much Does Orana DAMA Cost?

The employer pays the Orana DAMA endorsement processing fee. The current published fees are:

| Fee Type | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Initial endorsement, first five positions | AUD 990 plus GST per position |
| Initial endorsement, each position after the first five | AUD 550 plus GST per position |
| Variation adding positions | AUD 800 plus GST per position |
| Variation for specified smaller changes | AUD 500 plus GST per position |

These are Orana DAMA processing fees, not the total cost of sponsoring a worker. Other charges may include the visa application fee, nomination fee, Skilling Australians Fund levy and skills assessment or verification costs, where applicable.

## FAQs

### 1. Can I apply for the Orana DAMA without an employer?

No. You need an eligible employer in the designated region who is willing to sponsor you for an eligible occupation.

### 2. Which visas are available under the Orana DAMA?

Depending on the approved labour agreement, pathways may include subclass 482, subclass 494 and subclass 186 visas.

### 3. Can the Orana DAMA lead to Australian PR?

Yes. Eligible workers may qualify for PR through pathways such as subclass 186 or subclass 191, subject to the relevant visa requirements.

### 4. Does the Orana DAMA offer age and English concessions?

Yes. Concessions may be available for specified occupations, but eligibility depends on the occupation and visa pathway.

### 5. Does the Orana DAMA guarantee a job or visa approval?

No. Applicants need an eligible sponsoring employer and must meet the applicable visa requirements. Sponsorship and visa approval are not guaranteed.
