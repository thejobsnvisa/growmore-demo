## 1. Contract Administrator

A **Contract Administrator** may be suitable even for a small cleaning company where the business manages multiple commercial cleaning contracts, clients or sites.

The role may involve administering contracts, variations, renewals, contractual documentation and obligations between the company and its clients. The actual duties must genuinely correspond with the nominated occupation.

## 2. Office Manager

Where the cleaning company operates from an established office and has sufficient administrative activities, an **Office Manager** may potentially be considered.

The business should be able to demonstrate a genuine requirement to manage its office systems, procedures, records and administrative operations. Having an office alone does not automatically establish eligibility.

## 3. Accountant (General)

An **Accountant (General)** may potentially be considered where the cleaning company has sufficient financial operations to genuinely require an in-house accounting position.

Relevant occupation caveats may apply, including requirements relating to business turnover and employee numbers. In particular, a business with **turnover exceeding $1 million and at least five employees** may potentially meet those particular business-size components, subject to all other requirements.

## 4. Cleaner – DAMA Pathway

A **Cleaner is generally not available through the standard subclass 482 Core Skills pathway**. However, certain cleaning occupations may be available under a **Designated Area Migration Agreement (DAMA)**.

The business must operate within the relevant DAMA area and the particular cleaning occupation must be available under that agreement.

Depending on the DAMA and occupation, concessions relating to English, work experience, age or salary may also be available.

## Which Visa Pathway?

### Subclass 482 – Skills in Demand Visa

For the **subclass 482 Skills in Demand visa**, an applicant generally requires at least **one year of relevant work experience**, appropriate skills and qualifications where applicable, and must satisfy the relevant English requirement.

### Subclass 494 – Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional Visa

For the **subclass 494 Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional visa**, an applicant will generally require at least **three years of relevant post qualification work experience**, an appropriate skills assessment unless exempt, relevant skills and qualifications, and **Competent English**.

The position must also be in a designated regional area.

### DAMA Pathway

For a **DAMA pathway**, different eligibility requirements and concessions may apply depending on the region and nominated occupation.

## Can a Small Cleaning Company Sponsor?

Yes. A small cleaning company may potentially sponsor an overseas worker, but the position must be **genuine, commercially reasonable and appropriate to the business**.

For a small cleaning company, the four occupations particularly worth assessing are:

1. **Contract Administrator**
2. **Office Manager**
3. **Accountant (General)**
4. **Cleaner**, where an appropriate DAMA pathway is available
