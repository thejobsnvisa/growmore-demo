**Eligibility Requirements**

Australia offers several PR pathways under the **General Skilled Migration (GSM) Program**. The most popular PR visa options include: 

### **Subclass 189 – Skilled Independent Visa**

For skilled workers without employer or state government sponsorship, this is a point-tested visa. To qualify, you have to:

• At the time of the invitation, be younger than 45.  
• Your line of work is listed on the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL).  
• Have a favorable skills evaluation from the appropriate evaluating body  
• Complete the Points Test with at least 65 points.  
• Be proficient in English (IELTS 6 in each band, or equivalent)

### **Subclass 190 – Skilled Nominated Visa**

Like the 189, but it needs to be nominated by a state or territory in Australia. A state nomination can greatly improve your chances of getting an invitation and adds five points to your score.

### **Subclass 491 – Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa**

For individuals who are willing to live and work in regional Australia, there is a five-year provisional visa. With the Subclass 191 visa, it can result in permanent residency. Sponsorship is also available for relatives in regional areas who meet the eligibility requirements.

To qualify for these visas, applicants must meet specific eligibility criteria based on the **Australia points-based immigration system**. 

**Key Eligibility Criteria** 

### **1\. Age Requirement** 

Applicants must be between 18 and 45 years at the time of invitation.

### **2\. Skilled Occupation** 

Your occupation must be listed on Australia’s Skilled Occupation List (SOL).

### **3\. Skills Assessment**

 You must obtain a positive skills assessment from the relevant Australian assessing authority. 

### **4\. English Language Proficiency** 

Applicants must prove English proficiency through tests such as: 

*   IELTS 

*   PTE Academic 

*   TOEFL 

*   OET 

*   CELPIP General,  

*   LanguageCert Academic 

*   Michigan English Test (MET) 

### **5\. Points Test** 

You must score **at least 65 points** on the Australian immigration points test. 

### **6\. Health and Character Requirements** 

Applicants must pass medical examinations and provide police clearance certificates. Meeting these criteria is the first step toward securing your **Australia PR visa**.

****The Step-by-Step PR Application Process**** 

Applying for Australia PR involves several stages. Understanding the process can help you prepare effectively. 

### **Step 1: Check Eligibility** 

The first step is to determine whether you qualify for Australia PR based on age, occupation, qualifications, and points score. 

### **Step 2: Skills Assessment** 

Your education and work experience must be assessed by the relevant authority for your occupation. 

### **Step 3: English Language Test** 

Take an approved English language test such as IELTS, PTE, etc and obtain the required score. 

### **Step 4: Submit Expression of Interest (EOI)** 

Create and submit your **EOI in the SkillSelect system**, providing details about your qualifications, experience, and points score. 

### **Step 5: Receive Invitation to Apply (ITA)** 

If your profile is selected, you will receive an **Invitation to Apply for the PR visa**. 

### **Step 6: Submit Visa Application** 

After receiving the invitation, submit your visa application along with the required documents. 

### **Step 7: Medical and Character Verification** 

Complete medical examinations and provide police clearance certificates. 

### **Step 8: Visa Decision** 

Once all checks are completed, the Department of Home Affairs will issue a decision on your PR visa application.
