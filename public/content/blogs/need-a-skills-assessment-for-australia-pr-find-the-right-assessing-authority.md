If you are planning to apply for Australia PR through skilled migration, you have likely heard about one of the most important requirements: a positive skills assessment.

To get a positive skills assessment, you must apply to the correct assessing authority for your occupation. This is where many applicants get confused.

Should you apply through VETASSESS, Engineers Australia, ACS, Trades Recognition Australia (TRA), or another assessing body? With more than 40 recognised assessing authorities in Australia, it is natural to feel unsure.

So, let's clear your confusion and find out which Skills Assessment Authority is right for your Australia PR journey.

## Why Skills Assessment is Important?

A skills assessment is a formal assessment process of an individual that is conducted by an authority approved by the Australian government. By applying for this assessment, applicants can verify their professional experience and the knowledge gained through overseas degrees for the purpose of working in Australia.

A positive skills assessment helps you meet the eligibility requirements for [skilled migration visas](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/individual/general-skilled-visa/) and strengthens your [Australia PR application](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/how-do-i-apply-for-australia-pr-a-step-by-step-expert-guide/). Without a positive skills assessment (where required), you generally cannot proceed with your Expression of Interest (EOI) or skilled visa application.

## Which Australian Visas Require a Skills Assessment?

A positive skills assessment is a key requirement for many Australian skilled and employer-sponsored visas. However, whether it is mandatory or not depends on the visa subclass, your occupation, and the stream you apply under.

| Visa Subclass | Skills Assessment Requirement |
|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent Visa) | Mandatory |
| Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated Visa) | Mandatory |
| Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional Visa) | Mandatory |
| Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand Visa) | Required for certain occupations |
| Subclass 186 (Employer Nomination Scheme Visa) | Required for the Direct Entry stream |
| Subclass 494 (Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa) | Required for the Employer Sponsored stream |

## How Long Does a Skills Assessment Take?

Processing times for skills assessments vary depending on the assessing authority, occupation, and assessment pathway. Some assessments may take a few weeks, while others can take several months.

A positive skills assessment is generally valid for 3 years from the date of issue. However, some authorities have different validity periods. For example, the ACS skills assessment for IT professionals is generally valid for 2 years.

## Tips to Get a Positive Skills Assessment for Australia PR

A positive skills assessment is an important step towards your Australia PR goal. To improve your chances of approval, **keep these key points in mind:**

- **Choose the correct occupation:** Make sure your nominated occupation matches your qualifications, skills, and work experience.
- **Apply through the right assessing authority:** Each occupation has a specific Skills Assessment Authority, so applying to the wrong one can lead to delays or refusal.
- **Prepare strong employment evidence:** Provide accurate documents such as reference letters, payslips, contracts, and other proof of your work experience.
- **Check qualification requirements:** Ensure your education and professional qualifications meet the standards set by your assessing authority.
- **Provide complete and accurate information:** Missing documents or incorrect details can negatively impact your assessment outcome.
- **Seek professional guidance if needed:** Expert advice from registered migration agent in Australia can help you avoid mistakes and improve your chances of receiving a positive skills assessment.

## FAQs

### **1. What is the skills assessment fee for Australia?**

The fee varies by assessing authority and occupation. Most skills assessments cost anywhere from a few hundred to over a thousand Australian dollars.

### **2. Which documents are required for a skills assessment in Australia?**

The required documents usually include your passport, educational qualifications, employment reference letters, work experience evidence, and any professional registrations or licences relevant to your occupation.

### **3. For which Australian visas do I need a skills assessment?**

A skills assessment may be required for General Skilled Migration visas, Employer Sponsored visas, Skills in Demand visa streams, and certain [Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485) applications](https://www.growmore.one/news/australia-suddenly-doubles-485-temporary-graduate-visa-fee-to-aud-4600/).

### **4. What is a VETASSESS skills assessment?**

A VETASSESS skills assessment evaluates whether your qualifications and work experience meet Australian standards for many professional, general, and trade occupations.

### **5. How do I apply for a skills assessment in Australia?**

You can apply by selecting your nominated occupation, identifying the relevant assessing authority, submitting the required documents, and completing the assessment application process.
