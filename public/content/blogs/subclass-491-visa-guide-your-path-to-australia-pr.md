## What Is the Subclass 491 Visa?

The Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Subclass 491 visa is a points-tested temporary skilled visa that allows eligible professionals to live, work, and study in designated regional areas of Australia for up to five years.

**To receive an invitation for the 491 visa, you must be either:**

- Nominated by an Australian state or territory government, or
- Sponsored by an eligible family member living in a designated regional area of Australia.

## What Are the Benefits of a 491 Visa?

The Subclass 491 visa offers several advantages for skilled migrants, which means applicants can;

- Live, work, and study in regional Australia for up to 5 years.
- Receive 15 additional points toward the skilled migration points test through state nomination or eligible family sponsorship.
- Get Australian permanent residency through the Subclass 191 visa by meeting eligibility requirements.
- Include eligible family members, such as a partner and dependent children, in the application.
- Access strong employment opportunities across regional Australia in high-demand occupations.
- Travel freely in and out of Australia.

## Who Can Apply for Subclass 491 visa?

**To apply for Subclass 491 visa, you must meet the following 491 visa requirements:**

- You should be under 45 years old at the time of receiving the invitation.
- Have an occupation on the relevant skilled occupation list and be eligible for nomination.
- Obtain a positive skills assessment from the relevant assessing authority for your nominated occupation.
- Demonstrate Competent English through an approved English language test.
- Score at least 65 points on the skilled migration points test.
- Receive an invitation to apply after submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) through SkillSelect.
- Be nominated by an Australian state or territory government or sponsored by an eligible family member living in a designated regional area of Australia.

## How to Get Australia PR from Subclass 491 Visa?

The Subclass 491 visa provides a pathway to permanent residency through the [Subclass 191](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/difference-between-subclass-491-and-subclass-190/) Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional) visa.

**To become eligible for Australia PR, you must:**

- Hold a Subclass 491 visa for at least 3 years.
- Live, work, and study in a designated regional area while complying with your visa conditions.
- Meet the taxable income requirement for the required period, as specified by the Australian Government.
- Satisfy all health, character, and visa eligibility requirements at the time of applying.

## FAQs

### **1. Is the Australia 491 visa a direct PR visa?**

No. The Australia 491 visa is a 5-year provisional visa, not a direct PR visa. After meeting the eligibility requirements, you may apply for permanent residency through the Subclass 191 visa.

### **2. How many points are required for the Australia 491 visa?**

You need a minimum of 65 points to be eligible for the Subclass 491 Visa Australia. However, to receive an invitation, generally higher points are required.

### **3. Can offshore applicants apply for the Australia 491 visa?**

Yes. Both offshore and onshore applicants can apply for the Australia 491 visa by meeting all Australia 491 visa requirements, including skills assessment, English language proficiency, and nomination or eligible family sponsorship.

### **4. Do I need a skills assessment for the 491 visa?**

Yes. A positive skills assessment from the relevant assessing authority is a mandatory requirement for most applicants applying for the Subclass 491 Visa.

### **5. Which English tests are accepted for the Subclass 491 visa?**

Accepted English tests for the Subclass 491 Visa include IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, Cambridge C1 Advanced, OET, CELPIP General, LanguageCert Academic, and the Michigan English Test (MET).
