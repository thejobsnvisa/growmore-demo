## What Is the Section 48 Bar? 

Section 48 of the Migration Act 1958 can restrict certain people in Australia from making another substantive visa application after a relevant visa refusal or cancellation. In broad terms, Section 48 bar may become relevant where a person: 

* is physically present in Australia; 
* does not hold a substantive visa; and 
* has had a relevant visa application refused or a visa cancelled after their last entry into Australia. 

If the Section 48 bar applies, a person may not be able to apply for some visas despite being in Australia, unless the visa is a permitted exception. 

## Does a 407 Nomination Refusal Automatically Trigger Section 48? 

No. This is one of the most important points to understand. A nomination refusal by itself should not automatically be described as a Section 48 bar. 

The situation can change depending on what happens to the associated visa application and the person's visa status. For example, there is a significant difference between: 

Nomination refused, but visa application is still pending. 

and 

Nomination refused along with visa application refusal. 

There can also be a different situation where the applicant withdraws the visa application before a visa refusal is made. 

## What Happens After a 407 Nomination Refusal? 

If your nomination has been refused and your associated **Subclass 407 visa** application is still being processed, you may receive further correspondence from the Department. 

For some applicants, this can include a Section 57 Natural Justice letter. 

This gives the applicant an opportunity to respond to information or circumstances that may lead to the visa application being refused.  

## What Can You Do After a 407 Nomination Refusal? 

There are three potential pathways available for those whose 407 nominations have been refused but whose visa application has not yet been refused. The options depend on your particular circumstances and are as follows: 

**Pathway 1: Consider Withdrawing the Visa Application**

If your nomination has been refused but your associated visa application has not yet been refused, one option you may consider is withdrawing your visa application. 

Withdrawing the visa application before a refusal may help you avoid a Section 48 bar that could otherwise restrict your ability to make certain further substantive visa applications while in Australia. 

**Pathway 2: Seek Review Through the Administrative Review Tribunal** 

If you believe that your nomination refusal is genuinely wrong and want to challenge the government’s decision, you may have a right to seek review through the Administrative Review Tribunal (ART) based on the type of decision and your circumstances. 

This pathway may be relevant if you believe: 

* your training arrangement is genuine; 
* the nomination requirements were satisfied; 
* the Department misunderstood or overlooked your evidence; 
* relevant documents were not properly considered; or 
* the refusal was based on an incorrect assessment of your circumstances. 

However, seeking ART review does not automatically give you another opportunity to lodge any visa you want from Australia. If you don’t win ART then Section 48 restrictions may apply to you, which could prevent you from applying for certain substantive visas. 

Therefore, if you firmly believe that your nomination refusal is incorrect, and you have all genuine supporting documents to prove, then only you should go for ART review. 

**Pathway 3: Withdraw and Consider Another Eligible Visa Pathway** 

In some circumstances, you may decide not to continue with your Subclass 407 visa application and instead consider another visa pathway for which you are eligible. 

However, withdrawing your 407 applications does not automatically give you an unrestricted right to lodge another visa from Australia. The alternative visa must be one you are eligible for, and you must also be legally permitted to make that application while in Australia. 

After withdrawing your visa application, you may have 35 days to make arrangements to apply for another eligible visa but not subclass 407. 

**5 Mistakes to Avoid After a 407 Nomination Refusal** 

**1. Immediately lodging another visa** 
A refusal does not mean the next step is automatically another visa application. Check whether you are legally able to make that application first. 

**2. Assuming nomination refusal means visa refusal** 
No doubt they are related to each other, but they are separate decisions. Your associated visa application may still be pending after the nomination refusal. 

**3. Ignoring a Section 57 Natural Justice letter**
If you receive one, check the response requirements and deadline carefully. 

**4. Waiting until your visa situation becomes urgent** 
Do not wait until your current visa or bridging visa is close to ceasing before working out your next move. 

**5. Assuming Section 48 bar does not apply** 
Equally, do not assume that Section 48 definitely applies without assessing your circumstances. The timing and type of refusal, your current visa status and the visa you want to apply for can all matter.

## FAQs 

## 1. What happens if my Subclass 407 nomination is refused? 
Your visa application may still be pending, and you may receive further correspondence from the Department. Your options could include withdrawal, ART review or another eligible visa pathway. 

## 2. Does a 407 nomination refusal trigger Section 48? 
No, a nomination refusal alone does not automatically trigger Section 48 bar. Section 48 bar may become relevant if your associated visa application is subsequently refused, depending on your circumstances. 

## 3.Can I withdraw my 407 visa application after nomination refusal? 
Yes, you may be able to withdraw your visa application before it is refused. This may help avoid certain Section 48 consequences, but your next visa option should be assessed first. 

## 4.Can I appeal a 407 nomination refusal through the ART? 
You may be able to seek ART review if the decision is reviewable and you have review rights. Whether this is appropriate depends on your decision and individual circumstances. 

## 5.Can I apply for another visa after a 407 nomination refusal? 
You may be able to apply for another eligible visa if you are legally permitted to lodge it in Australia. Your Section 48 position and current visa or bridging visa status must also be considered.
