## How to Apply for a Subclass 189 Visa and Get Australia PR

If you want to apply for Australian permanent residency without employer sponsorship or state nomination, the Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189) may be a suitable pathway. This visa allows eligible skilled professionals to live and work permanently in Australia based on their skills, qualifications, work experience, and points score.

This guide explains how to apply for a Subclass 189 visa, including the eligibility criteria, application process, required documents, and key factors that can strengthen your chances of securing Australian permanent residency.

## What is the Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189)?

The Subclass 189 Visa is a permanent residence visa for skilled workers who are invited by the Australian Government based on their qualifications, work experience, English language ability, and points score.

One of the biggest advantages of this visa is that it does not require employer sponsorship, state nomination, or family sponsorship.

Once the visa is granted, you become an Australian permanent resident with the freedom to live, work, and study anywhere in Australia.

## Who Can Apply for 189 Visa Australia?

To apply for the Skilled Independent Visa Australia, applicants must meet the [189 Visa Eligibility](https://www.growmore.one/points-calculator/), **which is as follows:**

### **1. Hold an Eligible Visa (If Applying from Australia)**

**If you are applying while you are in Australia, you must hold one of the following visas at the time of application:**

- a substantive visa
- Bridging Visa A (Subclass 010)
- Bridging Visa B (Subclass 020)
- Bridging Visa C (Subclass 030)

If you currently hold, or your last substantive visa was a Subclass 491 or Subclass 494, you must have held that visa for at least three years before applying.

### **2. Receive an Invitation to Apply**

You cannot apply for the visa directly. The process begins by submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) through SkillSelect. Your EOI is ranked against other applicants based on your points score and profile. If your profile is competitive, you may receive an invitation then you can apply for this visa.

### **3. Obtain a Positive Skills Assessment**

Before you can apply, you must have a positive skills assessment for the occupation you have nominated.

Your skills assessment must be issued by the relevant Australian skills assessing authority for your occupation, be valid when you receive your invitation, and match the occupation which you have selected in your Expression of Interest (EOI).

However, if you are a medical or legal practitioner, your eligible Australian professional registration or admission may be required instead of a formal skills assessment.

### 4. **Be Under 45 Years of Age**

You must be under 45 years old when you receive your invitation to apply.

### 5. **Meet the Minimum Points Requirement**

The Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189) is a points-tested visa. You must score at least 65 points in the SkillSelect points test to be eligible for an invitation. However, the invitation score may be higher than the minimum requirement based on your nominated occupation.

### 6. **Demonstrate Competent English**

You must demonstrate Competent English by achieving the required score in an approved English language test that proves your English proficiency.

### 7. **Meet Health and Character Requirements**

You and any family members included in your application must satisfy Australia's health and character requirements.

### 8. **Apply through Eligible Occupation**

Your nominated occupation must appear on the relevant skilled occupation list for the Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189) Point-Tested Stream.

## How to Apply for 189 Visa

Many applicants search for the Subclass 189 visa application process because they want to understand the complete process before starting. Here are the major steps involved:

### **Step 1: Check Your Eligibility**

Meet all the eligibility requirements mentioned above, including age, occupation, qualification, work experience, English ability, and points score.

### **Step 2: Complete Your Skills Assessment**

Apply to the relevant skills assessing authority for your nominated occupation and obtain a positive skills assessment before submitting your Expression of Interest (EOI). Provide clear evidence of your qualifications and employment history to improve your chances of a successful outcome.

### **Step 3: Submit Expression of Interest (EOI)**

Create and submit your EOI through SkillSelect. At this stage, you are not applying for the visa, but you are just registering your interest in applying.

### **Step 4: Receive Invitation**

If your EOI is competitive and your points score meets the invitation threshold for your nominated occupation, you may receive an invitation to apply for the Subclass 189 visa.

## Benefits of the Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189)

One of the biggest advantages of the Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189) is the flexibility it offers after you become an Australian permanent resident.

**With this visa, you can:**

- Live, work, and study anywhere in Australia
- Stay in Australia permanently
- Include eligible family members in your application
