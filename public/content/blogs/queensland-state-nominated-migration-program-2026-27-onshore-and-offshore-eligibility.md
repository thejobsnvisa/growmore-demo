Queensland is set to open its 2026–27 State Nominated Migration Program on 6 November 2026. So, if you are planning to apply for Queensland state nomination, it is important to understand the eligibility requirements before submitting your Registration of Interest (ROI). 

The program offers five nomination pathways, each with its own eligibility criteria based on factors such as your occupation, employment, location, qualifications and business ownership. Every applicant must meet the general visa eligibility requirements set by the Australian Government, as well as Queensland’s specific criteria for their chosen pathway. 

Let’s take a closer look at the eligibility requirements for all five pathways. 

## Queensland Skilled Migration Pathways and Eligibility Criteria 

## 1.Skilled Workers Living Outside Australia (Offshore)

This pathway is for skilled professionals currently living outside Australia who want to obtain Queensland nomination. Depending on their nominated occupation, eligible applicants may be considered for the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) or Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491). 

## Eligibility criteria: 

* **Occupation:** You must have a valid skills assessment for an occupation on the Queensland Offshore Skilled Occupation List. 

* **Points:** You must score at least 65 points under the Department of Home Affairs' skilled migration points test. 

* **English:** You must have at least Competent English as defined by the Department of Home Affairs. 

* **Work experience:** You must have at least one year of skilled employment experience within the last three years before submitting your ROI. Your employment must: 

* Be in your nominated occupation or a closely related occupation with the same first three digits of the ANZSCO code. 
* Have been gained after completing your qualification. 
* Involve at least 20 hours of work per week. 

Casual employment is accepted, and multiple eligible jobs can be combined to meet the requirement. 

* **Visa subclass:** Your eligible visa subclass depends on your occupation. Some occupations are available only for subclass 491. You cannot obtain subclass 190 nomination if you have been nominated for, applied for or hold a subclass 491. 

* **Commitment to living in Queensland:** You must commit to living and working in: 

* Regional Queensland for at least three years after arriving in Australia on a subclass 491 visa; or 
* Queensland for at least two years after arriving in Australia on a subclass 190 visa. 

## 2.Skilled Workers Living in Queensland (Onshore) 

This pathway is for skilled workers already living and working in Queensland for a Queensland-based employer. Applicants must meet specific residence and employment periods depending on whether they are seeking subclass 190 or subclass 491 nomination. 

## Eligibility criteria: 

* **Occupation:** You must have a valid skills assessment for an occupation on the Queensland Onshore Skilled Occupation List. Chefs and Cooks working in takeaway or fast-food businesses are not eligible under this pathway. 

* **Points:** You must score at least 65 points under the Department of Home Affairs' skilled migration points test. 

* **English:** You must have at least Competent English. 

* **Queensland residence and employment:** You must be currently living and working in Queensland. In addition: 

* Subclass 491: You must have lived and worked in Regional Queensland for at least six out of the last nine months immediately before submitting your ROI. 

* Subclass 190: You must have lived and worked in Queensland for at least nine out of the last 12 months immediately before submitting your ROI. 

You must continue to meet the relevant requirements when submitting your ROI, receiving an invitation and obtaining nomination. 

* **Work experience:** Your employment must have been gained after completing your qualification, involve at least 20 paid hours per week, and be in your nominated occupation or a closely related occupation with the same first three ANZSCO digits. Casual employment is accepted, and you can combine multiple eligible job experiences. 

* **Visa subclass:** Your occupation must be eligible for the relevant visa subclass on the Queensland Onshore Skilled Occupation List. Some occupations are eligible only for subclass 491. Previous subclass 491 or subclass 489 nomination, applications or visa holdings can prevent subclass 190 nomination. 

* **Queensland commitment:** You must commit to living and working in Regional Queensland for at least three years after a subclass 491 grant, or Queensland for at least two years after a subclass 190 grant. 

## 3.Building and Construction Workers in Queensland 

This pathway is designed for eligible building and construction professionals who are currently living and working in Queensland. Unlike the general onshore skilled worker pathway, applicants need only three continuous months of Queensland residence and employment immediately before submitting their ROI. 

## Eligibility criteria: 

* **Occupation:** You must have a valid skills assessment for an occupation on the Queensland Onshore Skilled Occupation List that is also included in the Building and Construction Workers pathway. 

* **Points:** You must score at least 65 points under the Department of Home Affairs' skilled migration points test. 

* **English:** You must have at least Competent English. 

* **Queensland residence and employment:** You must currently live and work for a Queensland-based employer and have completed at least three continuous months of living and working in Queensland immediately before submitting your ROI. 

* **Work experience:** Your employment must have been gained after completing your qualification, involve at least 20 paid hours per week and be in your nominated occupation or a closely related occupation with the same first three ANZSCO digits. 

Casual employment is accepted, and you can combine the experience of multiple eligible jobs. 

* **Visa subclass:** Your occupation must be eligible for the relevant subclass under the Queensland Onshore Skilled Occupation List. Queensland may nominate eligible construction workers for subclass 190, while subclass 491 may be available where the occupation or applicant is not eligible for subclass 190. 

* **Queensland commitment:** You must commit to living and working in Queensland for at least two years after a subclass 190 grant, or Regional Queensland for at least three years after a subclass 491 grant. 

## 4.Queensland University Graduates 

This pathway is for eligible international graduates who have completed a higher education qualification at a Queensland university or eligible tertiary institution and are living and working in Queensland. An important feature is that the completed Queensland qualification does not need to be related to the applicant's nominated occupation. 

## Eligibility criteria: 

* **Occupation:** You must have a valid skills assessment for an occupation on the Queensland Onshore Skilled Occupation List. Chefs and Cooks working in takeaway or fast-food businesses are not eligible. 

* **Points:** You must score at least 65 points under the Department of Home Affairs' skilled migration points test. 

* **English:** You must have at least Competent English. 

* **Queensland qualification:** You must: 

* Hold a Bachelor's degree, Master's degree or PhD from a Queensland university or eligible Queensland tertiary institution. 
* Meet the Australian Study Requirement. 
* Have completed 100% of the qualification in Queensland. 
* Have graduated after 1 July 2022. 

Your Queensland qualification does not need to be related to your nominated occupation. 

* **Queensland residence and employment:** 

* Subclass 491: You must have lived and worked in Regional Queensland for at least six out of the last nine months immediately before submitting your ROI. 

* Subclass 190: You must have lived and worked in Queensland for at least nine out of the last 12 months immediately before submitting your ROI. 

You must continue to meet the applicable requirement when submitting your ROI, receiving an invitation and obtaining nomination. 

* **Work experience:** Your employment must have been gained after completing your qualification and involve at least 20 paid hours per week. It can be: 

* In your nominated occupation. 
* In a closely related occupation with the same first three ANZSCO digits. 
* In any Skill Level 1 occupation. 

Important: Although employment in any Skill Level 1 occupation can meet the pathway's work experience requirement, you can only claim skilled employment points in your EOI for work in your nominated occupation or a closely related occupation. 

* **Visa subclass:** Your occupation must be eligible for the relevant visa subclass under the Queensland Onshore Skilled Occupation List. Some occupations are eligible only for subclass 491. Previous subclass 491 or subclass 489 nomination, applications or visa holdings can affect subclass 190 eligibility. 

* **Queensland commitment:** You must commit to living and working in Queensland for at least two years after a subclass 190 grant, or Regional Queensland for at least three years after a subclass 491 grant. 

## 5.Small Business Owners in Regional Queensland (Subclass 491 Only)

This pathway is for eligible migrants who currently live and operate a qualifying small business in regional Queensland. Unlike the other four pathways, it is exclusively for subclass 491 nomination. The business must have been started before 19 September 2025 and must satisfy the applicable establishment, ownership, profitability and turnover requirements. 

## Eligibility criteria: 

* **Occupation:** You must have an occupation listed in Legislative Instrument LIN 19/051. It does not need to be on the Queensland Onshore Skilled Occupation List. 

* **Regional residence:** You must provide evidence that you live in an eligible regional Queensland postcode that includes 4019–4022, 4025, 4037, 4074, 4076–4078, 4124–4125, 4133, 4183–4184, 4207–4275, 4280–4287, 4300–4301, 4303–4498, 4500–4512, 4514–4519, 4521, 4550–4575, 4580–4895. 

* **Business location:** Your business must be located and operating in an eligible regional Queensland postcode. 

* **Business ownership:** You must demonstrate that you have owned 100% of the business since it started, or that it is jointly owned with your spouse or de facto partner. Joint ownership with another business partner is not accepted. 

* **Business operation and turnover:** You must provide evidence that your business: 
* Has operated profitably for at least two years, unless you qualify for the specified exemption. 
* Has achieved a minimum turnover of $200,000 in the 12 months immediately before submitting your ROI.
