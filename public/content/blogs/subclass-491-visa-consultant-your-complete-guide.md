**What Is the Subclass 491 Visa?**
----------------------------------

The Subclass 491 visa is a points-tested, provisional visa that allows skilled migrants and their families to live, work, and study in a designated regional area of Australia for up to five years. It replaced the former Subclass 489 visa in November 2019 and sits within Australia’s skilled migration framework.

The visa is available through two nomination pathways:

### 1\. State or Territory Government Nomination

An Australian state or territory government nominates applicants. Each state and territory runs its own nomination program with unique occupation lists, eligibility criteria, and processing timelines. Understanding which state is your best fit requires detailed research and expert knowledge.

### 2\. Eligible Relative Sponsorship

If you have an eligible relative who is an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or eligible New Zealand citizen living in a designated regional area, they may be able to sponsor you. This pathway has its own set of requirements and is less commonly used, but can be highly effective in the right circumstances.

Once granted, the Subclass 491 visa is a stepping stone to permanent residency through the [Subclass 191 Permanent Residence (Regional) visa](https://www.growmore.one/list-of-eligible-occupations-and-qualifications/) – provided you meet the income and residency requirements after three years.

**Key Eligibility Requirements at a Glance**
--------------------------------------------

Before diving into how a consultant can help, it helps to understand the core eligibility requirements for the Subclass 491 visa. You generally need to:

*   Be invited to apply through an Expression of Interest (EOI) in SkillSelect
*   Have a nominated occupation on the relevant skilled occupation list (MLTSSL or STSOL, depending on state requirements)
*   Be under 45 years of age at the time of invitation
*   Score at least 65 points on the points test — though competitive scores are often much higher
*   Have a successful skills assessment from the relevant assessing authority
*   Meet the English language proficiency requirement (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, or equivalent)
*   Receive a nomination from a state/territory government or sponsorship from an eligible relative
*   Meet health and character requirements

Every one of these steps involves specific documentation, deadlines, and decision points. An experienced Subclass 491 visa consultant guides you through each of them with precision.

**The Points Test And Why It Matters More Than You Think**
----------------------------------------------------------

The Subclass 491 visa is points-tested. While 65 points is the minimum threshold to submit an Expression of Interest, the actual invitation scores in most SkillSelect rounds are significantly higher, often 80, 85, or more, depending on the occupation and state.

**Points are awarded across several categories:**

*   Age (maximum points for applicants aged 25–32)
*   English language ability (competent, proficient, or superior)
*   Skilled employment experience (in Australia and overseas)
*   Educational qualifications
*   Australian study requirement
*   Partner skills and qualifications
*   State or territory nomination (an additional 15 points for Subclass 491)
*   Eligible relative sponsorship in a regional area (5 points)
*   Credentialled community language (5 points)
*   Professional year in Australia

Maximising your points score is not just about what you have — it is about how you present what you have. A Subclass 491 visa consultant reviews every point-scoring category, identifies opportunities you may have missed, and ensures your EOI reflects your true competitive position.
