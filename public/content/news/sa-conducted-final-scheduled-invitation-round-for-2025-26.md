South Australia has officially conducted its final scheduled invitation round for the 2025–26 skilled migration program and has now entered into its closing stage. For many skilled migration applicants waiting for state nomination opportunities in WA, this round may have been one of the last major chances before the new program year begins.

The latest round, held on 21 May 2026, issued 238 invitations across Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 visa pathways. However, the state has indicated that additional invitation activity may still occur to utilise any remaining nomination places.

## Key Highlights From the Final Scheduled Round 

Out of the total 2,250 nomination places South Australia was granted for the 2025–26 migration program year, the state has already issued 2,853 invitations this year. This means the majority of SA’s nomination quota has already been utilised this program year.

Healthcare occupations again received the strongest priority in this round, with Health Professionals securing 66 invitations, including 59 invitations under the [Subclass 190](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/difference-between-subclass-491-and-subclass-190/) pathway alone.

Engineering, construction, and trade occupations also continued to receive strong attention that reflects ongoing workforce shortages across South Australia.

The data also highlights SA’s continued preference for regional migration pathways in selected occupations, particularly for ICT Professionals. During the current program year, ICT occupations received only 7 invitations under Subclass 190 compared to 236 invitations under the Subclass 491 pathway.

Although this was the final scheduled invitation round, South Australia has confirmed that additional invitation activity may still occur before the financial year ends if unused nomination places remain available.
