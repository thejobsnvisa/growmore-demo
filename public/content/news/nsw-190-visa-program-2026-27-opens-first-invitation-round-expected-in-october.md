Good news for skilled migrants planning to apply for Australian permanent residency through NSW! New South Wales (NSW) has announced the opening of its 2026–27 Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) nomination program, giving eligible skilled professionals an opportunity to pursue state-nominated permanent residency in Australia. 

The latest update also brings important information for applicants considering NSW as their preferred destination, including the updated Skills List and the current status of skilled visa nomination pathways. Here is what you need to know. 

## NSW Subclass 190 Nomination Program Opens for 2026–27 

NSW has announced that its nomination program for the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) is open for the 2026–27 program year. 

For skilled migrants targeting NSW, the program opening means now is the time to review their eligibility and prepare for the upcoming invitation round. Before submitting or updating an Expression of Interest (EOI) through SkillSelect, applicants should review the relevant visa requirements and NSW nomination criteria. 

## First NSW Subclass 190 Invitation Round Expected in October 

NSW expects to conduct its first subclass 190 invitation round for 2026–27 in the week commencing 19 October 2026. 

Applicants hoping to receive an invitation should ensure their SkillSelect EOI is accurate and up to date. They should also be able to provide valid evidence to support every claim made in their EOI if invited to apply. 

Key points to review include: 

* **Occupation eligibility:** Confirm that your nominated occupation is eligible for the subclass 190 visa and falls within an ANZSCO unit group identified on the NSW Skills List. 

* **Points score:** Check that the points claimed in your EOI are accurate and supported by the relevant evidence. 

* **Qualifications and skills assessment:** Ensure your qualifications and skills assessment meet the applicable requirements. 

* **English proficiency and work experience:** Verify that any claims relating to English-language ability or skilled employment can be substantiated. 

## Updated NSW Skills List for 2026–27 

NSW has released its updated Skills List for the 2026–27 program year. The list aligns with critical skills priorities identified in the NSW Government's State Migration Plan 2024–28 and focuses on occupations associated with significant demand. 

It identifies eligible occupations at the Australian and New Zealand Standard Classification of Occupations (ANZSCO) unit group level.  

Read an Official Updated NSW Skills List here: https://www.nsw.gov.au/visas-and-migration/skilled-visas/nsw-skills-lists  

NSW has clarified that occupations outside the identified unit groups will not be considered for an invitation for NSW subclass 190 nomination. However, inclusion on the list does not guarantee selection. Applicants must still meet the applicable visa and nomination requirements. 

## NSW Subclass 491 Nomination Pathways Remain Closed 

While the subclass 190 program has opened, NSW subclass 491 nomination applications under all pathways remain closed to new applicants. 

For applicants specifically targeting regional NSW nomination, the current closure means they will need to wait for further updates. 

## NSW Health Deployment Program for Nurses and Allied Health Professionals 

The NSW update also highlights the NSW Health Deployment Program, which offers nurses, midwives and allied health professionals opportunities to work across NSW for periods ranging from 2 to 13 weeks. 

The program includes paid travel, accommodation and additional financial support, with placements intended to support regional and rural communities. Interested health professionals should review the program's eligibility and participation requirements, noting that this is a separate initiative from skilled visa nomination.
