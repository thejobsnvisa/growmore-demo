Tasmania continues to invite skilled applicants through its 2026–27 state nomination program under the Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 visa pathways. The latest invitation round, held on **10 September 2026**, saw Tasmania issue **66 invitations** across both visa subclasses.

These results provide applicants with a useful snapshot of the state’s current nomination activity. However, invitation scores can vary between rounds and should not be treated as guaranteed thresholds for future invitations.

Here is the full update on this invitation round, along with the related numbers after the latest round.

In the latest Tasmania invitation round, a total of **66 invitations** were issued across the Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 visa pathways.

The latest round shows a clear difference in the level of competition between Tasmania’s two skilled visa pathways. Subclass 190 recorded a substantially higher lowest invited score, while Subclass 491 had a much lower entry point in this round.

This may make the regional Subclass 491 pathway comparatively accessible for applicants who are seeking a state-nominated regional option.

However, these figures represent the lowest scores invited in this particular round and should not be treated as a guaranteed score requirement for future Tasmania invitations.

## Tasmania Continues to Verify Priority Attributes

Tasmania verifies the priority attributes claimed in an ROI before approving a nomination. If any claimed attribute cannot be verified and the applicant’s score falls below the applicable lowest score threshold, the application may be declined.

Applicants should therefore ensure that all their claimed priority attributes are accurate and supported by evidence.

An application may result in refusal if an applicant has intentionally claimed attributes just to receive an invitation.

For **Gold Pass** holders, failure to meet the required Gold priority attribute can result in the application being declined regardless of the total points score.

## Tasmania’s Current Processing Queue

The latest update also provides information about applications waiting to be assessed.

As of the latest update, the oldest nomination application lodged but not yet allocated to a case officer was lodged on **9 September 2026**.

This indicates that the state is continuing to process recently lodged nomination applications.

## What These Invitation Results Mean

The latest round indicates a shift towards **Green priority invitations for Subclass 190**, with Tasmania selecting more Green applicants than in the previous round.

At the same time, the lower invited score for **Subclass 491** suggests comparatively broader opportunities for regional applicants in this round.

However, invitation outcomes can vary from one round to another based on the number and profile of ROIs selected.

For applicants currently holding an ROI, the key consideration is not simply the lowest score reported in one invitation round. Applicants should also ensure that their points, priority attributes and supporting evidence accurately reflect their circumstances.
