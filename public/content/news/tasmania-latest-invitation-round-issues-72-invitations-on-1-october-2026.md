Tasmania continues to attract the attention of skilled migrants by regularly releasing updates on its state nomination invitation rounds. As October 2026 begins, the state has released its latest invitation round and invited applicants for Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional visa (subclass 491).

A total of **72 invitations** were issued on **1 October 2026**, which provides applicants with an opportunity to secure state nomination and progress towards Australia PR.

Here is the breakdown of the latest invitation round, including invitation scores, ROI numbers, available nomination places and other important figures for skilled migrants considering Tasmania.

## Tasmania Subclass 190 and 491 Invitation Results

The latest round shows that Tasmania continues to invite candidates under both skilled migration pathways, with different invitation numbers and score outcomes for each visa subclass.

For **Subclass 190**, Tasmania issued **38 invitations**, including **7 Gold** and **31 Green** invitations. For **Subclass 491**, the state issued **34 invitations**, all recorded as further invitations.

| Visa Subclass | Invitations Issued | Invitation Type | Lowest Invited Score |

The lowest invited scores just provide an outline of this particular round. However, applicants should not treat these figures as guaranteed thresholds for future invitations, as Tasmania's selection process also considers its nomination criteria and the priority attributes claimed in each ROI.

## Changes in Tasmania's Nomination Position

Compared with the **24 September 2026** update, Tasmania's latest figures show the following changes:

- **ROIs on hand:** Subclass 190 decreased from 905 to 900, while Subclass 491 decreased from 593 to 591.
- **Applications awaiting decisions:** Subclass 190 increased from 41 to 62, while Subclass 491 increased from 37 to 51.
- **Invitations awaiting application lodgement:** Subclass 190 increased from 76 to 79, while Subclass 491 increased from 63 to 67.

The rolling three-month average of the lowest scores resulting in an invitation also decreased for both visa subclasses. For Subclass 190, it fell from **478 to 457 points**, while the Subclass 491 figure decreased from **81 to 79 points**.

Tasmania also reported that the oldest nomination application lodged but not yet allocated to a case officer was dated **21 September 2026**, compared with **17 September 2026** in the previous update.

## Important Checks Before Proceeding with Tasmania Nomination

Applicants who have already submitted a Tasmania ROI should make sure their details are correct and that they have all required evidence to support the information provided.

If their employment, occupation, English proficiency or skills assessment has changed since submitting the ROI, they should review their details and check whether the changes affect their eligibility.

Tasmania also verifies the priority attributes claimed by applicants. If an attribute cannot be verified and the applicant's score falls below the required threshold, their nomination application will be declined. Applicants who deliberately claim attributes to increase their scores may also face rejection.

Gold pass holders must meet the requirements for their claimed Gold priority attribute. If they fail to meet these requirements, their nomination application will be declined, regardless of their overall score.
