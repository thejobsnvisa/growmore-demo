## ACT Nomination Program Status as of 7 May 2026

* Out of the total 1600 ACT nominations, approvals have now reached 1,288 for the 2025–26 program year.  
* This includes 615 subclass 190 approvals and 673 subclass 491 approvals.  
* A total of 195 applications have been refused so far this financial year.  
* Refusals included 91 subclass 190 applications and 104 subclass 491 applications.  
* Canberra residents continued to secure the majority of ACT nominations for Australia PR, receiving 1,131 approvals (87.8%), while overseas applicants accounted for 157 approvals (12.2%).  

## Key Highlights from the Latest ACT Matrix Round

* Hospitality occupations such as Chefs, Cooks, and Cafe and Restaurant Managers recorded strong score requirements, particularly for Canberra residents.  

* Healthcare and trade occupations continued to receive invitations across both ACT residents and offshore applicants, showing continued demand across these sectors.  

* Accounting occupations continued to face very high competition, with Accountants requiring up to 135 points for subclass 190 invitations.  

* ICT occupations again remained among the most competitive categories, with several occupations requiring 120 to 130+ Matrix scores that highlights how competitive Australia PR pathways have become for ICT professionals.
