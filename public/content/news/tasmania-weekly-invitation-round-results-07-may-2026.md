Tasmania has kicked off May 2026 with its first weekly skilled migration invitation round of the month under the nomination program. The results show ongoing invitations, with a continued focus on selective and controlled nomination management.

In the latest round, 62 invitations were issued in total across Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 streams. At the same time, a large number of ROIs remain on hand, which indicates competition for Tasmania nomination continues to remain high.
