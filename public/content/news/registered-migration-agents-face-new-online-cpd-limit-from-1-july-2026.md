If you are a Registered Migration Agent (RMA) in Australia, there is an important compliance update you should be aware of.

From 1 July 2026, a new operational rule has changed how online Continuing Professional Development (CPD) can be completed. While the total annual CPD requirement remains unchanged, RMAs can now earn a maximum of 6 online CPD points within any continuous 24-hour period.

This change is designed to encourage regular learning rather than allowing agents to complete all their online CPD at once.

## What Are CPD Points?

Continuing Professional Development (CPD) points measure the professional learning activities completed by Registered Migration Agents each year. It helps RMAs stay up to date with changes to Australian migration law, visa policies, legislative amendments, and professional standards.

RMAs must complete the required number of CPD points each year through approved learning activities such as webinars, workshops, conferences, and online private study to keep their registration active.

## What Does the New Rule Mean?

The new 6-point limit applies to all online CPD activities combined, including:

**1. Category A: Online interactive workshop-based learning, such as live webinars.**

**2. Category B: Online private study or distance learning.**

In practical terms, an RMA can no longer complete their entire annual online CPD requirement in a single day. For example, completing a combination of webinars and online private study worth 10 CPD points within one continuous 24-hour period would no longer comply with the new requirement.

## Things RMAs Should Check Before Booking Online CPD

To remain compliant, [Registered Migration Agents](https://www.growmore.one/) should review the following before registering for any online CPD activities:

**1. How many CPD points they have already completed.**

**2. Whether they have fulfilled the mandatory Ethics and Code of Conduct requirement.**

**3. Whether additional Category A CPD points are still required.**

**4.  Whether their planned online learning schedule complies with the new limit of 6 online CPD points within any continuous 24-hour period.**

A little planning throughout the CPD year can help avoid last-minute scheduling issues and ensure all professional obligations are met.
