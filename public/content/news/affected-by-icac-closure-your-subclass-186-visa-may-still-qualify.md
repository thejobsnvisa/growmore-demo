The temporary closure of the Indian Consular Application Centres (ICACs) in Australia from 1 July 2026 created uncertainty for many Indian nationals. While the disruption affected applicants across different Australian visa pathways, skilled workers planning to apply for the Subclass 186 Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS) visa were particularly concerned about meeting important visa requirements and application deadlines.

But the relieving part is that the Australian Department of Home Affairs has confirmed that certain affected Subclass 186 visa applicants may still have options to complete their visa application. The Department will consider providing flexibility on a case-by-case basis for applicants who were directly impacted by the disruption between 1 July and 23 July 2026.

## Why Has the Department Announced This Flexibility?

The Department recognises that the temporary disruption of ICAC services was beyond the control of affected applicants. During this period, some individuals were unable to renew their Indian passports, which may have prevented them from completing essential steps required for their Subclass 186 visa application.

Without a valid passport, applicants may have been unable to sit an English language test, lodge their visa application online, or meet important visa lodgement deadlines. In some cases, this could have affected their eligibility for permanent residency through the employer-sponsored visa pathway.

## Who Could Benefit From This Update?

This flexibility may be particularly relevant for applicants applying for the Subclass 186 visa.

For example, if an applicant had to apply for a Subclass 186 TRT visa before a deadline after holding a Subclass 482 visa for at least two of the previous three years but missed it because they couldn't renew their passport during the ICAC disruption, the Department may consider their individual circumstances.

## What Evidence Will Applicants Need to Provide?

Applicants seeking consideration should provide all relevant details and supporting evidence demonstrating that they:

- Were unable to renew their passport.
- Were unable to sit an English language test because they did not hold a current passport.
- Were unable to lodge their visa application online.
- Contacted eServices to request manual lodgement of their application.

Providing clear and sufficient supporting documentation will be important when requesting the Department's consideration.
