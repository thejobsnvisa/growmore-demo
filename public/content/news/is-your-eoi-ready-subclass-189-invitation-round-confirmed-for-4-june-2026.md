# Is Your EOI Ready? Subclass 189 Invitation Round Confirmed for 4 June 2026

If you have been waiting for the next Subclass 189 invitation round, the wait is finally over.

Australia has officially confirmed that the next Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189) invitation round will be conducted on 4 June 2026. This announcement is important for thousands of skilled migration applicants planning to apply under Australia’s [General Skilled Migration (GSM) program](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/gsm-general-skilled-migration), both from inside and outside Australia.

Along with this announcement, the government has also issued an important reminder to all applicants to ensure their Expression of Interest (EOI) is fully updated and contains accurate information.

## What Makes the Subclass 189 Visa So Important

The Subclass 189 visa remains one of the most attractive [Australia PR pathways](https://www.growmore.one/) among applicants because it offers direct permanent residency without requiring state nomination, employer sponsorship, or regional sponsorship.

This visa is entirely based on the applicant’s own ranking within the SkillSelect system. This means every invitation round becomes highly competitive, especially for occupations with large numbers of active EOIs.

It also offers the flexibility to live and work anywhere in Australia after receiving an invitation, without being restricted to a specific region or tied to a particular employer, like some other visa pathways.

## Important EOI Checks Ahead of the Invitation Round

A large number of applicants assume that once their EOI is submitted, nothing further needs to be reviewed. But the department has specifically reminded applicants to make sure all EOI information is accurate because invitations are issued based on the claims made inside SkillSelect at the time of invitation. If the claimed points cannot later be verified during visa processing, the application can face refusal.

**This is why applicants should immediately review:**

* English test validity  
* Skills assessment expiry  
* Skilled employment calculations  
* Qualification claims  
* Partner points claims  
* Overseas and Australian work experience  
* Passport details  
* Residency status information  
* Points calculation accuracy  

Because factors like points score, occupation demand, number of competing EOIs, annual invitation allocations, and occupation ceilings all influence invitation outcomes. This means two applicants with the same points can receive completely different results because of the above-mentioned factors.

## What Applicants Should Do Before 4 June 2026

With the invitation round now officially confirmed, this is the time for applicants to take practical action instead of waiting passively. 

**Recommended steps include:**

* Review your EOI carefully  
* Check expiry dates of your English tests or skills assessments  
* Recalculate your skilled employment score  
* Consider points improvement options
