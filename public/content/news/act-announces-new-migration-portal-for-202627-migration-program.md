Before the ACT begins its invitation rounds for the 2026–27 financial year, the territory has given skilled migrants an early look at what's ahead. The ACT Government has announced the launch of its new ACT Migration Application Portal, which will go live on 30 July and serve as the platform for managing ACT nomination applications.

The introduction of the new portal marks an important update for prospective applicants and signals the beginning of preparations for the new program year.

So, if you're planning to seek ACT nomination, now is the right time to familiarise yourself with the new portal and prepare for the upcoming nomination process.

## **New ACT Migration Application Portal Goes Live on 30 July**

The ACT Government has confirmed that the new ACT Migration Application Portal will officially go live around midday on 30 July. The portal will become the primary platform for managing ACT nomination applications and introduces several improvements to make the process more secure, transparent, and user-friendly for both applicants and migration agents.

While the portal is launching before the start of the 2026–27 invitation rounds, applicants should use this time to ensure that they are prepared when nomination opportunities become available.

## **ACT Migration Portal: New Account Creation Guide**

## **ACT Nomination Allocations for 2026–27 Yet to Be Confirmed**

The ACT Government has also confirmed that discussions with the Australian Government regarding nomination allocations for the 2026–27 financial year are still ongoing.

At this stage, the total number of nomination places available for ACT has not been announced. Once allocations are finalised, the ACT Government will publish further details, including a tentative invitation round schedule for the financial year.

## **ACT Occupation List Review Is Underway**

Another important update is that the ACT Nominated Migration Program occupation list is currently under review.

The ACT Government expects to release the updated occupation list in the coming weeks. While some changes may occur, it has indicated that the list is expected to remain broadly similar and will continue to reflect the ACT's labour market needs.
