If you are targeting an Australian State or Territory nomination under the Subclass 190 or Subclass 491 visa, or you've already lodged an Expression of Interest (EOI) and are waiting for an invitation, now is an important time to assess where you stand as the 2025–26 state nomination program year draws to a close. 

Understanding these nomination trends can help you assess your current position and plan your next steps more effectively. The figures released by the Department of Home Affairs cover the period from 1 July 2025 to 31 May 2026 and show the number of EOIs that received state or territory nominations under the [Subclass 190](https://www.growmore.one/news/is-your-eoi-ready-subclass-189-invitation-round-confirmed-for-4-june-2026/) and [Subclass 491 visa programs](https://www.growmore.one/news/is-your-eoi-ready-subclass-189-invitation-round-confirmed-for-4-june-2026/). 

These statistics provide a useful overview of nomination activity across Australia during the 2025–26 program year and offer valuable insights before the new nomination cycle begins.

## State Nomination Activity for the Subclass 190 Visa

The table below shows the number of EOIs that received a Subclass 190 nomination between **1 July 2025 and 31 May 2026**.

| **State/Territory** | **Nominations** |
|-----------------|------------:|
| Victoria | 2,649 |
| New South Wales | 2,082 |
| Western Australia | 1,742 |
| Queensland | 1,602 |
| South Australia | 1,164 |
| Tasmania | 1,152 |
| Northern Territory | 755 |
| ACT (Australian Capital Territory) | 715 |
| **Total** | **11,861** |

Victoria has issued the highest number of Subclass 190 nominations during the reporting period, followed by New South Wales and Western Australia, which reflects strong nomination activity across these states.

## State Nomination Activity for the Subclass 491 Visa

**For applicants pursuing the regional route via state and territory nomination, here's where things stand:**

| **State/Territory** | **Nominations** |
|-----------------|------------:|
| New South Wales | 1,424 |
| Western Australia | 1,400 |
| South Australia | 796 |
| ACT (Australian Capital Territory) | 745 |
| Queensland | 694 |
| Victoria | 694 |
| Northern Territory | 532 |
| Tasmania | 501 |
| **Total** | **6,786** |

New South Wales and Western Australia have recorded the highest number of Subclass 491 nominations during the reporting period, which highlights the importance of skilled workers in regional Australia. However, higher numbers just indicate active state participation; it doesn’t mean invitations are easier to secure. 

For that, applicants still need to meet the state's eligibility requirements, remain competitive in the invitation process, and satisfy all visa criteria.

## What This Means If You're Targeting a State or Territory Nomination

**1. Nomination activity varies by state, so focus on the state that best matches your occupation and profile.**

**2. The Subclass 491 visa continues to offer strong opportunities for applicants considering regional Australia.**

**3. If you are eligible, consider applying to more than one state or territory for Australia PR where nomination policies allow.**

**4. Also, keep your EOI up to date so you're ready when your preferred state issues invitations.**

As state nomination priorities continue to evolve, choosing the right pathway is as important as meeting the eligibility requirements.
