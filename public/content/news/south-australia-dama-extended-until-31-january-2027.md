The timeframe has been extended for South Australia’s two DAMAs, giving employers and skilled workers more time to benefit from the existing arrangements.

Both Designated Area Migration Agreements (DAMAs) of South Australia have been extended for a further four months. As a result, the current arrangements will continue until **31 January 2027**.

The extension comes while negotiations continue on a new five-year statewide DAMA for South Australia.

## Current South Australia DAMA Terms Remain Unchanged

The South Australian Government has confirmed that the terms and conditions of both existing DAMAs remain unchanged following the extension.

The current terms were last varied in June 2026.

This means the four-month extension does not introduce new DAMA occupations, concessions or other changes to the existing arrangements. Employers and skilled workers should continue to assess their eligibility against the current DAMA requirements.

You can find more information about [South Australia’s Designated Area Migration Agreements (DAMAs) here](https://migration.sa.gov.au/employer-hub/dama).

## New Five-Year South Australia DAMA Under Negotiation

South Australia is continuing negotiations on a proposed new five-year statewide DAMA.

The Government has not yet released the final details of the new agreement. Further announcements about the new five-year DAMA and the transition arrangements will be made once negotiations have concluded.

Until those details are announced, the existing DAMA arrangements continue to apply under the extended period.

## What Does the DAMA Extension Mean for Employers and Skilled Workers?

The extension allows eligible South Australian employers to continue accessing overseas workers under the current DAMA occupation lists and available concessions.

For skilled workers, it provides continued access to employer-sponsored visa pathways under the DAMA, allowing them to pursue work opportunities in Australia and, where eligible, work towards Australian permanent residency (PR).

However, a DAMA does not grant a visa or guarantee nomination. The employer, position and applicant must still meet the requirements of the relevant DAMA and visa subclass.

## Need Help to Apply for DAMA in South Australia?

Understanding whether you qualify under a South Australia DAMA requires more than checking whether your occupation appears on the relevant list.

The employer, position, salary, experience, concessions and visa requirements may all need to be assessed.

If you are an employer looking to sponsor an overseas worker or a skilled worker considering a South Australia DAMA pathway, speak with a Registered Migration Agent in Australia to understand your options and the requirements that apply to your circumstances.
