Planning to lodge a Great South Coast DAMA nomination? Then, be prepared to pay more as from 1 July 2026, employers sponsoring overseas workers through Victoria's Great South Coast DAMA will be subject to paying higher nomination fees that increase the cost of accessing the regional migration program.

The updated fees only apply to applications lodged on or after 1 July 2026, while applications submitted before that date are assessed under the earlier fee structure.

## New Great South Coast DAMA Nomination Fees

**From 1 July the nomination fees for the Great South Coast DAMA have increased as follows:**

- businesses with an annual turnover of less than $10 million: $850 (ex GST) per worker nominated
- businesses with an annual turnover of more than $10 million: $1000 (ex GST) per worker nominated

These fees are charged per nominated worker and form part of the overall employer sponsorship costs under the GSC DAMA program.

## What Is the Great South Coast DAMA?

The Great South Coast (GSC) DAMA is a regional labour agreement designed to help employers across Victoria's Great South Coast region to address ongoing workforce shortages. It allows eligible employers to sponsor overseas skilled and semi-skilled workers for occupations that are difficult to fill locally.

Compared to standard employer-sponsored visa pathways, the GSC DAMA offers access to a broader range of occupations. In some cases, concessions related to age, English language, salary, or work experience may also apply based on the occupation and eligibility requirements.

## What Does This Mean for Employers?

Even though the increase is relatively small, employers should keep the revised nomination fees in mind when planning to hire overseas workers. For businesses sponsoring multiple employees through the GSC DAMA, the overall cost of nominations will naturally be higher from 1 July 2026.

It is also important to remember that the nomination fee is only one part of the total sponsorship cost. Visa application charges, skills assessment fees (where required), the Skilling Australians Fund (SAF) levy type other charges are also included under an employer-sponsored visa.
