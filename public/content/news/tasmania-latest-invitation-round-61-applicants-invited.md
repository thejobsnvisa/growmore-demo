Tasmania remains one of the states to watch for skilled migrants in the 2026–27 state nomination program. While applicants in some other states are still waiting for invitation activity to begin, the Northern Territory has mostly closed its Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 nomination pathways for 2026–27, while Tasmania continues to run regular invitation rounds.

On 8 October 2026, Tasmania issued its latest round of invitations, inviting a total of **61 applicants** across the Skilled Nominated visa (Subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (Subclass 491).

## Tasmania Issues 37 Subclass 190 and 24 Subclass 491 Invitations

Tasmania issued 61 invitations in its 8 October 2026 round. This included 37 invitations for the Skilled Nominated visa (Subclass 190) and 24 for the Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (Subclass 491).

The latest invitation data also shows a clear difference in priority categories and scores between the two visa pathways.

| Visa Subclass | Invitations Issued | Priority Category  | Lowest Invited Score |
| ------------- | -----------------: | ------------------ | -------------------: |
| Subclass 190  |                 37 | 13 Gold, 24 Green  |           323 points |
| Subclass 491  |                 24 | 2 Gold, 22 Further |            89 points |

These figures represent the lowest scores among applicants invited in this particular round. They should not be treated as a guaranteed points threshold for the next Tasmania invitation round.

## What the Latest Tasmania Invitation Round Means for Applicants

Tasmania's 8 October invitation round confirms that the state is continuing to issue invitations under both Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 in the 2026–27 program year.

However, applicants need to be careful when interpreting the published scores. Tasmania ROI points differ from SkillSelect EOI points, so you should not compare them as if they use the same scoring system.

This means the 323 points recorded for Subclass 190 and 89 points for Subclass 491 in this round are Tasmania ROI scores, rather than SkillSelect EOI points cut-offs.

Applicants should also avoid treating these scores as guaranteed thresholds for future invitation rounds. The lowest score invited can change from one round to another depending on the composition of the ROI pool, Tasmania's priorities and the availability of nomination places.

For example, Tasmania's current rolling three-month lowest point score resulting in an invitation is 440 points for Subclass 190 and 81 points for Subclass 491. These figures are different from the lowest scores recorded in the latest round.

For applicants seeking a more accessible nomination option, Subclass 491 may be worth considering based on the latest Tasmania scores, while Subclass 190 offers the advantage of a direct permanent visa pathway.

## Planning Your Tasmania Skilled Migration Pathway?

If you are considering Tasmania Subclass 190 or Subclass 491 nomination, the latest invitation results can help you understand the current selection landscape, but your individual eligibility, ROI points and priority attributes should be assessed before choosing any pathway.
