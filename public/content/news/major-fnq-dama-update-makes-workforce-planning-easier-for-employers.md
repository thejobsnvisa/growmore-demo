If you are planning to hire overseas workers through the FNQ DAMA, then make a note that the Cairns Chamber of Commerce brings several practical updates that employers and migration professionals should know before lodging their next endorsement request.

These updates provide greater clarity on the FNQ DAMA process, which includes endorsing positions across the full five-year labour agreement, requesting concessions upfront and avoiding common documentation issues.

So, let’s know about each point so that you can make a successful and more efficient FNQ DAMA application.

## 1.  **Employers Can Now Seek Endorsements for the Full Five-Year Labour Agreement**

One of the most significant updates is related to the Department of Home Affairs' revised approach to FNQ DAMA endorsements.

Previously, employers were generally advised to request endorsements only for positions which are required during the first year of their labour agreement. But under the new guidance, Designated Area Representatives (DARs) are encouraged to consider an employer's current and future workforce requirements and endorse eligible positions for the entire five years of the labour agreement.

The change applies to both initial endorsement applications and variation requests. It also allows Designated Area Representatives to consider Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS) pathways for eligible Skills in Demand (SID) visa holders.

For employers, this means they need to make fewer variation requests, reduce administrative work, and have greater certainty at the time of planning future recruitment.

## 2.  **Apply for Available Concessions from the Start**

The Cairns Chamber also recommends that employers should request all available FNQ DAMA concessions during the initial endorsement process. This includes concessions related to English language or Age.

Many employers are currently requesting these concessions when they need to replace their original nominee. However, adding these concessions later can lead to extra paperwork and longer processing times.

So, the better approach is to request any concessions your business is eligible for when you submit the initial endorsement application. Even if you do not want to use them at that time, having them approved from the start gives you more flexibility if your hiring plans change later.

## 3.  **Be Careful When Using Third-Party or AI-Generated Reports**

Another important reminder relates to supporting documents submitted with endorsement applications. The Cairns Chamber has observed that some migration agents in Australia involve third-party providers to prepare documents such as Genuine Position Reports and Labour Market Testing (LMT) Reports.

In some cases, outsourced services or artificial intelligence are preparing these reports, which results in generic content and does not accurately reflect the employer's business.

According to the Chamber, reports that are repetitive, inaccurate or misleading may add little value and have even contributed to endorsement applications being declined.

So, it is being advised that every report should be specifically related to the business, factually accurate and properly attributed to the report provider where applicable.

## 4.  **FNQ DAMA Does Not Generally Support On-Hire Arrangements**

The Department of Home Affairs has also clarified its position on on-hire labour arrangements during these FNQ DAMA updates.

Businesses cannot generally use the FNQ DAMA Labour Agreement to sponsor workers who will be hired out to other employers. Instead, these arrangements are only supported under the On-Hire Industry Labour Agreement (OHLA), which has separate eligibility requirements.

This clarification helps employers choose the correct sponsorship pathway before lodging an application.

## 5.  **Practical Tips to Avoid Processing Delays**

The Cairns Chamber has also shared a few practical reminders that can help employers make the endorsement application process smoother. It includes:

- Financial documents should generally be issued within the last 12 months.
- Any changes to your business address or contact details should be updated through the Change of Circumstances form in ImmiAccount before submitting an endorsement request.

By keeping business records up to date, you can prevent unnecessary delays during assessment.

## *What Do These Mean for Employers?**

These updates provide guidance for employers by giving clarity on how endorsement applications should be prepared and managed.

For employers, the biggest advantage is the ability to plan workforce needs across the full five-year labour agreement, which reduces future variation requests. The guidance also highlights the importance of submitting clear, business-specific documentation and requesting any eligible concessions upfront to avoid unnecessary delays.

So, if your business is considering sponsoring overseas workers through the FNQ DAMA, understanding these operational updates can help you lodge a stronger endorsement application and avoid common pitfalls.
