As previously announced by Tasmania, the state has conducted its final state nomination invitation round for the 2025–26 program year and issued a total of 71 invitations to candidates who want to migrate to Tasmania under the Skilled Nominated (Subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional (Subclass 491) visa pathways.

The final round, held on 4 June 2026, provides an important snapshot of Tasmania's remaining nomination capacity, application pipeline, and the level of competition among skilled migration applicants as the state approaches the end of the current program year.

**Here is the full update:**

## Latest Tasmania Invitation Round Results – 4 June 2026

| Visa Subclass | Invitations Issued | Lowest Score Invited |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Subclass 190 | 44 | 500 points (Green Pass) |
| Subclass 491 | 27 | 300 points (Green Pass) |
| Total | 71 | - |

The latest results show that Tasmania continued to prioritise Green Pass candidates in its final invitation round of the 2025–26 program year across both subclasses.

## Tasmania Skilled Migration Program Status After the Final Round

**Following the invitation round, Tasmania reported the following figures:**

| Category | Subclass 190 | Subclass 491 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ROIs on Hand After the Invitation Round | 580 | 598 |
| Nomination Places Available | 41 | 123 |
| Applications Lodged but Not Yet Decided | 179 | 138 |
| Invitations Issued but Application Not Yet Lodged | 62 | 76 |

The figures highlight strong ongoing demand for Tasmania state nomination, with more than 1,100 Registrations of Interest (ROIs) still on hand despite the limited number of nomination places remaining across both visa pathways. At the same time, a significant number of applications and invitations are still progressing through the nomination process.

## **Processing Times Update**

Tasmania has also confirmed that the oldest nomination application awaiting allocation to a case officer was lodged on 6 March 2026.

## **What Applicants Should Know Now**

With the final round already completed, the remaining number of places stands at 41 for Subclass 190 and 123 for Subclass 491, keeping competition for Tasmania nomination high. 

The latest figures also show that there are still 580 ROIs on hand for Subclass 190 and 598 ROIs on hand for Subclass 491, compared to 317 nomination applications currently awaiting a decision across both visa subclasses. 

This suggests that a large number of the remaining nomination places may already be committed to applications currently under assessment or to candidates who have already received invitations. As a result, applicants who have not yet received an invitation may need to wait for Tasmania's next program year.

Applicants with an active ROI should continue to monitor Tasmania's migration updates and ensure that their information remains accurate and up to date.
