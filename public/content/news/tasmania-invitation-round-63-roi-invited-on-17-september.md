Thursday means another Tasmania invitation round that brings a fresh update for skilled migrants waiting for an opportunity to apply for state nomination.

The state continues to assess Registrations of Interest (ROIs) from skilled migrants looking to secure a nomination through the state’s skilled migration program and issues invitations every week throughout the program year. The latest round was held on **17 September 2026** and provides a fresh update for applicants.

So, what do the numbers mean for applicants considering **Subclass 190** and **Subclass 491** nomination in Tasmania? Let’s take a closer look.

## Tasmania Invitation Round: 17 September 2026

In the latest invitation round, Tasmania issued **37 invitations for Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated visa)** and **26 invitations for Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa)**.

For Subclass 190, **7 of the invitations were Gold and 30 were Green**, while for Subclass 491, there were **no Gold invitations**, with all **26 issued as further invitations**.

Here is the complete breakdown of this invitation round:

| Factor                                              | Subclass 190 | Subclass 491 |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -----------: | -----------: |
| Invitations issued                                  |           37 |           26 |
| Gold invitations                                    |            7 |            0 |
| Green / further invitations                         |           30 |           26 |
| Lowest score invited                                |   375 points |    72 points |
| ROIs on hand                                        |          880 |          590 |
| Nomination places available                         |        1,000 |          713 |
| Nomination applications lodged but not decided      |            6 |           24 |
| Invitations issued but not lodged                   |           81 |           56 |
| Lowest score at assessment, rolling 3-month average |   504 points |    83 points |

The latest invitation round also shows a notable change in the lowest scores invited compared with the previous round held on **10 September 2026**.

For **Subclass 190**, the lowest invited score dropped from **502 points** in the previous round to **375 points** in this round. For **Subclass 491**, the lowest invited score also decreased from **74 points to 72 points**.

This means applicants were invited at lower scores in this round than in the previous round. However, these figures reflect the outcome of a specific invitation round and should not be treated as guaranteed scores for future invitations.

## Subclass 491 Score Remains Much Lower Than Subclass 190

The latest invitation scores show a significant difference between Tasmania's two skilled nomination pathways.

The lowest score invited was **375 points for Subclass 190**, compared with **72 points for Subclass 491**. This shows that the minimum invited score for Subclass 491 was substantially lower in this round than for Subclass 190.

This may make the regional pathway worth considering for applicants who have a lower points score but meet the relevant eligibility and nomination requirements.
