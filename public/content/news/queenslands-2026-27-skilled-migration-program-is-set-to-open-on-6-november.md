Queensland's 2026–27 Skilled Migration Program will open for Registrations of Interest (ROIs) on 6 November 2026, with updated program requirements. So, skilled migrants who have been waiting for Queensland's next nomination program can now start preparing ahead of the opening date.  

Queensland Government has provided information about skilled visa pathways, occupation lists and a documentation checklist for the upcoming program year. Therefore, applicants planning to apply for a nomination under the Subclass 190 or Subclass 491 visa must take a close look at the requirements for this program year. 

## What Are the Skilled Migration Pathways Offered by Queensland? 

There are many different skilled migration pathways offered by the Queensland state based on an individual's residence status, occupation, employment history and other criteria. For 2026–27, the state has identified the following nomination pathways: 

**1.Skilled Workers Living Outside Australia (Offshore):** This is for skilled workers from overseas who can comply with Queensland's offshore occupation and nomination requirements.   

**2.Skilled Workers Living in Queensland (Onshore):** This pathway is designed for skilled workers currently residing in Queensland who meet the necessary occupation, employment and other eligibility criteria. 

**3.Building and Construction Workers in Queensland (Onshore):** This pathway is available for eligible personnel working in the building and construction sector of Queensland. 

**4.Queensland University Graduates (Onshore):** Eligible graduates who have completed their studies at a Queensland university may be considered under this pathway, subject to the applicable requirements. 

**5.Small Business Owners in Regional Queensland (Onshore):** This pathway is designed for eligible small business owners operating in regional Queensland. It is available for the Subclass 491 Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa only. 

Each pathway has its own eligibility conditions. Therefore, applicants should identify the pathway that matches their circumstances rather than relying on the general skilled migration requirements alone. 

## Queensland Skilled Occupation Lists for 2026–27 

The Queensland Skilled Occupation Lists (QSOLs) identify occupations eligible for state nomination under the 2026–27 SNMP. These lists cover occupations linked to workforce shortages and the skill requirements of Queensland's growing industries. 

Queensland publishes separate occupation lists for: 

* Skilled workers currently residing in Australia - [Queensland onshore skilled occupation list](https://www.migration.qld.gov.au/occupation-lists/queensland-onshore-skilled-occupation-list) 

* Skilled workers residing outside Australia - [Queensland offshore skilled occupation list](https://www.migration.qld.gov.au/occupation-lists/offshore-queensland-skilled-occupation-lists-(qsol))

The occupation lists are based on the 2013 ANZSCO classification, in line with the Australian Government's requirements for subclass 190 and subclass 491 visa nominations. 

## Work Experience Requirements for Queensland State Nomination 

For the 2026–27 program, the work experience requirements are: 

Nomination pathway 
| Nomination pathway | Required work experience |
| --- | --- |
| Subclass 190 visa | 9 out of the last 12 months |
| Subclass 491 visa | 6 out of the last 9 months |
| Building and construction workers pathway | 3 continuous months |
| Skilled workers living offshore | 1 year within the last 3 years |

Applicants should ensure that their employment evidence covers the complete qualifying period applicable to their pathway. 

## ATO Notice of Assessment Mandatory for Onshore Applicants 

Queensland has specified an important tax documentation requirement for onshore applicants under the 2026–27 Skilled Migration Program. 

Before submitting their Registration of Interest (ROI), onshore applicants must have: 

* Lodged their 2025–26 income tax return; and 

* Received their corresponding ATO Notice of Assessment (NOA).

With Queensland's 2026–27 ROI opening date confirmed, skilled migrants should start preparing rather than wait until the last minute.
