Good news for applicants waiting for an Australia Work and Holiday (subclass 462) visa ballot update, as the Department of Home Affairs has recently conducted another round of ballot selections for eligible passport holders from India and Vietnam.

For Indian passport holders, the latest selection was conducted on **25 September 2026**, following the earlier selection held on **10 August 2026**. Vietnam also recorded a further selection on **29 September 2026**, after its first selection on **26 August 2026**.

Here is a look at the latest developments in the 462 visa ballot process for India and Vietnam.

## Recent Australia 462 Visa Ballot Selections

The first Work and Holiday (subclass 462) visa ballot is open for applicants with passports from **China, India and Vietnam**.

The latest selection activity for the **2026–27 program year** includes:

| Country | First Selection | Latest Selection  |
| ------- | --------------- | ----------------- |
| India   | 10 August 2026  | 25 September 2026 |
| Vietnam | 26 August 2026  | 29 September 2026 |

The Department will conduct a further random selection during the selection period. The selection period for India and Vietnam for the 2026–27 program year ends on **30 April 2027**.

However, new applicants who did not register during the registration period have missed the opportunity to register for the current ballot.

Those who registered for the India or Vietnam ballot will stay in the selection pool until they are selected, their registration expires, or they withdraw their registration.

## What Happens After 462 Visa Ballot Selection?

Applicants who are randomly selected receive a **Notification of Selection** by email and through their ImmiAccount.

Selection does not mean the subclass 462 visa has been granted. It allows the selected applicant to proceed with a first Work and Holiday visa application by meeting the particular visa requirements.

Selected applicants have **28 calendar days** from the date of notification to lodge their visa application. The deadline is based on **Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST)**. It is also mentioned in the selection notification.

So, make sure to follow this timeline carefully.

Applicants should regularly check their email and ImmiAccount. If you receive a selection notification, act quickly to avoid missing the deadline.

## Important Requirements for Selected Applicants

Applicants selected through the ballot must still meet the eligibility requirements for the first Work and Holiday (subclass 462) visa.

For **Indian passport holders**, the Department requires a valid **PAN card**, rather than an Aadhaar card, as the national identity document for the ballot process.

**Vietnamese passport holders** must hold a valid **12-digit national identity card**, rather than the older 9-digit card.

The identity details provided during ballot registration must also match the details provided in the subsequent visa application.
