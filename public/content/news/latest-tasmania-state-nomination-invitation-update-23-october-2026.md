## Tasmania Subclass 190 and 491: Latest Points Score Update

The latest Tasmania state nomination update reports the lowest scores invited as **317 points for Subclass 190** and **76 points for Subclass 491**; both are lower than in the previous round.

Tasmania also reported the lowest points scores resulting in nomination at assessment, calculated as a rolling three-month average:

- **Subclass 190:** 416 points
- **Subclass 491:** 81 points

These assessment figures differ from the lowest scores invited in the latest round. The latest invitation scores reflect the most recent round, whereas the assessment figures provide a rolling three-month view.

## Important Tasmania Nomination Rule: Priority Attributes Must Be Verified

One of the most important details in Tasmania's latest update concerns the verification of priority attributes claimed by candidates.

When an ROI is invited, Tasmania examines the priority attributes claimed by the applicant. If the information provided in the ROI and nomination application is found to be true and correct, the nomination may be approved on the basis of the applicable requirements.

However, if one or more priority attributes cannot be verified and the applicant's points score falls below the applicable lowest-score threshold, the nomination application will be declined.
