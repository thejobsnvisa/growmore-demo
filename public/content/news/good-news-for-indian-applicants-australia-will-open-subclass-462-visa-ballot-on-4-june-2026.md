Young Indian applicants planning to work and travel in Australia have finally received an important update. The Australian Government has officially confirmed that ballot registrations for the 2026–27 Work and Holiday (subclass 462) visa program will open from 4 June 2026.

Along with India, the ballot process will also run for China and Vietnam during the same registration period. As the registration will remain open for only a limited time, interested applicants are expected to begin preparing early to avoid last-minute issues during the ballot process.

## What Is the Subclass 462 Visa Ballot Process?

Australia introduced the visa pre-application ballot system for selecting applicants from high-demand countries.

Instead of allowing unlimited direct applications, eligible applicants must first register through the ballot system inside ImmiAccount. Registrations are then randomly selected throughout the program year, meaning selection is random once applicants meet the eligibility requirements.

**The ballot registration fee is AUD25.**

Importantly, being selected in the ballot does not automatically guarantee visa approval. Applicants must still meet all eligibility and visa requirements when lodging the actual Subclass 462 Work and Holiday visa application.

## Key Registration Dates

**For the 2026–2027 program year, the registration period for applicants is:**

* Registration Opens: 4 June 2026  
* Registration Closes: 25 June 2026  
* Selection Period Starts: 2 July 2026  
* Selection Period Ends: 30 April 2027  

Multiple random selection rounds may occur throughout the program year until available places are filled or registrations expire.

## Who Can Register for the Ballot?

**To register for the subclass 462 visa ballot, applicants must:**

* Be between 18 and 30 years of age (inclusive)  
* Hold a valid Indian passport  
* Hold a valid national identity card  
* Create an ImmiAccount  
* Have a valid email address  
* Pay the AUD25 registration fee  
* Agree to the required declarations  

## How the Selection Process Works

The selection process is fully random and automated. Australia does not use ranking scores, occupations, or additional selection criteria during the ballot process.

If an applicant is randomly selected, they will receive a ‘Notification of Selection’ letter by email. Selected applicants will then have 28 calendar days to lodge their first [Work and Holiday (subclass 462) visa application](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/australias-working-holiday-maker-program-a-visa-opportunity-for-young-travellers/) through ImmiAccount.

However, applicants who are not selected will not receive a separate notification. The Department has also confirmed that the AUD25 ballot registration fee is non-refundable, including situations where applicants withdraw their registration or are not selected.

## Practical Advice Before Registrations Open

As demand for the subclass 462 program remains very high among Indian applicants, it is important to prepare documents and account access early instead of waiting until the final days of registration.

**Applicants should ideally:**

* Create or access their ImmiAccount early  
* Set up MFA before registrations open  
* Keep passport and PAN card details ready  
* Carefully review all entered information  
* Avoid submitting registrations at the last minute
