Two Australian states have received extensions to their Designated Area Migration Agreements (DAMAs) that gives regional employers more time to sponsor overseas skilled workers under existing arrangements.

The extensions apply to the WA Goldfields DAMA, which has been extended until 31 December 2026, and South Australia's two existing DAMAs, which will remain in effect until 30 September 2026.

Below are the key details of both DAMA extensions.

## WA Goldfields DAMA Extended Until December 2026

The Australian Government has extended the Goldfields Designated Area Migration Agreement (DAMA) until 31 December 2026, which gives employers in the region more time to access overseas skilled workers through the existing program.

Earlier, the Goldfields DAMA was expected to transition to the statewide WA DAMA on 1 July 2026. However, it got an extension, which means eligible businesses can continue using the current agreement for another six months.

To use the Goldfields DAMA, employers must first receive endorsement from the City of Kalgoorlie-Boulder, which acts as the region's Designated Area Representative (DAR). After receiving endorsement, businesses can apply for a DAMA Labour Agreement with the Australian Government and sponsor eligible overseas workers.

**Read the Official [Goldfields DAMA Extension Announcement here](https://migration.wa.gov.au/news/goldfields-employers-continue-access-goldfields-dama-until-31-december-2026)**

## South Australia DAMAs Extended Until September 2026

The South Australia has also extended its two existing Designated Area Migration Agreements (DAMAs) until 30 September 2026. This gives regional employers a continued access for overseas skilled workers while negotiations continue on a new five-year statewide DAMA.

This three-month extension means employers can continue using the current DAMA arrangements without any changes. The existing occupation lists, eligibility requirements, and available concessions remain the same as those introduced in June 2025.

**Read the Official [South Australia DAMAs Extension Update here](https://migration.sa.gov.au/news/extension-for-designated-area-migration-agreements-damas)**

## Planning to Apply Under a DAMA?

The extensions to the WA Goldfields and South Australia DAMAs ensure that eligible employers can continue sponsoring overseas skilled workers under the current agreements while new long-term arrangements are being developed.

For employers and skilled migrants, this means existing occupation lists, concessions, and eligibility requirements remain available during the extension period. However, staying updated on future changes will be also important as both states prepare to introduce new DAMA agreements.
