The wait is finally over for those who are waiting for Tasmania to reopen its State Nomination Program. Migration Tasmania has confirmed that the state will open to new Registrations of Interest (ROIs) from 17 August 2026 for eligible skilled migrants. This indicates the start of the 2026–27 Skilled Migration Program in Tasmania.

Along with the opening, there are also several important changes to the program, including updated priority attributes, revised selection criteria, a new scoring approach, increased application fees, and a new online application portal.

So, if you are planning to apply for Tasmania state nomination under the Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190) or Skilled Work Regional (Subclass 491), understand these changes and start preparing for your visa application.

## New ROIs Open from 17 August

The state will begin accepting new Registrations of Interest (ROIs) from 17 August 2026 for 2,050 nomination places, including:

- 1,250 places for the Subclass 190 Skilled Nominated visa
- 800 places for the Subclass 491 Skilled Work Regional visa

Submitting an ROI is the first step in securing a Tasmania state nomination.

After that, eligible applicants are ranked against the state's priority attributes, and selected candidates are invited to lodge a nomination application.

> **Note:** Candidates who have submitted an ROI within the last six months should carefully review the updated priority attributes. They are also advised to withdraw an existing ROI and lodge a new one to improve selection chances under the revised scoring system.

## Weekly Invitation Rounds Begin on 20 August

Migration Tasmania will commence weekly invitation rounds from Thursday, 20 August 2026.

Like last year, invitations will be issued regularly throughout the program year until all nomination places are filled.

Each week, Migration Tasmania will publish the following data so that applicants have an idea of their chances of receiving an invitation:

- The number of ROIs invited
- The lowest invitation scores
- Remaining nomination places
- The number of ROIs currently on hand

The department will also publish the average of the lowest ROI scores invited over the previous three months. This provides applicants with an indication of the score currently required for nomination.

## Tasmania Updates Priority Attributes and Selection Policy

Following a review of the previous program and feedback from stakeholders, Tasmania has introduced several changes to better align its nomination process with long-term settlement and workforce priorities.

The Tasmanian Government found that many applicants found it difficult to choose the correct priority attribute, especially when salary or work experience requirements were involved. It also found that the previous priority system did not clearly separate applicants based on their employment history in Tasmania.

In some cases, the TSE Gold priority did not properly recognise people with long-term employment or a genuine commitment to living and working in Tasmania.

To address these issues, Tasmania has introduced several important changes, including:

- Clearer and easier-to-understand priority attribute wording.
- Revised scoring that better recognises longer contributions to Tasmania.
- Greater differentiation in points based on employment duration.
- Updated guidance for leave periods and breaks between jobs.
- The TSE Gold priority attribute now requires applicants to work at least 30 hours per week in at least one eligible position.
- Income-based priority attributes have been updated to reflect Australian median earnings, average earnings and the Temporary Skilled Migration Income Threshold (TSMIT). The existing Priority Income Level of $57,000 per year or $28.85 per hour remains unchanged.
- Additional assessment requirements now apply for Massage Therapists and Motor Mechanics/Service Technicians, where employment must appropriately align with the applicant's skills assessment or relevant study.

## Nomination Application Fee Increased

Migration Tasmania has increased the service fee for lodging a state nomination application to **$387 ($425.70 including GST).**

However, note that there is **no fee** to submit a Registration of Interest (ROI).

## New Application Gateway

Applicants should also note that Migration Tasmania has changed the domain name for its Application Gateway, which is used to submit ROIs and nomination applications.

Anyone who previously bookmarked the portal should update the website address before the program opens on **17 August 2026**. The new Application Gateway will become available from the reopening date.

## No Invitations for Overseas Invitation-Only Subclass 491 Pathway

Migration Tasmania has confirmed that it will **not** issue invitations during the **2026–27** program year under the **Subclass 491 Overseas Skilled Occupation Profiles – Invitation Only Pathway.**

This pathway was designed for overseas skilled workers who do not have employment in Tasmania but possess occupations considered critical to the state's workforce needs.

However, overseas healthcare professionals and teachers who secure employment with a Tasmanian health provider or school may still be eligible to apply for **Subclass 190** state nomination through the **Overseas Applicant – Health or Education Sector Job Offer** pathway.
