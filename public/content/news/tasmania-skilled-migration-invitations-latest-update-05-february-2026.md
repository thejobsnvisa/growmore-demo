## Tasmania Skilled Migration Invitations: Latest Update (05 February 2026)

Migration Tasmania has released this week’s latest invitation figures on 5th February, which offer a clear picture of how Tasmania’s skilled nomination program is currently processing. The update provides information about current invitation numbers, minimum points selected, and the size of the remaining application pipeline.

So, if you are students, graduates, or skilled migrants who are targeting Tasmania, these numbers indicate how competitive the program is right now and how realistic an invitation may be in the coming weeks.

## **Invitations Issued – 05 February 2026**

In this invitation round, Migration Tasmania issued a total of **61 invitations** across the two skilled nomination pathways:

- **Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated – Permanent):** 35 ROIs invited
- **Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional – Provisional):** 26 ROIs invited

This shows how Tasmania continues to manage overall program allocations while addressing talent shortages through the use of both permanent and regional visas.

**Lowest Points Scores Invited**

The minimum points scores selected in this round were:

- **Subclass 190:** 51 points (Orange pass)
- **Subclass 491:** 37 points (Orange pass)

**Nomination Places Available**

Tasmania continues to have a substantial number of nomination places remaining for the program year:

- **Subclass 190:** 698 places available
- **Subclass 491**: 486 places available

**ROIs Currently on Hand**

After this invitation round, the number of submitted ROIs remaining in the system stands at:

- **Subclass 190:** 484 ROIs
- **Subclass 491:** 199 ROIs

This shows that although competition exists, there are still enough nomination places available compared to the number of pending ROIs.

**Applications in Progress**

As per the latest update, nomination applications are at the following stages:

**Applications lodged but not yet decided:**

- Subclass 190: 266 applications
- Subclass 491: 166 applications

**Invitations issued but application not yet lodged:**

- Subclass 190: 104 invitations
- Subclass 491: 70 invitations

The **oldest nomination application not yet allocated to a case officer** was lodged on **03 December 2025,** which indicates ongoing processing with expected wait times.

## **What This Means for Applicants**

### **If You Have Already Submitted an ROI**

Based on the latest data, applicants with 51 points for Subclass 190 or 37 points for Subclass 491 under an orange pass are still being considered. So, if you can increase your points by improving your English score, gaining more skilled work experience, or adding a higher qualification, it can improve your chances of receiving an invitation.

### **If You Are Planning to Submit an ROI**

Tasmania is still open for skilled nomination under both Subclass 190 and Subclass 491, and the number of ROIs is also not overly high. This shows that the state is continuing to invite skilled migrants.

To improve your chances, you should clearly show strong links to Tasmania, such as:

- Working for, or having a job offer from, a Tasmanian employer
- Completing studies in Tasmania
- Having close family members living in Tasmania

These factors significantly influence selection outcomes in Tasmania’s skilled migration program.

### **If You Have Received an Invitation**

A total of 174 invitations issued to date (104 for Subclass 190 and 70 for Subclass 491) have not yet been converted into lodged applications. This highlights the importance of acting promptly once invited.

So, if you receive an invitation for state nomination, you have 28 days to log in to your Gateway account and submit your application.
