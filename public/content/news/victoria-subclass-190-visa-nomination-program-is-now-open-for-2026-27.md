Victoria has officially opened its 2026–27 skilled visa nomination program for Registrations of Interest (ROIs) for the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190). The state has received **900 nomination places for Subclass 190** in the current program year.

For skilled migrants planning to pursue Australian PR through Victorian state nomination, now is the time to review their eligibility, ensure their SkillSelect Expression of Interest (EOI) is accurate and prepare to submit an ROI in line with Victoria’s current requirements.

## Is Victoria Open for Subclass 190 Visa Nomination in 2026–27?

Yes. According to the Victorian Government’s announcement on 26 October 2026, Victoria’s 2026–27 skilled visa nomination program is now open for Registrations of Interest (ROIs) for the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190).

However, the opening of the nomination program does not mean every applicant will receive an invitation. Victoria ranks ROIs using the Victorian Points Test (VPT), with priority industries also considered as part of its selection process.

Applicants must meet the current Victorian 190 nomination criteria, including the following requirements:

- Be living in Victoria or overseas.
- Demonstrate a commitment to living and working in Victoria.
- Have their Registration of Interest (ROI) selected by the Victorian Government.
- Be under 45 years of age.
- Have at least Competent English.
- Hold a valid skills assessment for an occupation on the eligible skilled occupation list for the Subclass 190 visa.
- Have at least 65 points under the Australian Government’s points test for their SkillSelect Expression of Interest (EOI).

**Note:** The 65-point requirement relates to the Australian Government’s skilled visa points test. Victoria’s VPT is a separate ranking system used to assess ROIs.

## How Does Victoria’s 2026–27 Allocation Compare With 2025–26?

Victoria’s nomination allocation has changed significantly compared with the previous program year. Based on the 2025–26 allocation figures published by the Department of Home Affairs, Victoria’s Subclass 190 places have dropped from 2,700 to just 900 for 2026–27. This is a significant reduction, and it may make state nomination more competitive for skilled migrants hoping to secure Australian PR through Victoria.

Meanwhile, the Subclass 491 allocation for 2026–27 is still awaiting confirmation. Applicants interested in this pathway will need to wait for Victoria to confirm its nomination places before further information about the program becomes available.

| Visa subclass | 2025–26 allocation | 2026–27 allocation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Skilled Nominated visa (Subclass 190) | 2,700 | 900 |
| Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (Subclass 491) | 700 | Not yet finalised |

## Is Victoria Open for Subclass 491 Visa Nomination in 2026–27?

No. Victoria’s Subclass 491 Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa nomination program is still not open for Registrations of Interest (ROIs) in 2026–27.

According to the Victorian Government, the state’s nomination places for Subclass 491 have not yet been finalised. ROIs are not currently being accepted, and further information will be published once the nomination places are confirmed.

## Victorian Nomination Fees for 2026–27

As Victoria has introduced fees for submitting a Registration of Interest (ROI) for the 2026–27 program, applicants planning to apply for Subclass 190 nomination should be aware of the following costs:

| Application stage | Excluding GST | Including GST* |
|---|---:|---:|
| Registration of Interest (ROI) | AUD 60 | AUD 66 |
| Subclass 190 nomination application | AUD 180 | AUD 198 |

The ROI fee is payable once per program year and is generally non-refundable, even if your ROI does not result in an invitation.
