After months of anticipation, skilled migrants looking to secure Australia PR through Victoria can now get a clearer picture of the 2026–27 Skilled Migration Program. Victoria has outlined its Victorian Points Test (VPT), explaining how applicants will be ranked for state nomination under the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491).

The updated criteria also identify priority occupations across healthcare, social assistance, education and construction. Here’s what skilled migrants need to know about Victoria’s new points system, how ROIs will be ranked, and which occupations are included in the 2026–27 priority list.

## What Is the Victorian Points Test (VPT)?

The Victorian Points Test is used to rank ROIs when selecting applicants for Victorian nomination for subclasses 190 and 491.

The ranking considers seven weighted factors:

| Selection factor | What it considers |
|---|---|
| Age | The applicant's age |
| English language | The applicant's English proficiency |
| Years of experience | Experience in the nominated occupation |
| Educational qualification | The applicant's educational qualifications |
| Occupation skill level | The skill level associated with the occupation |
| Partner's skill | Relevant partner skills, where applicable |
| Annual earnings | Earnings from skilled employment in Victoria, where applicable |

## How Will Victoria Select Applicants for Nomination?

Victoria has outlined two main selection approaches under its skilled visa nomination program.

### Most competitive applicants

In each invitation round, ROIs with the highest VPT totals across all eligible occupations in the pool may be invited to apply for nomination.

### Priority industry applicants

Victoria also identifies occupations within priority industries to address critical skills needs. In each invitation round, ROIs with the highest VPT totals within each priority industry or industry subgroup may be invited.

## Victoria's Priority Industries and Occupations for 2026–27

## What Should Applicants Do Before Submitting an ROI?

Applicants considering Victorian state nomination should review both their eligibility and their potential competitiveness under the VPT. Key steps include:

- **Check occupation eligibility:** Confirm that the nominated occupation meets the relevant visa and Victorian program requirements.
- **Review the VPT factors:** Understand how age, English proficiency, qualifications, work experience and other applicable factors contribute to the score.
- **Check priority industry criteria:** If the occupation appears on Victoria's priority list, review any additional selection conditions that apply to the relevant industry subgroup.
- **Keep information consistent:** Ensure the SkillSelect EOI and Victorian ROI contain accurate, consistent information.
- **Review the current program settings:** Check official guidance before submitting an ROI or nomination application.
