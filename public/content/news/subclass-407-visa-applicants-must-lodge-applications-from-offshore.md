Disappointing news has come for people who were planning to apply for a **Subclass 407 Training Visa** without leaving Australia. The Australian Government has introduced new regulations requiring most applicants to be outside Australia when lodging their Subclass 407 Training Visa application and when their visa is granted.

These changes will come into effect on **12 November 2026**, meaning that most applicants who were planning to apply for the 407 Training Visa while staying in Australia will need to reconsider their plans.

The change has been introduced through the *Migration Amendment (Training Visas—Application Requirements) Regulations 2026*, made on 29 October 2026. The Department of Home Affairs has also updated its official website to reflect these changes to the Subclass 407 visa application requirements.

## What Has Changed in the Subclass 407 Visa Rules?

Under the new regulations, two important requirements will apply to most Subclass 407 visa applicants.

### 1. Applicants Must Be Outside Australia When Lodging the Application

From 12 November 2026, most Subclass 407 visa applicants will need to be outside Australia when submitting their visa application.

Previously, eligible applicants could lodge their 407 visa applications while in Australia after meeting the relevant application requirements. However, from 12 November 2026, applying from within Australia will no longer be the general option.

### 2. Applicants Must Be Outside Australia When the Visa Is Granted

The new regulations also introduce an offshore requirement at the time of 407 visa grant.

This means that applicants must generally be outside Australia not only when submitting their application but also when the Department of Home Affairs makes the decision to grant the visa.

Therefore, applicants should carefully consider their travel plans while their 407 visa application is being processed, as they may need to leave Australia before the visa is granted.

## What Happens to Subclass 407 Applications Lodged Before 12 November 2026?

The new rules will apply only to Subclass 407 visa applications lodged on or after 12 November 2026.

This means:

- Applications lodged before 12 November 2026 will not be affected by the new offshore requirements.
- Applications lodged on or after 12 November 2026 must meet the new offshore requirements, unless an exemption applies.
- Applications lodged before the changes take effect will continue to be assessed under the previous rules, even if the visa decision is made after 12 November 2026.

**Example:** If you lodge your 407 visa application on 10 November 2026 and the Department makes a decision in December, the new offshore requirements will not apply to your application.

## What Should You Do If You Are Planning to Apply for a Subclass 407 Visa?

If you are currently in Australia and considering a Subclass 407 Training Visa, this change could affect your next visa decision.

Here are some important points to consider:

- **Check Your Current Visa Expiry Date:** Understand how much time you have to prepare your 407 visa application and make appropriate arrangements.
- **Review Your Application Timeline:** If you plan to apply on or after 12 November 2026, you will generally need to lodge your application from outside Australia.
- **Plan Your Travel Carefully:** If you apply offshore and subsequently return to Australia on another valid visa, you will generally need to leave Australia again before your 407 visa can be granted, unless you qualify for an exemption.
- **Check Your Eligibility:** Make sure your sponsorship, nomination and training arrangements meet the applicable Subclass 407 visa requirements.
