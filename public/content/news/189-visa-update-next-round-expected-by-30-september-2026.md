Thousands of skilled migration applicants waiting for a Subclass 189 invitation may not have to wait much longer. The Department of Home Affairs has confirmed that the next invitation round is expected to be conducted by 30 September 2026.

The announcement comes shortly after the release of the [Subclass 189 - 4 June 2026 invitation round results](https://www.growmore.one/news/subclass-189-invitation-round-data-for-4-june-2026-is-now-available/), which issued 10,000 invitations among skilled workers.

Applicants are encouraged to ensure their Expression of Interest (EOI) is up to date and that all information submitted through SkillSelect remains accurate to maximise their chances in the upcoming round.

## What Applicants Should Do Before the Next Invitation Round

**With the next round expected by the end of September, applicants are recommended to:**

* Review and update their Expression of Interest.  
* Ensure English language test results remain valid.  
* Update any new skilled employment experience.  
* Check the validity of their skills assessment.  
* Verify that all claimed points can be supported by evidence.  
* Consider alternative pathways, such as state nomination or employer-sponsored visa, if their occupation remains highly competitive.  

## Ready for the Next Subclass 189 Invitation Round?

Based on recent invitation round trends, applicants in healthcare and trade occupations may continue to receive invitations at comparatively lower points scores in the upcoming round due to ongoing workforce shortages in these sectors. In contrast, some highly competitive and specialised occupations may require substantially higher points scores to secure an invitation.

As competition for invitations continues, maintaining an accurate and competitive EOI is more important than ever. Even small improvements in English language scores, skilled work experience, partner points, or state nomination options can significantly strengthen an applicant's chances of receiving an invitation.
