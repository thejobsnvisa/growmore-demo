Another month of waiting, another change in expectations. For thousands of skilled migrants hoping to secure Australia PR through the Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189), the latest update from the Department of Home Affairs brings more disappointment than clarity.

The Department’s website had previously stated that the next Subclass 189 invitation round was expected by 30 September 2026. So, applicants were waiting for an invitation and hoping to hear some good news by the end of September. However, September ended without a new invitation round.

Now, on 1 October 2026, the Department has updated its website again, stating that the next invitation round is expected to be held by 31 October 2026.

For applicants who have spent months waiting, this means another period of uncertainty. Many have invested time and money in skills assessments, English language tests and preparing their Expressions of Interest (EOIs). Despite this preparation, they still do not have a confirmed date for the next invitation round.

## What Does the October 2026 Update Mean for Subclass 189 Applicants?

The updated timeline means that skilled migrants waiting for an invitation will need to wait longer for the next round. However, the Department’s expected date of 31 October 2026 should not be treated as a confirmed invitation date.

For applicants who have already submitted their Expression of Interest (EOI), the key concern remains the same: when will invitations be issued, and what points score will be required?

At this stage, the expected date alone does not confirm how many invitations will be issued, which occupations will receive invitations or what the minimum points scores will be. These details will depend on the actual invitation round and the candidates selected.

## What About Subclass 190 and 491 Applicants?

The uncertainty is not limited to Subclass 189 applicants. Those waiting for Subclass 190 and 491 nominations are also seeking updates from the Department, as many states and territories are still awaiting nomination allocations for 2026–27. This leaves many skilled migrants unsure about when they might get an opportunity to move closer to Australia PR.

With invitation timelines changing and nomination updates still awaited, the Department needs to provide clearer and more timely communication to help skilled migrants plan their future. Without greater clarity, applicants are left waiting and uncertain about when they may get their opportunity to pursue Australia PR.

## What Should You Do While Waiting for a Subclass 189 Invitation?

While applicants cannot control the timing of invitation rounds, they can take practical steps to ensure they are prepared.

- **Review your EOI:** Check that your qualifications, employment history, English test results and other claimed points are accurate.
- **Reassess your points score:** Confirm that you meet the requirements for each points claim and retain the supporting evidence.
- **Check your skills assessment:** Ensure it is appropriate for your nominated occupation and remains valid where required.
- **Keep your documents ready:** Organise employment references, qualification documents, English test results and other relevant evidence.
- **Explore other migration pathways:** Depending on your occupation and circumstances, Subclass 190, Subclass 491 or an eligible employer-sponsored visa may be worth considering.
