Many skilled migrants hoping to secure Australia PR through state nomination are still waiting for clarity on when invitation rounds will begin and how many nomination places each state and territory will receive for the 2026–27 program year.

Amid this uncertainty, the Australian Capital Territory (ACT) has announced tentative plans to begin its 2026–27 skilled migration invitation rounds in October.

According to an update, the ACT has scheduled upcoming invitation rounds to maintain continuity while awaiting confirmation of its final nomination allocation. However, the proposed schedule may change once the allocation is finalised.

## ACT Invitation Round Scheduled for October 2026

The first ACT invitation round of the 2026–27 program year is scheduled for the week commencing **12 October 2026**.

The ACT has also tentatively planned further invitation rounds for the remainder of the program year. However, the dates remain subject to change, as the territory has not yet received confirmation of its final nomination allocation.

For skilled migrants considering ACT nomination through the **Skilled Nominated visa (Subclass 190)** or **Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (Subclass 491)**, this announcement provides an indicative timeline for the first round. It does not confirm how many invitations will be issued or which applicants will be selected.

## ACT Nomination Allocation for 2026–27 Still Pending

The ACT has confirmed that its final allocation of nomination places for the **2026–27 program year** has not yet been finalised.

The uncertainty surrounding the allocation has also delayed the finalisation of the ACT Nominated Migration Program occupation list for the new financial year.

Although the territory is planning invitation rounds in advance, applicants should not assume that the previous year's nomination numbers, occupation eligibility or selection arrangements will remain unchanged.

Further details about the number of places available for **Subclass 190** and **Subclass 491** are expected once the allocation is confirmed.

## ACT Canberra Matrix Submission Rules Clarified

Alongside the invitation round update, the ACT has made minor changes to its nomination guidelines following feedback from migration agents. The changes clarify how many Canberra Matrix submissions an applicant can hold at one time.

Under the clarified guidelines, applicants can submit up to **two Canberra Matrix submissions per nominated occupation across a maximum of four occupations**, regardless of the nomination pathway selected.

For example, an applicant may hold two active Canberra Matrix submissions for the same occupation, whether they are applying through **Subclass 190, Subclass 491, or a combination of both pathways**.

This clarification is particularly relevant to applicants considering different ACT nomination pathways for their nominated occupations. However, the ability to hold multiple submissions does not guarantee an invitation or nomination.

## ACT Migration Application Portal Continues to Improve

The ACT Migration Application Portal has been operational since **30 July 2026**.

According to the update, its implementation went smoothly overall, although migration agents reported some issues and requested improvements to certain features.

The ACT has acknowledged this feedback and confirmed that it will continue to improve the portal through iterative updates. It will also monitor the portal's performance and user experience to maintain reliability, security and efficiency.

## Warning About Duplicate Payments for Canberra Matrix Submissions

The ACT has also highlighted instances of duplicate payments for the same Canberra Matrix submission.

After a payment is completed, the submission status in the portal may take several days to change to **"Submitted"**.

According to ACT Migration, this delay is normal and does not necessarily mean that the payment has failed or has not been received.

Applicants and migration agents are therefore advised **not to make another payment** simply because the submission status has not updated.

If there is uncertainty about whether a payment has been received, they should contact ACT Migration to verify the transaction before taking further action. This can help prevent unnecessary duplicate payments.

## ACT Occupation List for 2026–27 Yet to Be Finalised

The ACT has confirmed that its review of the **Nominated Migration Program occupation list for the 2026–27 financial year** is in its final stages.

However, the lack of clarity surrounding the final nomination allocation has delayed the publication of the updated list.

Until the new list and associated guidelines are confirmed, skilled migrants should avoid assuming that their occupations will remain eligible under the same conditions as the previous program year.
