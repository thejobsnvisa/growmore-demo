The Australian Government has confirmed that the National Planning Level (NPL) for international student commencements will remain at 295,000 places in 2027. This is the same intake level as in 2026, reflecting the government's commitment to supporting sustainable growth in Australia's international education sector.

Alongside confirming the student cap, the government also announced a 25% increase in Student Visa and Temporary Graduate Visa application charges from 2027, which is one of the most significant updates for prospective students planning to study in Australia.

## What Is the 2027 National Planning Level?

The National Planning Level (NPL) is the Australian Government's annual target for the number of new international student commencements across higher education institutions.

For 2027, the government has decided to keep the planning level at 295,000 student commencements, which is the same as in 2026. According to the joint announcement, this figure remains 8% below the immediate post-COVID peak.

## Australian Tertiary Education Commission to Manage Student Allocations

A significant administrative change will also take effect from 2027. The newly established Australian Tertiary Education Commission (ATEC) will become responsible for allocating international student places among individual higher education providers. However, the Australian Government will continue to determine the overall national planning level each year.

The change will help allocate student places more evenly without reducing the government’s oversight of international student numbers.

## Student Visa and Temporary Graduate Visa Fees to Increase by 25%

**One of the biggest announcements is the planned 25% increase in application charges for both:**

- Student Visa
- Temporary Graduate Visa

So, students who are planning to study or remain in Australia after graduation should be prepared for these higher government fees when allocating their budget.

## Lower Visa Fees for Selected Student Groups

While visa charges will generally increase, the government confirmed that reduced visa application fees will **apply to certain categories of students, including:**

- Students from ASEAN countries
- Standalone English Language Intensive Courses for Overseas Students (ELICOS)
- Non-award course students
- Existing concessional arrangements for students from Pacific nations and Timor-Leste

These concessions are designed to support Australia's regional education partnerships while maintaining access for priority student groups.

## Priority Visa Processing Will Continue

**The media release also confirmed that priority visa processing will remain in place during 2027 for:**

- Students from Pacific nations and Timor-Leste
- Australian Government scholarship recipients

For the Vocational Education and Training (VET) sector, the government will continue managing growth through visa processing and integrity measures rather than introducing a fixed allocation of student places.
