The wait is over for applicants hoping to secure Tasmania state nomination in 2026–27 . The state has officially started its weekly invitation rounds for the new program year, with the first round now completed on 20 August 2026.

Tasmania issued a total of 93 invitations in this opening round covering both subclass 190 and subclass 491. The state has provided data on the invitations issued, points scores, ROIs on hand and nomination places available for this program year, which will help you get a clearer picture of the state’s current nomination activity.

Here is the full update:

## Tasmania 2026–27 First Invitation Round Results – 20 August 2026

| Visa Subclass | Invitations Issued | Invitation Breakdown | Lowest Score Invited |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subclass 190** | 67 | 65 Gold, 2 Green | 580 points |
| **Subclass 491** | 26 | 3 Gold, 23 further invitations | 97 points |
| **Total** | **93** | **-** | **-** |

The first round shows that Gold Pass candidates continued to receive strong priority, particularly under Subclass 190, where 65 of the 67 invitations were issued to Gold ROIs.

Tasmania has said that the number of additional Subclass 190 invitations was limited because there are many Gold Pass ROIs in the system right now . The state believes this number will drop in the next few weeks, which could allow it to issue more invitations in future rounds.

However, for the upcoming Tasmania invitation round, outcomes may change based on the ROI pool, priority attributes and available nomination places.

## Tasmania 2026–27 Lowest Invitation Scores

The lowest scores invited in the first round were :
* **Subclass 190:** 580 points
* **Subclass 491:** 97 points

Tasmania has clarified that these figures represent a rolling three-month average of the lowest point score resulting in an invitation.

Therefore, applicants should not interpret 580 points for Subclass 190 or 97 points for Subclass 491 as a fixed minimum score for future rounds.

The actual score required can move up or down depending on the number and quality of ROIs, priority attributes, nomination availability and Tasmania's selection criteria at the time of each round.

## Important Warning for Gold Pass ROI Holders

Applicants who have received a Gold Pass should pay particular attention to Tasmania's verification requirements .

Where an ROI is invited, and all priority attributes claimed are verified, the nomination will be approved . However, if one or more claimed priority attributes cannot be verified and the applicant's points score falls below the applicable lowest-score threshold, the application will be declined.

Tasmania has also issued a clear warning against intentionally claiming priority attributes simply to increase an ROI score .

If a candidate is found to have intentionally claimed priority attributes to artificially inflate their score, the application will be declined .

Most importantly, a Gold Pass holder who does not meet the Gold priority attribute may be declined regardless of their total points score .

For applicants, this means it is not enough to simply maximise the points shown in an ROI . Every claim should be genuine, accurate and capable of being verified .
