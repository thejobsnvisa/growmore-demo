After months of waiting, there is finally some positive news from the Department of Home Affairs for skilled migrants who are hoping to secure Australia PR through the Subclass 189 visa.

The Department’s website has now been updated and mentions that the next invitation round is scheduled for 15 October 2026. This gives applicants a clearer idea of when they can expect the next opportunity to receive an invitation and brings some relief to those who have been waiting for months.

Earlier, applicants had been expecting an invitation round in November, but it did not take place as anticipated. Expectations then shifted to October which leaves many skilled migrants waiting for a confirmed update.

But finally, 15 October has now been announced as the next round date, so applicants have a specific timeline to prepare for.

## What Does the Next Subclass 189 Invitation Round Mean for Applicants?

The Subclass 189 Skilled Independent visa is a points-tested permanent visa for eligible skilled workers. One of the key advantages of this visa is that applicants do not need state or territory nomination or employer sponsorship to apply.

However, receiving an invitation depends on more than simply meeting the basic eligibility requirements. Applicants must have a valid Expression of Interest (EOI) in SkillSelect, meet the relevant criteria and have a competitive points score. The number of invitations available and the occupations selected can also influence the outcome.

While the upcoming round is encouraging news, it does not mean every applicant with an EOI will receive an invitation. Meeting the minimum points threshold of 65 is not a guarantee of receiving an invitation.

## What Should You Do Before 15 October?

If you have already submitted your Subclass 189 EOI, this is a good time to review your details and make sure everything is accurate and up to date.

Check your points score, skills assessment, English test results, qualifications and skilled employment claims. If your circumstances have changed, review whether your EOI needs to be updated. Make sure you can provide evidence for the points you have claimed.

Remember, an EOI is not a visa application. If you receive an invitation, you must lodge your Australian visa application within the specified timeframe and provide the required supporting documents. For the Subclass 189 visa, invited applicants generally have 60 days to submit their application through ImmiAccount.

## What If You Do Not Receive an Invitation?

If you do not receive an invitation in this round, it does not necessarily mean your Australia PR plans are over. Depending on your occupation, points score and circumstances, you may also be eligible to explore other pathways, including the Subclass 190 state-nominated visa, the Subclass 491 regional visa or an eligible employer-sponsored visa.

Each pathway has different requirements, so it is important to assess your options based on your individual profile rather than relying on a single visa subclass.
