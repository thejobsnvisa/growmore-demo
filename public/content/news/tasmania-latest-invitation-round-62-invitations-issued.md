As several Australian states and territories remain closed or have not yet commenced their invitation round for 2026–27 State Nomination Program, Tasmania continues to issue regular invitations to eligible skilled candidates. This makes each Tasmanian invitation round an important update for applicants who are considering Australia PR through state nomination. 

The latest invitation round in Tasmania was held on 24 September 2026, and in this round the state has issued a total of 62 invitations across Subclass 190 and Subclass 491.  

Here is the breakdown of this invitation round that provides information about key invitation results, ROI numbers and nomination places. 

## Tasmania Subclass 190 and 491 Invitation Results 

The latest round shows that Tasmania continues to use its priority attributes and nomination criteria when deciding which ROIs progress to nomination. For Subclass 190, the invitations included 20 Gold and 18 Green invitations, while all 24 Subclass 491 invitations were listed as further invitations. 

| Visa Subclass | Invitations Issued | Invitation Type | Lowest Invited Score |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Subclass 190 | 38 | 20 Gold, 18 Green | 352 points |
| Subclass 491 | 24 | 24 Further | 72 points |

## Tasmania ROIs, Nomination Places and Score Changes 

The latest Tasmania invitation round also gives applicants an indication of the current competition and nomination capacity across Subclass 190 and Subclass 491. 

The lowest Subclass 190 invitation score has continued to decline over the past few invitation rounds, falling from 375 points in the previous round to 352 points in the latest round. Meanwhile, the lowest Subclass 491 invitation score remained unchanged at 72 points from the previous round.  

The lower Subclass 491 score also shows that the regional pathway can be a more accessible option for applicants with lower points if you meet the relevant visa and Tasmania nomination requirements. 

| Tasmania nomination position | Subclass 190 | Subclass 491 |
|---|---:|---:|
| ROIs on hand | 905 | 593 |
| Nomination places available | 992 | 712 |
| Nomination applications lodged but not yet decided | 41 | 37 |
| Invitations issued but application not yet lodged | 76 | 63 |

| Lowest score at assessment, rolling 3-month average | 478 points | 81 points |

 

The rolling three-month lowest score also decreased for both streams. For Subclass 190, it fell from 504 to 478 points, while the Subclass 491 figure decreased from 83 to 81 points. These figures should be viewed as a reference from recent invitation activity and not as fixed scores for future invitation rounds. 

Tasmania has also reported that the oldest nomination application lodged but not yet allocated to a case officer was lodged on 17 September 2026. 

## What Applicants Should Consider for Tasmania Nomination 

Applicants with a Tasmania ROI should make sure the information provided in their ROI is accurate and can be supported with evidence if requested during the nomination assessment process. 

It is also worth checking whether there have been any changes to their employment, occupation, English proficiency, skills assessment or other relevant circumstances since submitting the ROI. These changes could affect their nomination eligibility or overall migration strategy. 

For those who already have an ROI in the system, reviewing the information now can help ensure that the claims made in the ROI are still accurate and that the relevant supporting evidence is available if required.
