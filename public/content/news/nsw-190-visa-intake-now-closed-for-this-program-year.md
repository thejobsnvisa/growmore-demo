If you were planning to secure a NSW nomination under the subclass 190 visa, then the window has officially closed for this program year. The state has confirmed that it has reached its full nomination allocation for this visa category, which means no further invitations will be issued under the subclass 190 pathway for the 2025–26 migration program year.

For applicants targeting NSW, the primary nomination pathway that currently remains available is the Skilled Work Regional visa (subclass 491) – Pathway 2.

## NSW Reaches Full Allocation for Subclass 190 Visa

According to the latest NSW update, the state has already invited enough EOIs to fully utilise its subclass 190 nomination allocation for this program year.

This means:

- No further NSW subclass 190 invitation rounds are expected this year

- EOIs already submitted in SkillSelect will remain active, but new invitations are unlikely

- Any new nomination opportunities will now depend on the 2026–27 allocation provided by the Australian Government

However, this update does not impact applicants who have already received an invitation or lodged their NSW nomination application.

## Subclass 491 – Pathway 2: Invitations Still Open

While the NSW subclass 190 program has now reached its allocation limit, opportunities are still available under the Skilled Work Regional visa (subclass 491) – Pathway 2.

NSW recently conducted another invitation round for this pathway, and the state has confirmed that EOIs will continue to be considered until the remaining nomination places are filled. However, competition is expected to increase as the remaining quota becomes more limited.

For applicants currently in the SkillSelect EOI pool, the subclass 491 visa still offers a pathway to NSW nomination for those who are willing to live and work in regional areas.
