If you are planning to obtain a VETASSESS skills assessment for Australian migration, don’t wait too long. From 9 September 2026, VETASSESS will increase fees for Professional Skills Assessments. The fee increase will also affect Priority Processing, Skills Assessment Support, Chinese Qualifications Verification, non-migration skills assessments and several administration services.

The new fees have been updated to reflect inflation and follow Australian Government guidance. However, there is one important exception: **the fee increase does not apply to skills assessments for trade occupations.**

Applicants should also note that the new fees will apply based on when the application is received. Applications received before 9 September 2026 will be charged at the current rates.

## **VETASSESS Professional Skills Assessment Fees: Current vs New**

The main Full Professional Skills Assessment fee will increase from $1,096 to $1,146, which means a rise of $50 before GST. Here are the key changes:

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Service** | **Current Fee** | **New Fee from 9 Sep 2026** |
| Full Skills Assessment – including DAMA & ILA | $1,096 | **$1,146** |
| Priority Processing – supplementary fee | $825 | **$863** |
| Points Test Advice – non-VETASSESS occupations | $311 | **$325** |
| Post-485 Assessment – same occupation | $852 | **$891** |
| Reassessment – within 12 months | $745 | **$779** |
| Appeal – within 90 days | $1,082 | **$1,132** |
| Renewal of Skills Assessment – within 3 years | $473 | **$495** |
| Post-Vocational Education Work Visa (Subclass 485) Review | $416 | **$436** |
| Review – Post-Vocational Education Work Visa | $265 | **$277** |
| Reassessment – Post-Vocational Education Work Visa | $379 | **$396** |
