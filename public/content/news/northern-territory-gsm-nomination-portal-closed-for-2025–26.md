## **Current Status of NT GSM Nominations**

The nomination portal for the Northern Territory is no longer accepting new GSM applications. This means that only applications submitted before the deadline will be processed further for assessment, with final outcomes expected by 30 June 2026.

### **Are There Any Exceptions?**

If you think about any exceptions, then yes, there is some exception, but they are limited, time-sensitive, and not guaranteed.

The NT Government has stated that new applications will only be accepted for candidates who:

- Meet NT Graduate or NT Resident eligibility criteria, and

- Have one of the following visa deadlines **falling before 31 December 2026:**

> 1. Applicants who have visas **expiring before 31 December 2026 and are not eligible** for a Temporary Graduate visa;
>
> 2. Applicants who will **turn 45 years of age before 31 December 2026**, making them ineligible for GSM;
>
> 3. Applicants whose **birthday falls before December 31, 2026,** will cause their subclass 491 points to drop below the required 65.
