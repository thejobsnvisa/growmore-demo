## NSW Subclass 491 Pathway 1 & Pathway 3 Closed on the Same Day Due to Strong Demand

If you were preparing to apply for NSW Subclass 491 under Pathway 1 or Pathway 3, this is a critical update: **both pathways have now reached full allocation and are closed for the 2025–26 program year due to overwhelming demand.**

## **What Happened**

- Subclass 491 Pathway 1 opened for skilled workers employed in regional NSW

- Subclass 491 Pathway 3 opened for recent graduates from regional NSW institutions

- Demand exceeded available nomination places almost immediately

- NSW closed both Pathway 1 and Pathway 3 to new applications on the same day

- Applications already submitted will continue to be processed

This confirms that NSW regional nomination has now become one of the most competitive pathways in Australia.

## **Processing Time Update**

- Due to the exceptionally high number of lodged applications, NSW has indicated that **standard processing timelines of approximately six weeks may be extended**. Applicants with urgent circumstances may request expedited assessment if:

- Their current visa or their partner’s visa is close to expiry

- English test results, skills assessments, or passports are expiring

- They are about to lose points due to age, dropping below the minimum requirement

Eligible applicants can contact Investment NSW directly via [skilled.migration@investment.nsw.gov.au](mailto:skilled.migration@investment.nsw.gov.au). to request priority consideration.

## **What This Means for Skilled Migrants**

The same-day closure of both pathways sends a clear message:

- Meeting eligibility criteria alone is no longer sufficient

- Timing is very critical to securing a nomination

- Applications must be fully prepared before pathways open

- Delays, missing documents, or incomplete planning can result in lost opportunities
