## What Is the Subclass 190 Eligibility Requirement for the 2026–27 Program Year?

To be considered for NSW nomination, applicants must meet the Department of Home Affairs visa requirements and the specific eligibility criteria published by the NSW Government. Here are the key requirements applicants should understand before preparing their applications.

### Meet the Department of Home Affairs Visa Requirements

First, applicants must meet the applicable subclass 190 visa requirements set by the Department of Home Affairs. These generally include being under 45 years of age when invited to apply, meeting the English language requirements, satisfying the points test and meeting the other relevant visa criteria.

Applicants must hold a valid skills assessment for their nominated occupation. The occupation must meet both of the following conditions:

### Have a Valid Skills Assessment

- It must be included on the eligible skilled occupation list for the subclass 190 visa.
- It must fall within an ANZSCO unit group identified on the NSW Skills List.

This means applicants should check both the NSW Skills List and the relevant occupation list published by the Department of Home Affairs. Not every occupation within an eligible ANZSCO unit group automatically qualifies for the subclass 190 visa.

### Meet the NSW Residency or Employment Requirements

Applicants must satisfy one of the following criteria, depending on their circumstances:

| Applicant category | Eligibility requirement |
|---|---|
| Onshore applicants | Work in NSW in the nominated occupation for remuneration for at least 20 hours per week; or |
| Onshore applicants | Have continuously resided in NSW for at least six months; or |
| Offshore applicants | Have continuously resided outside Australia for at least six months. |

Applicants should ensure their usual country of residence is accurately recorded in their SkillSelect Expression of Interest (EOI), as NSW uses this information to determine whether an applicant is considered onshore or offshore.

## How Does the NSW Subclass 190 Invitation Process Work?

NSW follows a selection-based invitation process, meaning applicants cannot directly apply for state nomination without first receiving an invitation.

The process generally involves these steps:

1. **Check your eligibility:** Confirm that your occupation, skills assessment and personal circumstances meet the applicable criteria.
2. **Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI):** Lodge an EOI through SkillSelect with accurate information about your qualifications, work experience, English proficiency and points claims.
3. **Wait for an invitation:** NSW selects candidates based on factors that may include age, English proficiency, education, points score and skilled work experience. Submitting an EOI does not guarantee an invitation.
4. **Submit your nomination application:** If invited, provide supporting documents within the specified timeframe. NSW's published process gives applicants 14 days to respond to a nomination invitation.
5. **Apply for the visa:** If nominated and subsequently invited by the Department of Home Affairs, lodge your subclass 190 visa application and satisfy the relevant visa requirements.
