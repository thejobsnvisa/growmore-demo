The Department of Home Affairs has officially released the results of the latest SkillSelect invitation round held on 4 June 2026. This data provides valuable insights for skilled migration applicants who are aiming to secure permanent residency through the Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189).

The latest round saw 10,000 invitations issued under the [Subclass 189 visa program](https://www.growmore.one/news/is-your-eoi-ready-subclass-189-invitation-round-confirmed-for-4-june-2026/), matching the number issued in the November 2025 round. The results confirm continued demand for skilled professionals across healthcare, education, trades, engineering, science, and other priority sectors.

## Key Takeaways from the June 2026 Invitation Round

The June 2026 Subclass 189 invitation round once again shows where Australia's skilled migration priorities currently lie. With a total of 10,000 invitations issued, the points required to receive an invitation varied significantly across occupations depending on demand.

One of the most noticeable trends in this round was the continued need for skilled trades workers to support Australia's construction, housing, and infrastructure projects. Occupations such as Carpenters, Bricklayers, Electricians, Plumbers, Roof Plumbers, Wall and Floor Tilers, and Stonemasons received invitations with a minimum threshold of 65 points.

Healthcare professionals such as Registered Nurses, Physiotherapists, Occupational Therapists, Social Workers, Midwives, and Medical Practitioners also continued to get an invitation at a competitive score due to strong demand.

On the other hand, specialised occupations required much higher scores to secure an invitation. Urologists recorded the highest invitation threshold at 100 points.

Occupations such as Biotechnologists, Electronics Engineers, Telecommunications Engineers, Marine Biologists, Microbiologists, Multimedia Specialists, and Thoracic Medicine Specialists require at least 95 points, which indicates strong competition among applicants in these fields.

The tie-break date for the 4 June 2026 invitation round was 24 April 2026. This means that when multiple applicants had the same points score, invitations were issued to those who submitted their Expression of Interest (EOI) before this date.
