The South Australian Skilled Migration State Nomination Program has reached its final stage for the 2025–26 program year. Registrations of Interest (ROIs) closed at 9:00 AM on 2 June 2026, marking the end of new submissions under the current intake cycle.

With the last formal invitation round already conducted on 21 May 2026, the program has now moved into its concluding phase for the current program year.

So, applicants who are targeting [Australia PR](https://www.growmore.one/) through SA’s state nomination, this marks the end of new consideration under the 2025–26 intake cycle.

## Program Status and Final Round Activity

The state has confirmed that the 21 May 2026 round was the last scheduled invitation round for both Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 under this program year.

However, limited activity may still occur to manage remaining nomination places; no further structured invitation rounds are expected for this program year. The state is currently focusing on finalising assessments within the remaining allocation rather than opening new opportunities.

## What Happens After ROI Closure

With ROI submissions now closed, Skilled and Business Migration (SBM) will no longer accept new applications under the 2025–26 program year.

Applicants who were not invited or did not submit an ROI will need to wait until the next cycle. SBM has confirmed that ROI submissions will reopen once the 2026–27 program commences, along with updated program criteria and allocation details.

Existing applications will continue to be processed, but approvals will depend on the availability of remaining nomination places for this program year.

## What This Means for Applicants Moving Forward

With the closure of ROIs for 2025–26, South Australia has effectively paused new nomination intake for [Subclass 190 and Subclass 491](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/difference-between-subclass-491-and-subclass-190/) for this cycle. Applicants aiming for Australia PR should now expect no further entry opportunities under the current program year.

For applicants planning Australia PR, this signals the need to explore alternative migration pathways rather than relying solely on South Australia nomination. Options may include other state nomination programs, employer-sponsored visas, or regional pathways.

To understand the most suitable route for your profile and improve your chances of selection, it is advisable to consult a MARA registered migration agent (MARA No. 2217941) for personalised guidance on your Australia PR strategy.
