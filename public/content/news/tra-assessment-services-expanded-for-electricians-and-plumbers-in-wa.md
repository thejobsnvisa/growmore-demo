Electricians and plumbers who are looking to have their overseas trade skills recognised in Australia have another assessment option available in Western Australia. 

North Metropolitan TAFE has expanded its service delivery to conduct Trades Recognition Australia (TRA) skills assessments in Western Australia for Electrician (General), Electrician (Special Class) and Plumber (General) occupations. As a TRA-approved Registered Training Organisation (RTO), it will provide eligible tradespeople with access to these assessment services locally in WA. 

The expanded service provides eligible tradespeople with local access to TRA assessment programs for skilled migration visa applications through the Department of Home Affairs. 

## Support Beyond the Skills Assessment 

North Metropolitan TAFE's support extends beyond the TRA skills assessment stage, helping eligible tradespeople progress towards employment and licensing in Western Australia. 

This includes gap training for licensing pathways once candidates have secured employment and the required provisional licences. Candidates can also access free support through Jobs and Skills Centres, including: 

* CV preparation and review 
* Job search assistance 
* Job application support 
* Interview preparation 

This provides skilled migrants with access to support across different stages of their transition into the Western Australian workforce. 

## Local Assessment Pathway for Eligible Overseas-Trained Electricians 

North Metropolitan TAFE also provides a local assessment pathway for overseas-trained electricians who already hold Australian permanent residency and need skills recognition for employment and licensing purposes. 

However, it is important to note that for electricians and plumbers, the TRA skills assessment is only one part of the broader process. Depending on the occupation and circumstances, applicants may also need to meet Australian licensing requirements before they can work in their trade. 

For applicants pursuing Australia skilled migration, understanding the difference between TRA skills assessment, visa eligibility and occupational licensing is necessary because only meeting the skills assessment requirement does not automatically provide a licence to work in Australia.
