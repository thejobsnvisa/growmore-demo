The Australian Department of Home Affairs will cease immigration operations at the Australian Embassy in Seoul from 30 June 2026, according to a notice issued to registered migration agents by the Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA).

However, this administrative change is not expected to affect visa applicants or the processing of Australian visa applications, as applications will continue to be lodged online through Australia's digital visa systems.

## What Is Changing?

From 30 June 2026, the Department of Home Affairs will no longer maintain immigration operations at the Australian Embassy in Seoul. 

This means immigration-related functions previously associated with the Seoul office will be managed through the Department's broader global immigration network. The Department has also updated its official website to reflect the change.

## Will This Affect Australian Visa Applicants?

For most applicants, the answer is no.

Australia's visa application process is largely digital, with applications submitted online through the Department of Home Affairs' electronic platforms. Applicants are generally not required to visit an Australian embassy or overseas mission in person to lodge a visa application.

As a result, the closure of immigration operations at the Australian Embassy in Seoul is expected to have minimal impact on individuals who are relying on this embassy for [Australian visa-related services](https://www.growmore.one/), including applications for student visas, [skilled visas](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/individual/general-skilled-visa/), employer-sponsored visas, partner visas, visitor visas, and permanent residency pathways.

## Why Is Home Affairs Closing Immigration Operations in Seoul?

The change reflects the Department of Home Affairs' continued move towards digital visa services and centralised processing. As visa applications are lodged online and processed through a global network, immigration operations are no longer tied to a specific embassy location.

## What Applicants Need to Know

For students, skilled workers, families, and other migrants, the closure of immigration operations in Seoul does not affect any existing visa pathways or migration opportunities. 

Visa applications will continue to be lodged online, visa requirements and eligibility criteria remain unchanged, and applications will continue to be processed through Home Affairs' global immigration network. In practical terms, applicants do not need to take any additional action as a result of this announcement.
