Australia's Family visa processing priorities have changed effective **25 July 2026**. As part of three new Ministerial Directions introduced by the Australian Government, **Ministerial Direction 117 (MD 117)** introduces a revised framework for prioritising Family visa applications.

If you are planning to reunite with your loved ones in Australia, this update is critical to understand. The new Direction introduces a restructured priority framework for Family visa processing by factoring in whether the applicant is onshore or offshore and whether the visa can be granted while the applicant remains in Australia.

Here is the full information about which Family visas receive priority and how the new processing order works. 

## What is Ministerial Direction 117?

Ministerial Direction 117 is a formal direction issued by the Minister under **section 499 of the *Migration Act 1958***. It replaces Ministerial Direction 102 and sets out how the Department of Home Affairs must prioritise the consideration and finalisation of Family visa applications.

Importantly, this Direction does not create new visa requirements or guarantee faster processing times. Instead, it determines the order in which applications are generally considered by the Department. This applies to both new Family visa applications and applications that were not finalised before 25 July 2026. 

This means pending applications will now be processed according to the new priority rules. 

## What Has Changed Under Ministerial Direction 117? 

The most significant modification is the introduction of a **two-tiered structural grouping** prior to applying visa category priorities. 

Previously, Family visa applications were prioritised based solely on visa categories. Under MD 117, applications are first divided into two distinct groups based on location and grant eligibility:

### Group 1: Applications Eligible for Onshore Grant
Applications meeting **both** of the following criteria:
1. The primary applicant is physically in Australia at the time of application.
2. The visa can be lawfully granted while the applicant remains in Australia.

### Group 2: Applications Requiring Offshore Grant
Applications where:
1. The primary applicant is outside Australia when applying, **OR**
2. The visa framework requires the applicant to be outside Australia at the time of visa grant.

After this initial separation, the existing Family visa processing order is applied within each group. 

This means some onshore applicants could benefit from earlier consideration compared with offshore applicants applying under the same visa category. 

## How Does Australia's New Family Visa Processing Priority Order Work? 
After considering whether an applicant is eligible for an onshore grant or requires an offshore grant, applications are prioritised in the following order: 

First Priority 

It is given to applications where the Minister has substituted a decision of the Administrative Review Tribunal or its predecessor under sections 351 and 417 of the Migration Act. 

Second Priority 

It is given to applications where the primary applicant is a spouse, a de facto partner, a prospective spouse, or a dependent child of the sponsor or proposed sponsor. This category includes many Partner and Child visa applications, which remain among the highest priority Family visas. 

Third Priority 

It is given to applications where the primary applicant is an orphan relative of the sponsor or proposed sponsor. 

Fourth Priority 

It is mainly given for Contributory Parent visas, Contributory Aged Parent visas, Temporary Contributory Parent visas, and Temporary Contributory Aged Parent visas. 

Fifth Priority 

It is given to applications where the primary applicant is applying as a Carer. 

Sixth Priority 

It is given for applications including Parent (Class AX) & Aged Parent (Class BP) visas and applications on the basis of the primary applicant being a remaining relative or an aged dependent relative. 

Here, the main thing is that parent and other family visa categories continue to receive lower processing priority under the new Direction as well
