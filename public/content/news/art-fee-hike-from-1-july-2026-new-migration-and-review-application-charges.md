The Administrative Review Tribunal (ART) will increase application fees from 1 July 2026, which affects key review areas including migration, protection, and taxation matters. These updates will result in higher costs for most applicants compared to current charges.

So, if you are planning to lodge a review, it may be worth acting before the new fees apply, as the increase could add a noticeable amount depending on the type of application.

## **What Is Changing in ART Fees?**

From 1 July 2026, ART will apply revised fees across several review streams under its existing legislative framework. The changes primarily impact:

- Migration review applications
- Protection visa review decisions
- Standard tribunal applications
- Small business and taxation-related reviews
- Guidance and Appeals Panel matters
- Future act determination applications

## **ART Application Fees: Old vs New (Effective 1 July 2026)**

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Application Type** | **Current Fees (Until 30 June 2026)** | **New Fees (From 1 July 2026)** |
| Standard application fee | $1,148 | $1,195 |
| Small business taxation decision | $616 | $641 |
| Certain taxation decisions (low-value disputes) | $114 | $119 |
| Review of migration decision | $3,580 | $3,727 |
| Review of protection decision | $2,203 | $2,293 |
| Guidance & Appeals Panel application | $1,148 | $1,195 |

## **Key Highlights of the ART Fee Increase**

### **Migration reviews become more expensive:** The fee rises to $3,727, up from $3,580, affecting skilled visa refusals and migration appeals.

### **Protection visa reviews also increased:** Applicants will now pay $2,293 instead of $2,203, which increases upfront financial exposure even though fees are often only charged on unsuccessful outcomes.

### **Gradual increases across other categories:**

* Standard applications: $1,195 (up from $1,148)

* Small business taxation: $641 (up from $616)

* Low-value tax disputes: $119 (up from $114)
