## Tasmania April Weekly Round: Subclass 190 & 491 Invitations Released

Tasmania’s weekly state nomination round for April has officially begun, with the state releasing invitations for skilled migrants under both Subclass 190 (State Nominated) and Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional) visas.

In this round, the state invited an equal number of applicants for both pathways, which was 25 ROIs each. It highlights the state’s balanced focus on metropolitan and regional skilled migration.

## **2nd April Tasmania Invitation Round Results at a Glance**

In this round, a total of **50 invitations** were issued to attract skilled migrants under the skilled migration program.

## **Key Insights From this Round**

- Subclass 190 is Highly Competitive, in which 25 invitations were issued in this round, and only Gold Pass holders were invited.
- Tasmania continues to invite Green Pass applicants under Subclass 491, and the minimum points to get an invitation are comparatively lower than in the previous round.
- Nomination places are filling quickly due to high competition. So, applicants should keep their ROI updated and be ready to submit a nomination application immediately if invited.
- Profile quality matters more than ever. So, updating English test scores, work experience documentation, and occupation alignment can improve your chances.
