If you are an Indian citizen living in Australia and need to renew your passport, apply for an Indian visa, OCI card, or use any consular services, this latest development could affect you. 

The Delhi High Court has cancelled the tender that gave VFS Global the contract to handle Indian passport, visa and consular services in Australia. The court has asked India's Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) and the High Commission of India in Canberra to start the process again and issue a fresh tender within the next one month. 

Although these services are not being cancelled permanently, the decision has created uncertainty regarding the future delivery of Indian consular services across Australia. 

## What Has Happened? 

The Delhi High Court found that the technical evaluation process used to award the contract to VFS Global was arbitrary, irrational, and lacking transparency. 

The court observed that: 

* Bidders were assessed using undisclosed evaluation standards. 
* Similar proposals received different scores in different countries. 
* Certain deductions in technical scores were not properly explained. 
* The evaluation process breached principles of fairness and transparency. 

As a result, the court has set aside the tender process for Indian consular services in Australia, the UAE, Kuwait, and Singapore. 

This means the Indian government will now have to conduct a new bidding process to select a service provider.

## What Services Are Affected in Australia? 

The contract covers outsourced Indian Consular, Passport and Visa (CPV) services, including: 

* Indian passport renewals 
* OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) applications 
* Indian visa applications 
* Attestation and miscellaneous consular services 
* Passport surrender services 
* Police Clearance Certificates (PCC) 
* Other consular services provided by Indian missions in Australia 

These services are generally accessed through application centres located in Adelaide, 
Brisbane, Canberra, Melbourne, Perth, and Sydney. 

## Will VFS Global Stop Providing Services Immediately? 

No. 

No doubt, VFS Global had previously announced that Indian consular, passport and visa services in Australia would be temporarily unavailable from July 1, but the Delhi High Court has specifically allowed the existing service provider to continue operating until the fresh tender process is completed. This means Indian applicants should not assume that all services have stopped immediately and permanently. 

However, there may still be: 

* Appointment delays 
* Temporary service interruptions 
* Changes in processing arrangements 
* Updates regarding service centres and application procedures 

The court's order aims to avoid inconvenience for applicants while the new tender process takes place.

If you are planning to:

You should stay updated on official announcements before booking appointments or making travel plans. Applicants may experience temporary disruptions while the Indian government completes the fresh tender process. 

So, if your travel plans are time-sensitive, it is advisable to apply as early as possible and regularly check service updates. 


## Will This Affect Australian Visa Applications? 

No. 

This development only relates to the Indian government's consular and passport services outsourced in Australia. 

Australian immigration applications remain unaffected. This means no effect on Australia PR applications, Employer-sponsored visas, Skilled visas, Student visas, Visitor visas, or Partner visas, which are handled by the Australian Department of Home Affairs.
