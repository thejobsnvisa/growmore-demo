Paying the Australian visa application charge is set to become a little less expensive for applicants who use a card. From **1 October 2026**, the Department of Home Affairs will stop charging the additional card payment surcharge following changes to Australia's payment surcharge rules.

The update means applicants will no longer have to account for this extra charge when making eligible card payments. However, the **Visa Application Charge (VAC) will remain unchanged**, so the saving will apply only to the additional surcharge.

## What Is Changing from 1 October 2026?

The **Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA)** has confirmed that surcharging on debit, prepaid and credit card payments across the **eftpos, Mastercard and Visa networks** will be banned from **1 October 2026**. The change follows its review of merchant card payment costs and surcharging.

The **Migration Institute of Australia (MIA)** contacted the Department of Home Affairs to clarify how these changes would affect visa payments. The Department subsequently confirmed that it would stop charging the card payment surcharge from the same date.

For visa applicants, this means the additional surcharge currently applied to eligible card payments will no longer be charged by the Department from **1 October 2026**.

## How Does This Affect Australian Visa Applicants?

The change will be relevant to people paying visa application charges through **ImmiAccount**, where card payments are commonly used. It can be relevant to payments involving sponsorship or nomination fees, depending on the payment method and applicable payment arrangements.

From **1 October 2026**, eligible card payments will no longer attract the additional surcharge charged by the Department. However, the **visa application charge itself will remain unchanged**.

## What Were the Existing Card Payment Surcharge Rates?

The Department's published payment information has listed the following surcharge rates for visa application charges, sponsorship fees and nomination fees before the change takes effect:

| Payment Method   | Published Surcharge Rate |
| ---------------- | -----------------------: |
| American Express |                    1.40% |
| JCB              |                    1.40% |
| Mastercard       |                    1.40% |
| PayPal           |                    1.01% |
| UnionPay         |                    1.90% |
| Visa             |                    1.40% |

## What Should Applicants Do Before Paying Their Visa Fees?

Applicants should confirm the correct **Visa Application Charge** for their intended visa subclass and check the Department's payment instructions before making a payment.

Anyone who is getting ready to submit an application around **1 October 2026** should also find out which surcharge rules are in place based on when they pay and how they choose to pay.

If you are planning to apply for an Australian visa, check the applicable visa fee, confirm the payment requirements, and review all visa requirements before lodging your application.
