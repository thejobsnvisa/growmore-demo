As the 2025–26 Australian state nomination program year approaches its final weeks, Tasmania has released three major skilled migration updates today related to its state nomination program. 

It includes the closure date for Registrations of Interest (ROIs), confirmation of the final invitation round for the current program year, and the newest weekly invitation round results for Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 pathways.  

So, if you are planning to secure Tasmania state nomination this financial year, the latest update is important as remaining nomination places continue to reduce. 

## 1. Registrations of Interest Closing on 3 June 2026 

Migration Tasmania has officially confirmed that Registrations of Interest (ROIs) for all pathways and passes under the **Tasmanian Skilled Migration State Nomination Program 2025-26 will close at 5:00 PM on Wednesday, 3 June 2026.** 

So, applicants who are still planning to submit an ROI for Tasmania pathways should now prepare urgently, as the submission window is now entering its final days. 

Migration Tasmania has also confirmed that ROIs will reopen once the 2026–27 program officially commences.  

## 2. Final Invitation Round Confirmed for 4 June 2026
 
Tasmania has also announced that the **final invitation round for the 2025–26 program year will take place on Thursday, 4 June 2026.**  

This makes the upcoming rounds one of the valuable opportunities for skilled migration applicants who are looking for a Tasmania nomination this financial year. 

Even though the state will be closed for new ROIs, it will continue processing nomination applications that have already been lodged. Eligible applicants can still submit nomination applications where allowed. However, Tasmania has clearly stated that not all applicants may receive a nomination this program year due to the limited number of nomination places remaining.  

## 3. Tasmania Weekly Invitation Round Results – 21 May 2026 

Alongside the major program announcements, Tasmania has also released the latest weekly invitation round figures for 21 May 2026, in which a total of **57 ROIs were invited across Subclass 190 and Subclass 491.**
