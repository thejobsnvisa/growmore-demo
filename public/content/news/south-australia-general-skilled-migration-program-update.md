South Australia is now open for Registrations of Interest (ROIs) for individuals living and working in the state whose current visas are due to expire on or before **31 January 2027**.

After recent updates from NSW, Victoria and the ACT, along with the latest Subclass 189 invitation round, South Australia brings another important update for skilled migrants. However, this ROI opening is specifically for eligible onshore applicants, while offshore candidates may be considered through their active SkillSelect EOI.

So, let’s look at the eligibility requirements, available nomination streams and application process for South Australia’s 2026–27 skilled migration program.

## Who Can Apply for South Australia’s 2026–27 ROI?

The current ROI opening targets skilled migrants who are living and working in South Australia and whose current visas expire on or before **31 January 2027**.

If you are already employed in South Australia and planning to apply for state nomination, you should review the requirements for your nominated occupation and the relevant nomination stream before submitting an ROI.

All applicants, including onshore and offshore candidates, must have a current Expression of Interest (EOI) lodged in SkillSelect.

## South Australia’s Priority Industries for 2026–27

For the 2026–27 General Skilled Migration (GSM) program, South Australia will prioritise candidates working in the following industries:

- Building and construction
- Defence
- Education
- Engineering
- Health
- Manufacturing

Candidates with skills and experience in these industries may receive priority consideration based on their occupation eligibility and the relevant nomination requirements.

For the complete list of occupations eligible for South Australia’s skilled migration program, refer to the full South Australian Skilled Occupation List here.

## South Australia’s State Nomination Streams

South Australia offers four main nomination streams for skilled migrants:

1. **South Australian Graduates Stream:** For eligible international graduates who have studied in South Australia and meet the applicable nomination requirements.

2. **Skilled Employment in South Australia Stream:** For eligible skilled workers employed in South Australia who satisfy the relevant employment and occupation criteria.

3. **Outer Regional Skilled Employment Stream:** For eligible skilled workers employed in qualifying outer regional locations in South Australia.

4. **Offshore Stream:** For eligible skilled applicants living overseas who may be considered for South Australian nomination through their SkillSelect EOI.

Eligibility requirements are largely unchanged from the previous program year, but applicants should check the current official criteria for their selected stream before proceeding.

You can check stream-wise requirements here: Skilled Migration: Australian Visas to Work in South Australia.

## South Australia State Nomination Fees

| Application Type | Fee |
|---|---:|
| Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) | AUD $394 |
| Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491) | AUD $394 |
| Registration of Interest (ROI) | Nil |

Submitting an ROI is free. However, the state nomination application fee is separate from the visa application charge payable to the Department of Home Affairs.

## How to Apply for South Australia State Nomination

### Step 1: Check Your Eligibility

Review the Skilled Occupations List, nomination stream requirements and document checklist to confirm that your occupation and circumstances meet the relevant criteria.

### Step 2: Lodge an EOI in SkillSelect

All applicants must have a current EOI. Ensure that your occupation, skills assessment, English language results and points claims are accurate.

### Step 3: Submit an ROI if Applying Onshore

Eligible onshore applicants must submit an ROI through South Australia’s Apply Portal with the required information and documents. Offshore applicants do not need an ROI.

### Step 4: Wait for an Invitation

South Australia will consider suitable candidates from the onshore and offshore cohorts. Selection is not guaranteed.

### Step 5: Apply for State Nomination

If invited, lodge your nomination application through the Apply Portal and provide the mandatory supporting documents.

If nomination is approved, you may proceed with the relevant visa application to the Department of Home Affairs, subject to the applicable requirements.
