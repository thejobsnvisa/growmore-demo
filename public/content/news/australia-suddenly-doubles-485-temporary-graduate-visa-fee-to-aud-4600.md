## **Updated 485 Visa Fees from 1 March 2026**

This fee increase is officially published on the [Australian Government Department of Home Affairs website](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/temporary-graduate-485) and applies to all Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485) applications lodged on or after 1 March 2026.

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **Visa Stream** | **New Main Applicant Visa Lodgement Fee (AUD)** |
| Post-Vocational Education Visa | $4,600 |
| Initial Post-Higher Education Visa | $4,600 |
| Second Post-Higher Education Visa | $1,810 |

In addition to the main applicant fee shown above, extra charges also apply if you include family members such as your spouse or children in the same visa application.

**Applicants should also be prepared for additional mandatory expenses related to the visa process, including:**

- Health examinations
- Police clearance certificates
- Biometrics collection (if required)

However, a lower visa application fee applies to eligible citizens of certain Pacific Island countries and Timor-Leste. To qualify for this, the primary applicant must hold a valid passport issued by one of the following countries:

- Papua New Guinea

## **Why the Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485) is Still Important**

Despite the significant fee increase, the Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485) remains a key visa choice for international students.

- This allows graduates to work full-time and gain valuable practical experience in the Australian job market.
- Graduates can use this period to complete skills assessments and strengthen the profile that helps them in getting an [Australia PR](https://www.growmore.one/australia-permanent-resident-visa-options-2026/) after a student visa through pathways such as Subclass 189, 190, and 491.
- This visa also gives students the opportunity to find an eligible employer and transition to an [employer-sponsored visa](https://www.growmore.one/top-employer-sponsored-visas-everything-you-need-to-know/) pathway.
