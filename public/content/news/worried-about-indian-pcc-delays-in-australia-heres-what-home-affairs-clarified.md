If you are an Indian citizen in Australia and are currently planning to apply for any Australian visa, you may already be aware of the loss of VFS’s contract in Australia that may impact Indian Consular, Passport and Visa services since 1 July 2026. The temporary disruption to Indian consular services in Australia has created uncertainty for many Indian nationals who require important documents for their Australian visa applications.

One of the major concerns arising from this disruption is the availability of the Indian Police Clearance Certificate (PCC), which is a mandatory requirement for many Australian visa applications, including permanent residency, employer-sponsored, partner and some temporary visas.

To address this, the Migration Institute of Australia (MIA) requested clarification from the Department of Home Affairs on how affected visa applications will be assessed during this period, and DHA has now clarified the options available to affected applicants.

Here is what you need to know.

## Can You Request More Time to Submit Your Indian PCC?

Yes.

If you are unable to provide your Indian Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) only because the services remain suspended, you can request an extension of time from the Department of Home Affairs.

The Department has confirmed that applicants may submit a written request explaining the reason for the delay.

If you receive a request for further information from Home Affairs and cannot obtain your PCC, it is important that you:

- Do not ignore the deadline.
- Submit a written explanation before the due date.
- Request an extension of time.
- Provide any supporting evidence that shows the delay is outside your control.

## Will Home Affairs Accept an Existing Indian PCC?

If you have previously provided an Indian Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) and have not travelled to India since it was issued, you may not necessarily need to obtain a new PCC.

However, there is no automatic exemption or general policy that allows all Indian applicants to use an existing PCC.

The Department of Home Affairs has confirmed that each visa application will be assessed on a case-by-case basis. In some situations, a previously issued PCC may be accepted, while in others, an updated certificate may still be requested.

This means factors such as your travel history and the specific visa requirements may influence the Department's decision.

So, if the Department requests an updated PCC and you are unable to obtain one due to the service suspension, it is important to inform Home Affairs as soon as possible and explain your situation rather than waiting until the deadline.

## What Should You Do If You Cannot Obtain an Indian PCC?

If you are affected by the temporary suspension, consider the following steps:

- Check whether you already have a valid Indian PCC.
- Review whether you have travelled to India since it was issued.
- Keep records of any attempts made to obtain a new PCC.
- Request an extension of time if required.
- Respond to all correspondence from the Department of Home Affairs promptly.

## Will This Affect Your Australian Visa Application?

Not necessarily.

The temporary suspension of Indian consular services does not mean your Australian visa application will automatically be refused or placed on hold.

The Department of Home Affairs has indicated that it understands the current circumstances and is allowing applicants to request additional time whenever required. Apart from this, existing PCCs may also be considered in certain cases.
