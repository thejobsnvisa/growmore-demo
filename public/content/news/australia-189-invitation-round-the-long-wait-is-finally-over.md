## Key Highlights of the 15 October 189 Invitation Round

The reported results show that invitations were issued across a wide range of occupations and point scores ranged from 65 to 95. Several trade occupations, including Carpenters, Cabinetmakers and Airconditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics, were reported at 65 points. Meanwhile, occupations such as Biochemist and Telecommunication Network Engineer recorded higher reported scores of 95 points.

Onshore applicants appear to have received invitations across more of the reported occupations than offshore applicants. However, the Department of Home Affairs will soon release the full official details of this invitation round, providing a clearer picture of the results.

## What Should You Do After the 189 Invitation Round?

If you have received an invitation in the latest Australia 189 Invitation Round, congratulations! Start preparing your visa application and lodge it carefully by providing all the required documents. Make sure the information you submit is accurate and consistent with your EOI. You must also lodge your application within the timeframe specified in your invitation.

However, if you have not received an invitation this time, do not lose hope because the subclass 189 visa is not the only pathway to Australia PR. Based on your occupation, points score, work experience and overall eligibility, you may be able to explore alternative options, such as the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) or the Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491). Employer-sponsored pathways may also be worth considering if you meet the relevant requirements.
