The wait is finally over for Indian applicants who registered for Australia’s Work and Holiday (subclass 462) visa ballot and have been waiting to see whether their registration was selected.

The Department of Home Affairs conducted the first random ballot selection on 10 August 2026. So, if you have registered for the ballot, now is the time to check your ImmiAccount and registered email address for a selection notification.

Selected applicants must now lodge their subclass 462 visa application within the specified timeframe and meet all the relevant visa requirements to move forward with their Work and Holiday visa application.

## **First Subclass 462 Ballot Selection for India**

For the 2026–27 program year, the Australian Government introduced the visa ballot process for applicants from India, China and Vietnam because demand for the Work and Holiday visa is high compared with the number of places available.

For applicants, the wait for the first ballot results came to an end after the selection was conducted on 10 August 2026.

However, the Department has not yet announced the dates for the second and third selections.

## **Indian Applicants: What Happens If You Are Selected?**

If your ballot registration is selected, the Department of Home Affairs will send you a Notification of Selection by email.

Your registration status in ImmiAccount will also change to “selected.”

The notification will invite you to apply for your first Work and Holiday (subclass 462) visa.

## **You Have 28 Days to Apply**

This is one of the most important points for selected applicants.

Once you receive the selection notification, you will have 28 days to lodge your visa application. Do not assume that being selected means you can delay the application. The selection notification provides the timeframe within which you need to apply.

## **Important PAN Card Requirement for Indian Applicants**

Indian applicants should pay special attention to the requirements related to a national identity document.

For Indian passport holders, the Department requires a valid PAN card for the ballot and visa process. An Aadhaar card is not accepted as the required national identity card for this purpose.

If you are selected, the national ID details provided during your ballot registration must match the details in your visa application.

You must also provide a copy of **both sides of your PAN card** under the relevant national identity document section at the time of visa application submission. A mismatch between the details in your ballot registration and visa application can prevent you from submitting the application.

## **Indian Applicants Should Check:**

- PAN card validity
- PAN number entered during registration
- Name and other details against your passport
- National ID details entered in the ballot registration
- National ID details entered in the visa application
- Copies of both sides of the PAN card

## **How Were Indian Applicants Selected?**

The ballot selection is a random and automated process.

**This means the Department does not rank Indian applicants according to:**

- Points
- Occupation
- Salary
- Education level
- Work experience
- English test score

Your registration is either randomly selected or not selected.

## **What If You Were Not Selected in the First Round?**

If you have checked the Subclass 462 ballot results and were not selected in the 10 August 2026 selection round, do not lose hope because there is still a possibility of future selections.

The Department has confirmed that it will make further random selections during the program year. However, the dates for the second and third selections for India are currently not announced.

Registrations can remain in the selection pool until 30 April 2027.

Therefore, applicants should continue checking their ImmiAccount and email rather than assuming that not being selected in the first round means the opportunity is over.

## **Ready to Move Forward With Your 462 Visa?**

If you have been selected in the Subclass 462 ballot, the next step is to lodge your Work and Holiday visa application within 28 days. Before applying, ensure your PAN card details, passport information and other supporting documents are accurate and consistent with the details provided in your ballot registration.
