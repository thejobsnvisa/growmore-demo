From 1 July 2026, the Australian Government has increased visa application charges across a wide range of temporary and permanent visa categories as part of its annual fee update. The revised fees apply to many popular visas, including skilled migration, employer-sponsored, student, partner, parent, visitor, and graduate visas.

So, if you're planning to move to Australia for work, study, permanent residency, or to join your family, these higher application charges will increase the upfront cost of applying. The new visa fees apply to applications submitted on or after 1 July 2026, so applicants should plan their budgets accordingly.

## What Does This Mean for Applicants?

Visa Application Charges (VACs) are generally reviewed each year and are typically indexed in line with the Consumer Price Index (CPI). From 1 July 2026, the revised charges are now in effect and apply to all eligible visa applications lodged from today onwards.

It is also worth remembering that the visa application charge is only one part of the overall migration cost. Depending on your Australian visa type, you may also need to budget for expenses such as English language tests, skills assessments, health examinations, police clearance certificates, biometrics, and document translations.
