## **Eligibility Strictly Depends on the NSW Skills List**

The primary goal of the NSW nomination procedure is to align migrant intake with the state’s genuine workforce shortages. This is why eligibility under the [Skilled Nominated visa 190 pathway](https://www.growmore.one/nsw-skilled-migration-round-update-15-jan-2026/) is directly linked to the NSW Skills List.

This list highlights jobs that are currently in demand in major sectors of the state’s economy. Here, occupations are classified at the ANZSCO unit group level, which means your eligibility is determined by both your job title and its official classification.

**To be considered for nomination, applicants must:**

- Hold a valid skills assessment
- Ensure their occupation appears on the current NSW Skills List
- Meet subclass requirements
- Maintain accurate and verifiable claims in their EOI
