For anyone planning to study, work, or migrate to Australia, obtaining a passport is the first and most important step in the immigration journey. This process has now become simpler for passport applicants in Gujarat, as the state has simplified its passport verification process.

The Gujarat Police has announced that applicants will no longer be required to visit police stations for routine passport verification. As a result, the verification process will only focus on confirming the applicant's citizenship and checking their criminal antecedents, which makes the procedure faster and more citizen friendly.

## **No More Police Station Visits**

The new guidelines were issued by the Gujarat Director General of Police (DGP) to further simplify the passport verification process across the state.

According to the DGP's office, these administrative changes are being made to reduce unnecessary inconvenience for citizens while ensuring that security checks continue to be carried out effectively.

One of the biggest changes is that passport applicants will no longer be called to the police station for verification. Previously, many applicants had to appear in person during the verification process, but this requirement has now been removed.

## **Verification Limited to Citizenship and Criminal Record**

Under the revised procedure, police verification will now be restricted to two key checks:

- Verification of the applicant's Indian citizenship.
- Verification of the applicant's criminal history or pending criminal cases.

Gujarat Police has clarified that routine passport verification no longer requires officers to visit the applicant's address, meet them in person, or collect their signature.

## **Home Visits Only in Exceptional Cases**

If police officers identify any suspicious information or require further clarification, they may visit the applicant's residence. However, such visits will be limited to exceptional cases.

Even in these situations, applicants will not be asked to report to the police station, making the process more convenient while maintaining necessary security standards.

## **What This Means for Australian Visa Applicants**

A valid passport is a mandatory requirement for every Australian visa application, whether you are applying for a Skilled Visa, Employer-Sponsored Visa, Student Visa, Partner Visa, or Visitor Visa. Without a valid passport, applicants cannot lodge their visa application or complete important identity verification requirements.

The simplified passport verification process in Gujarat is expected to reduce unnecessary delays for applicants and help them obtain their passports more efficiently. This can be particularly beneficial for individuals preparing documents for Australian immigration, where timely submission of applications is often important.
