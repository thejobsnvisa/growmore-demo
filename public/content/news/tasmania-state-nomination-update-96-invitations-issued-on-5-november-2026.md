Tasmania has issued 96 invitations to apply for state nomination on 5 November 2026. This is an increase in the number of candidates invited compared with the previous weekly invitation round. The invitations cover both the Subclass 190 Skilled Nominated visa and Subclass 491 Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa, with 68 invitations issued for Subclass 190 and 28 for Subclass 491. 

As the state continues its weekly invitation rounds every Thursday, the latest update provides an opportunity for skilled migration candidates to understand the state's current nomination activity, available places and assessment approach.

## Tasmania Changes Nomination Assessment to a Six-Week Rolling Average 

One of the important updates in this announcement is the change to Tasmania's points assessment approach. 

Following a review of the rolling average invitation scores, Tasmania has moved from using a three-month rolling average to a six-week rolling average when determining the points score required for nomination approval at the assessment stage. 

The latest six-week rolling average invitation scores are: 

* **Subclass 190:** 324 points 
* **Subclass 491:** 77 points 

This change is important because receiving an invitation to apply for nomination does not automatically guarantee approval. Tasmania will still examine the priority attributes claimed by candidates and verify the information provided in their ROI and nomination application. 

The six-week rolling average is particularly relevant when Tasmania assesses applications where one or more claimed priority attributes cannot be verified. 

## What Happens If Tasmania Cannot Verify Your Priority Attributes? 

Tasmania has clarified that if any claimed priority attributes cannot be verified, the candidate's revised points score will determine the outcome. 

The assessment will follow these rules: 

**1.Revised score remains above the latest invitation threshold:** The application may still be approved. 

**2.Revised score falls below the latest threshold and six-week rolling average:** The nomination application will be declined. 

**3.Intentionally claiming incorrect attributes:** Applications will be declined if Tasmania finds that priority attributes were deliberately misrepresented to increase points. 

**4.Gold pass holders who do not meet Gold requirements:** The application will be declined, regardless of the total points score. 

## Tasmania Nomination Application Processing Update 

Tasmania has also provided an update on its nomination application processing position. 

The oldest nomination application that has been lodged but has not yet been allocated to a case officer was lodged on 29 September 2026. This provides an indication of the current application allocation position, but it should not be treated as a guaranteed processing timeframe for all applicants. 

## What Does the Latest Tasmania Invitation Round Mean for Applicants? 

The 5 November 2026 Tasmania invitation round is significant, as candidates must now consider the six-week rolling average alongside the latest invitation threshold when assessing their nomination prospects. 

If you are going to apply for a Tasmania state nomination, make sure to: 

* **Check your eligibility:** Understand your chosen pathway's requirements before submitting an ROI. 
* **Be accurate with priority attributes:** Claim only those you can support with valid evidence. 
* **Assess your points carefully:** Consider both the latest invitation threshold and six-week rolling average. 
* **Check Gold pass requirements:** Make sure you meet the specific Gold priority criteria if claiming this pathway. 
* **Keep your documents consistent:** Ensure your ROI claims match the evidence provided with your nomination application.
