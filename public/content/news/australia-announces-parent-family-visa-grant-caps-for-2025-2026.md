The Australian Government has officially released the grant limits for Contributory Parent, Parent and Other Family visas for the 2025–26 financial year, providing greater clarity on the number of visas that can be issued under these popular family migration pathways.

According to the latest legislative instrument, a maximum of 6,800 Contributory Parent visas, 1,700 Parent visas and 500 Other Family visas may be granted during the financial year running from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

Although the announcement does not introduce any changes to eligibility requirements or application procedures, it represents an important planning measure that helps regulate Australia's family migration program and manage visa demand across different categories.

For prospective applicants and families already waiting in lengthy processing queues, these annual planning levels offer valuable insight into the Government's migration priorities. Parent visas continue to attract significant demand each year, making allocation limits an important consideration for applicants planning long-term migration pathways.

It is important to understand that these figures relate to the number of visas that may be granted, rather than the number of applications that can be lodged. Eligible applicants may still submit applications in accordance with existing legislation, but visa grants remain subject to annual planning allocations and departmental processing priorities.

The legislative instrument commenced on 12 June 2026 and will automatically cease operation on 1 July 2026, as it applies exclusively to the 2025–26 financial year.

Applicants considering Parent or Other Family visa pathways are encouraged to stay updated on official migration planning announcements and seek professional advice when making long-term migration decisions, particularly given the historically high demand and extended processing times associated with these visa categories.

## Key Highlights

• 6,800 Contributory Parent visa grants  
• 1,700 Parent visa grants  
• 500 Other Family visa grants  
• Applicable for 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026  
• No change to eligibility requirements or visa application process
