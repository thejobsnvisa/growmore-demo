Australia is introducing significant changes to its Working Holiday Maker (WHM) visa program which will affect the people who are planning to apply for a second or third Working Holiday visa (Subclass 417 and Subclass 462). 

In a recent media release, Australia's Minister for Home Affairs, Tony Burke, announced changes to make the Working Holiday Maker (WHM) program fairer and more sustainable while continuing to support Australian businesses and regional communities. 

Here is what Working Holiday Maker visa applicants need to know about the upcoming changes. 

## What Are the Major Changes to the Working Holiday Maker Visa Program? 

The Australian Government is introducing a new registration and invitation system to manage applications for second and third Working Holiday Maker visas. 

Under this system, eligible applicants will need to register their interest and wait to be selected through an invitation round. Those who receive an invitation will be given a Next Year Regional Work Guarantee, allowing them to apply for another WHM visa once they have met the relevant specified work and other visa eligibility requirements. 

The key changes include: 

* A new registration and invitation process for second and third WHM visa applications. 
* Registration opening from 1 December 2026. 
* The new process taking effect from 1 March 2027. 
* An increase in the specified work requirement for a second WHM visa from 88 days to 120 days. 
* 45,000 places for second WHM visas and 25,000 places for third WHM visas in the 2026–27 program year. 
* An exemption from the new registration and invitation process for UK passport holders. 

**1.Transitional Arrangements: Apply Under the Existing Rules Until 28 February 2027**

Working Holiday Makers who are planning to apply for a second or third WHM visa can continue using the existing application process until 28 February 2027. 

To apply under the existing process, applicants must: 

* Complete the required specified work by 28 February 2027. 
* Lodge a valid second or third WHM visa application by 28 February 2027. 
* Meet all other relevant visa eligibility requirements. 

If an applicant does not lodge a valid visa application by 28 February 2027, they will be subject to the new registration and invitation process. 

This transitional arrangement is particularly important for Working Holiday Makers who are already completing their specified work or are planning to apply for their next visa before March 2027. 

**2. Specified Work Requirement for a Second Working Holiday Visa Will Increase** 

One of the major changes is the increase in the specified work requirement for a second Working Holiday Maker visa. 

Currently, eligible applicants generally need to complete 88 days (approximately three months) of specified work to qualify for a second WHM visa. 

However, from 1 March 2027, this requirement will increase to 120 days (approximately four months). 

There is also an important transitional provision. Eligible specified work completed before 1 March 2027 will count towards the new 120-day requirement. 

## What About the Third Working Holiday Visa? 

The specified work requirement for a third Working Holiday Maker visa will remain unchanged at six months (179 days). 

Therefore, the increase from 88 to 120 days applies only to the second WHM visa, not the third. 

**3. New Registration and Invitation Process from 1 March 2027**
 
From 1 March 2027, applicants seeking a second or third Working Holiday Maker visa (except UK passport holders) will need to follow the new registration and invitation process. The process will involve the following steps: 

### Step 1: Register Your Interest 

The registration portal opens on 1 December 2026. You can register from inside or outside Australia, even before completing your specified work. 

### Step 2: Wait for an Invitation 

Once registered, you will need to wait for an invitation from the Department of Home Affairs. The first monthly invitation round is expected in mid-January 2027. 

### Step 3: Receive the Next Year Regional Work Guarantee 

If selected, you will receive a Next Year Regional Work Guarantee through the invitation process. This gives you the opportunity to apply for a further WHM visa after completing the required specified work and meeting the other visa criteria. 

### Step 4: Lodge Your Visa Application 

Once invited, you will have 12 months to lodge your second or third WHM visa application. 

**4. Working Holiday Maker Visa Places for 2026–27** 

The Australian Government has announced a total allocation of 70,000 places for second and third Working Holiday Maker visas in the 2026–27 program year. The allocation is divided as follows: 

| Visa Category | Available Places |
| --- | ---: |
| Second Working Holiday Maker Visa | 45,000 |
| Third Working Holiday Maker Visa | 25,000 |
| Total | 70,000 |

The registration period will remain open until 30 June 2027, and registrations will remain valid until that date. 

**5. UK Passport Holders Exempt from the New Process** 

There is an important exemption for Working Holiday Makers holding a UK passport. 

UK passport holders will not be required to participate in the new registration and invitation process when applying for a second or third Working Holiday Maker visa. 

They will therefore be exempt from the new selection arrangements introduced from 1 March 2027. 

## Important Dates to Keep in Mind for Working Holiday Maker Visa Applicants 

If you are currently holding a Working Holiday visa or planning to apply for a second or third WHM visa, the following dates are important. 

| Date | Important Update |
| --- | --- |
| 1 December 2026 | Registration portal opens for second and third WHM visa applicants. |
| Mid-January 2027 | First monthly invitation round expected. |
| 28 February 2027 | Final date to complete specified work and lodge a valid application under the transitional arrangements. |
| 1 March 2027 | New registration and invitation process takes effect. |
| 1 March 2027 | Second WHM visa specified work requirement increases from 88 to 120 days. |
| 30 June 2027 | Registration period ends and registrations remain valid until this date. |
