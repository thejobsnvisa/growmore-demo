 Western Australia has released its latest State Nominated Migration Program invitation round on 3 May 2026. The round shows a clear focus on Subclass 190 visa nominations, with invitations directed primarily toward priority trade occupations across selected streams. 

As the program moves into the final stage of the 2025–26 year, the state has reduced overall allocations and is selecting applicants more carefully compared to earlier rounds. 

This round clearly reflects a stronger focus on in-demand trade occupations and onshore applicants already working or residing in Western Australia. Here are the full details about the latest WA invitation round:

**What This Round Means**

This round reflects a tightened selection approach in Western Australia’s State Nominated Migration Program, with invitations issued exclusively under Subclass 190 and no activity under Subclass 491.  

The allocation is focused mainly on WASMOL Schedule 2 trade occupations, which indicates ongoing demand for skilled workers in construction and technical fields in Western Australia.
