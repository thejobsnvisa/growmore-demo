If you are an Indian citizen living in Australia or an Australian citizen applying for an Indian visa through VFS Global, this update directly impacts your application process. VFS Global has officially announced a temporary suspension of Consular, Passport and Visa (CPV) services across Australia following instructions from the High Commission of India in Canberra. 

**This suspension takes effect from 1 July 2026 until further notice. Here is what the official notice said regarding the suspension and its impact on applicants.**

## What Has Been Suspended?

The suspension applies to CPV services, which generally include:

**1. Visa application processing support**

**2. Passport-related consular services**

**3. Document submission and collection services linked to Indian authorities**

In simple terms, applicants will not be able to access normal CPV processing services at VFS Global centres in Australia until services are officially restored.

## What This Means for Visa Applicants

**This suspension creates a processing pause but not a cancellation. That means:**

**1. Applications already submitted will remain on hold**

**2. No new processing movement will take place until services resume**

**3. Applicants should expect delays in timelines for visas and passport-related services**

For people planning travel, migration, or urgent document requirements, this is a significant disruption that requires patience and close monitoring of official updates.

## What Happens to Ongoing Applications?

**VFS Global has confirmed the following important points:**

**1. Applications currently under process will remain pending until services restart**

**2. Applicants must wait for official updates before expecting movement**

**3. There is no requirement to resubmit applications unless officially instructed**

This ensures that submitted applications are not lost, but they will not proceed during the suspension period.

## Passport Collection Status

**If your passport is already processed and ready:**

**1. VFS Global will continue to return ready passports through existing collection methods**

**2. Normal passport return services remain active where processing is already complete**

This is one of the few services that will continue during the disruption.

## Support Available During the Suspension

**To assist applicants during this period, VFS Global has strengthened its customer support system:**

### **Phone support:** **03 9956 3830 from 09:00 to 15:30 AEST**

### **Email support:** **info.ind_aus@vfshelpline.com**

Applicants can use these channels for updates, clarification, and general queries related to their applications.

## Emergency Consular Assistance

Applicants requiring urgent or emergency consular help are advised to:

**1. Visit the official website of the High Commission of India, Canberra, or**

**2. Contact the relevant Indian Consulate or High Commission based on their location in Australia**

This is especially important for cases involving urgent travel, medical emergencies, or critical documentation needs.

## When Will Services Resume?

At this stage, there is no confirmed resumption date. Services will restart only when authorised by the relevant competent authorities.

VFS Global has stated that all Visa Application Centres will resume CPV services once official approval is granted.

## What Should Applicants Do Now?

**If you are currently applying for or waiting on a visa or consular service in Australia:**

**1. Do not panic because your application remains valid**

**2. Expect delays in processing timelines**

**3. Stay updated through official channels only**

**4. Avoid submitting duplicate applications unless instructed**
