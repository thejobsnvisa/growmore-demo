If you are planning to apply for an Australian Working Holiday Visa, then you may need to allow extra time for your visa application.

The Department of Home Affairs has updated its Working Holiday Maker (WHM) Program webpage and advises that a high volume of applications for both the Working Holiday Visa (Subclass 417) and the Work and Holiday Visa (Subclass 462) is being received, which results in longer processing times than usual.

So, applicants are encouraged to plan ahead and lodge their visa application early rather than waiting until the last minute.

## Latest Working Holiday Visa Processing Times

**Here are the current processing timeframes for both subclasses:**

### Working Holiday Visa (Subclass 417)

- 50% of applications are processed in less than 1 day.
- 90% of applications are processed within 38 days.

### Work and Holiday Visa (Subclass 462)

- 50% of applications are processed within 21 days.
- 90% of applications are processed within 70 days.

These figures indicate that processing times can vary significantly based on the individual application and the overall number of applications being received.

## Why Are Processing Times Taking Longer?

According to the Department, the increase in application volumes under the Working Holiday Maker Program is the primary reason for the extended processing timeframes.

However, applicants should keep in mind that visa processing times are estimates and can change based on factors such as:

- The number of applications being processed.
- Whether all required documents have been provided.
- Individual circumstances and any additional checks required by the Department.

## What Should Applicants Do?

So, if you are planning to apply for a Working Holiday Visa, it is advisable to submit your application well in advance of your intended travel date. Applicants should also ensure that their application is complete and accurate to avoid unnecessary delays.

As the demand is increasing day by day, preparing your application early can make your Australian Working Holiday plans smoother and less stressful.
