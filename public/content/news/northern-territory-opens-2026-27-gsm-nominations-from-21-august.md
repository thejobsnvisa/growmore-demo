A new opportunity is coming for skilled migrants who are aiming for the Northern Territory. The NT Government has received its full General Skilled Migration (GSM) nomination allocation for the 2026–27 program year, covering the Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 skilled visa pathways, and will now move ahead with its 2026–27 skilled migration nomination program. 

Nomination applications for the onshore as well as selected offshore stream will open on 21 August 2026, based on updated eligibility criteria for the new program year. 

## NT GSM Nomination Applications Open on 21 August 

The Northern Territory Government has confirmed that its nomination application system will reopen to new applications from 21 August 2026. 

Applicants are also advised to review the updated requirements before submitting their nomination application, as the NT Government has changed its eligibility criteria for the 2026–27 program year. 

Want to know the updated NT nomination criteria? Read our [detailed guide here](https://www.growmore.one/blogs/nt-190&491-nomination-criteria-updated-for-2026-2027). 

## How to Apply for Northern Territory Nomination and Visa Application? 

Applicants planning to apply for Northern Territory GSM nomination must follow the required nomination and visa application process. The key steps include: 

**1.Check your visa eligibility:** See if you meet the requirements of the Department of Home Affairs, such as your age, English language skills, skills assessment and the migration points test. 

**2.Check NT nomination eligibility:** Review the eligibility requirements for the relevant NT Government GSM nomination stream. 

**3.Submit an EOI:** Lodge an Expression of Interest (EOI) through the Department of Home Affairs' SkillSelect system. 

**4.Apply for NT nomination:** Submit your NT Government nomination application along with the required supporting documents through the NT Government's online application system. 

**5.Receive a SkillSelect invitation:** If successfully nominated by the NT Government, you will receive an invitation through SkillSelect to apply for your skilled visa. 

**6.Lodge your visa application:** Submit your visa application through ImmiAccount to the Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. 

**7.Await the visa decision:** The Department of Home Affairs will assess your visa application and advise you of the outcome. 

**8.Meet NT commitments:** If your visa is granted, you will be required to meet your long-term commitments to living and working in the Northern Territory. 

## Get Your Documents Ready 

People who want to apply should make sure they have all the documents ready that are needed before the nomination application opens. This includes: 

* Positive skills assessment 
* Current CV or resume 
* Qualification documents and academic transcripts 
* Passport and identity documents 
* English language evidence, where required 
* SkillSelect EOI and points breakdown 
* Evidence supporting additional points claimed 
* Australian visa evidence, where applicable 
* NT commitment letter and other stream-specific documents 

Documents must be valid and meet the NT Government's specified requirements at the time of application. 

## Important Reminder for Applicants With Expiring Visas 

The NT Government has also reminded applicants that it cannot guarantee nominations before visa deadlines. 

So, people who are applying from Australia and whose visa is about to expire should not think that they can stay in Australia just because they have applied for a Northern Territory nomination. They should get advice from a Registered Migration Agent or a legal practitioner about what they can do with their visa and make sure they remain lawful in Australia. 

## Get Ready for NT Nomination Applications 

With the Northern Territory now having received its full 2026–27 GSM nomination allocation and confirmed the opening date for new applications, eligible applicants should start preparing for the program. 

Applicants should look at the rules to see if they are eligible, make sure their SkillSelect EOI is current and get all the documents they need before applications start on 21 August 2026.
