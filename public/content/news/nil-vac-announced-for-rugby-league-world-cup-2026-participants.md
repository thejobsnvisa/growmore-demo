Great news for Rugby League World Cup 2026 participants! As Australia prepares to welcome the Rugby League World Cup 2026 this October, the Government has announced a visa fee exemption under the Subclass 408 Temporary Activity Visa that allows certain applicants to apply without paying any Visa Application Charge (VAC).

This is welcome news for players, officials, media personnel, and other accredited participants who will be travelling to Australia for one of the world's biggest rugby league tournaments. This move not only reduces the cost of obtaining an Australian visa but also makes the visa process more accessible for those officially involved in the event.

## **What is the Announcement?**

Under the [Migration (Nil VAC Arrangements and Other Matters) Amendment (Rugby League World Cup 2026) Instrument 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2026L00950/asmade/text), which commenced on 15 July 2026, the Rugby League World Cup 2026 has been officially recognised as an Australian Government Endorsed Event (AGEE) for the purposes of the Subclass 408 Temporary Activity Visa.

As part of this legislative update, eligible primary applicants can apply for a Subclass 408 visa under the AGEE stream with a nil Visa Application Charge (VAC).

In simple terms, this means there is no government visa application fee for eligible applicants.

## **Who Can Apply for the Nil VAC Arrangement?**

**The visa fee exemption is available to individuals who are participating in the Rugby League World Cup 2026 in an official capacity, including:**

- Members of competing teams
- Official coaching staff
- Match officials
- Official support staff
- Personnel of broadcast rights holders
- Personnel of non-rights holder media organisations
- VIPs attending the event in an official capacity
- Other officially recognised participants of the Rugby League World Cup 2026

To qualify for the nil VAC arrangement, applicants must lodge their application as the primary applicant under the Australian Government Endorsed Events stream of the Subclass 408 Temporary Activity Visa.

## **What Is the Subclass 408 Temporary Activity Visa?**

The Subclass 408 Temporary Activity Visa allows individuals to travel to Australia temporarily to participate in specific activities or events.

For major international events, the Australian Government can designate an event under the Australian Government Endorsed Events (AGEE) stream. This stream is commonly used to facilitate the entry of athletes, officials, media personnel, and other accredited participants involved in internationally significant events held in Australia.

- Official recognition under Australia's Australian Government Endorsed Events framework.
- Reduced travel costs for accredited participants attending the tournament.
