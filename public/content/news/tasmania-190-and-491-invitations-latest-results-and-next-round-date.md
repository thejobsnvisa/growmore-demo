With several Australian states opening their skilled migration programs for 2026–27, Tasmania remains an important option for skilled migrants exploring state nomination for Australia PR. The state has continued its weekly invitation rounds, with the latest round held on 15 October 2026 showing an increase in invitations compared with the previous week.

For applicants waiting for a Tasmanian state nomination, the latest update provides a fresh look at invitation numbers and the ROI scores invited for the subclass 190 and 491 visas. So, how many invitations were issued this week, what were the lowest ROI scores invited, and when will the next round take place? Let's look at the latest results.

## Tasmania Invitation Round Results: 15 October 2026

The Tasmanian Government issued the following invitations to apply for state nomination on 15 October 2026.

| Details | Subclass 190 | Subclass 491 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total invitations issued | 45 | 32 |
| Gold invitations | 12 | 1 |
| Green invitations | 33 | - |
| Further invitations | - | 31 |
| Lowest ROI score invited | 319 points | 88 points |
| ROIs on hand after the round | 878 | 612 |
| Nomination places available | 944 places | 682 places |
| Nomination applications lodged but awaiting a decision | 103 applications | 64 applications |
| Invitations issued but applications not yet lodged | 83 invitations | 74 invitations |
| Lowest point score required to gain nomination at assessment, as published | 427 points | 81 points |

The latest round saw Tasmania issue 77 invitations in total, with 45 for the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) and 32 for the Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491).

The lowest ROI score invited decreased for both visa subclasses. For subclass 190, it fell from 323 to 319 points, while for subclass 491, it dropped from 89 to 88 points. Although these results provide an indication of recent selection activity, the lowest ROI score invited in one round does not guarantee an invitation at the same score in a future round.

The figures also show that Tasmania continues to have ROIs on hand and nomination applications awaiting a decision. Applicants should ensure that their claimed points and priority attributes are accurate and supported by evidence, as meeting a particular score alone does not guarantee nomination.

It is also important to distinguish the lowest ROI scores invited from the published assessment figures of 427 points for subclass 190 and 81 points for subclass 491. These published figures represent rolling three-month averages of the lowest point scores resulting in an invitation at assessment, rather than the lowest ROI scores invited in this round. They should not be treated as guaranteed invitation scores or fixed eligibility thresholds.

## Tasmania's Next ROI Invitation Round: 23 October 2026

Along with this week’s invitation round, the state has confirmed that its next round will take place on Friday, 23 October 2026, rather than the usual Thursday schedule.

The change is due to Hobart Show Day, which falls on Thursday, 22 October 2026. As this public holiday comes together with Tasmania's normal invitation day, the state will issue invitations on Friday instead.

Tasmania has also reported that the oldest nomination application lodged but not yet allocated to a case officer was lodged on 26 September 2026. This gives applicants an indication of the current allocation position, but it should not be treated as a guaranteed processing timeframe.
