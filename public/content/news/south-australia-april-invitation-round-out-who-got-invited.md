## South Australia April Invitation Round Out: Who Got Invited?

South Australia has officially released the results of its April 2026 invitation round, and the total numbers have increased by around 10% in compared to the previous round. In this round, a total of **445 invitations were** issued across the Subclass 190 and Subclass 491 visas, which shows ongoing demand for professionals in South Australia.

Skilled occupations from engineering, healthcare, IT, and other critical sectors were prioritised through this [General Skilled Migration program](https://www.growmore.one/our-services/gsm-general-skilled-migration/).

## **Key Takeaways From This Round**

- If we look at the April round alone, then Health Professionals, Design, Engineering, Science and Transport, Construction Trade, and ICT Professionals have clearly dominated.

- Same as the March round, all ICT invitations went through Subclass 491, showing South Australia is focusing on tech talent in regional areas for long-term growth.

- Carpenters, electricians, and other trade specialists received numerous invitations that highlight South Australia’s need for skilled construction workers.

- After this round, out of the total allocated 2,250 places, only a small portion remains for this year’s program, highlighting the high competition for the upcoming round.
