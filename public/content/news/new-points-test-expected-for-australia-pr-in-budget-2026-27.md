The countdown for Australia’s Federal Budget 2026–27 has officially started, with Treasurer Jim Chalmers set to announce the budget on Tuesday, 12 May 2026 at 7:30 PM (AEST). Based on recent government discussions and pre-budget announcements, one of the biggest reforms expected this year could be related to skilled migration and the Australia PR points test.

So, if you are a skilled migrant planning your future in Australia, this budget is a major development to watch closely. Early indications suggest the Government may be preparing to significantly reshape how skilled migrants are selected for Australia PR.

## Major Changes Expected in Australia PR Points Test

One of the biggest developments linked to the upcoming budget is a potential overhaul of the migration points test used for skilled migration.

Although the government has not yet officially revealed the final structure of the revised points system, recent migration reviews and policy discussions are already indicating where the changes may head. It will align with earlier recommendations from the Migration Review that target a productivity-focused skilled migration system.

**The revised point test model will place a stronger focus on:**

* Younger skilled applicants  
* Higher English language proficiency  
* Strong educational qualifications  
* Long-term workforce participation  

## Other Expected Reforms in Budget 2026–27

Apart from the Australia PR points test changes, the upcoming budget is also expected to include broader reforms focused on productivity and workforce participation, including:

### **1. Faster Overseas Skills Recognition**

The government plans to reduce delays in recognising overseas qualifications and occupational licensing by up to six months. This could help skilled migrants such as engineers, tradespeople, and health professionals enter the Australian workforce faster after arrival.

### **2. Reducing Business Red Tape**

The budget is also expected to include measures to simplify regulations, reduce compliance burdens, and improve digital reporting systems for businesses.

### **3. Permanent Instant Asset Write-Off**

The government is expected to make the $20,000 instant asset write-off permanent for eligible small businesses to support business investment.

### **4. Digital and Productivity Reforms**

The broader package may also include expansion of digital government services and reforms aimed at improving productivity and long-term economic growth.

## What This Could Mean for Skilled Migrants

If these reforms proceed, the selection process for skilled migration visas like Subclass 189, Subclass 190, and Subclass 491 could become more strategically focused. Here, applicants with strong English scores, younger age profiles, higher qualifications, and in-demand skilled work experience may get a significant benefit.

For many applicants already preparing their EOI or skills assessment, this becomes an important time to review overall profile strength, points competitiveness, and long-term migration planning.
