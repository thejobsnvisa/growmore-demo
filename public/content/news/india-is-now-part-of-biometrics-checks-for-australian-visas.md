From 26 October 2026, if you are applying for an Australian visa from India, you may be required to provide fingerprints and a facial image as part of the visa application process. The Australian Government has expanded its biometric collection requirements to nearly 60 countries, which also includes India.

The change is part of Australia's ongoing efforts to maintain a secure, efficient and trusted visa system. It is relevant to people planning to visit family in Australia, apply for a student visa or pursue other Australian visa pathways.

However, this does not mean every Australian visa applicant in India must automatically provide biometrics. The Department of Home Affairs will notify applicants if they are required to provide their biometric information and explain how to complete the process.

## How Can You Provide Biometrics for an Australian Visa?

Depending on your circumstances, you may be asked to provide biometrics in one of two ways:

- **In person:** Attend an Australian Biometrics Collection Centre (ABCC) to have your fingerprints and facial image collected.
- **Digitally:** Use the Australian Immi App if you are eligible.

Eligible applicants who have previously provided biometrics at an ABCC and hold a valid passport may have the option to use the Australian Immi App at no cost.

## Do the New Biometrics Rules Apply to Existing Australian Visa Applications?

No. The expanded biometric collection requirements apply only to new visa applications lodged on or after 26 October 2026.

Applications lodged before this date are not affected by the new requirements, and visas already granted remain unaffected by this change.

## Will This Change Affect Australian Visa Processing Times?

The Department of Home Affairs does not expect visa processing timelines to be delayed if applicants complete any needed biometric steps on time.

So, if you receive a biometric request, do not ignore it or delay in taking action. Completing the requested steps within the specified timeframe can help prevent avoidable delays in the processing of your application.

## How Is Your Biometric Information Protected?

According to the Australian Government, biometric information will be collected, stored and managed in accordance with Australian legislation and applicable local laws, with privacy and data protection safeguards in place.

Applicants should provide their information only through the process specified by the Department of Home Affairs and the authorised channels identified in their instructions.
