One after another, Australian states and territories are reaching the end of their 2025–26 skilled migration allocations. After the ACT, Tasmania, and Queensland, the Northern Territory has now officially confirmed that its General Skilled Migration (GSM) nomination allocation has been fully exhausted.

This means the Territory has used all available nomination places allocated by the Australian Government for the current program year. While this marks the end of available nomination places for 2025–26, it is important for applicants to understand that this is not the full end of the application process.

## Existing Applications Will Continue to Be Processed

If you have already lodged an NT nomination application for Australia PR, there is no need to submit a new application. Applications already in the assessment queue will continue to be processed under standard arrangements.

Applicants who satisfy the NT nomination requirements will be considered for nomination once the Northern Territory receives its allocation for the 2026–27 migration program year.

## Which Visa Applicants Are Impacted?

The update affects applicants who are seeking Northern Territory nomination for:

- Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190)
- Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa (Subclass 491)

Because during every year, each state and territory receives a fixed number of nomination places from the Australian Government. Once those places are fully allocated, no further nominations can be issued until the Australian Government provides a new quota. This means new NT nomination places will only become available after the Australian Government allocates a new quota for a future program year.

## MigrationNT Cannot Guarantee Nomination Before Visa Expiry

One of the most important reminders from MigrationNT is that nomination cannot be guaranteed before an applicant's current visa expires.

So, if you are waiting for your nomination outcome, you should ensure that:

- Your current Australian visa remains valid
- Your skills assessment does not expire
- Your English language test results remain valid
- Any other supporting documents are kept up to date throughout the assessment process

## What Should Applicants Do Now?

If your NT nomination application has already been lodged:

- Continue waiting for assessment
- Regularly check for updates from MigrationNT
- Keep all required documents valid
- Maintain a lawful visa status while your application is being processed

If you have not applied yet, this is a good opportunity to prepare your documents, review your eligibility, and monitor announcements for the 2026–27 Northern Territory Skilled Migration Program. Being prepared in advance can help you submit a complete and competitive application once new nomination places become available.
