South Australia has opened Registrations of Interest (ROIs) for its 2026–27 General Skilled Migration program, providing an opportunity for eligible skilled migrants to seek state nomination for Australian permanent residency.

The state's nomination pathways are mainly divided into four streams covering both onshore and offshore applicants. Each stream has eligibility requirements relating to occupation, residence, skilled employment, qualifications, and English proficiency.

If you want to apply for Australian state nomination but are unsure about your eligibility, this guide explains the key requirements for all four streams.

## State Nomination Eligibility Criteria for Onshore Applicants

South Australia has three streams available for eligible applicants living and working in the state. These streams differ according to whether applicants graduated in South Australia, gained skilled employment in the state, or work in an eligible outer regional location.

### Stream 1: South Australian Graduates

This stream is for eligible graduates who completed their studies in South Australia and meet the relevant study, residence, and skilled employment requirements.

**Eligibility requirements:**

- Have an active SkillSelect Expression of Interest (EOI), with South Australia selected as the first preferred state or territory.
- Have an occupation eligible under the South Australian Graduates stream occupation list.
- Currently live and work in South Australia.
- Be under 45 years of age when state nomination is approved.
- Hold a valid and positive skills assessment for the nominated occupation.
- Have at least Competent English and a minimum of 65 points in the Department of Home Affairs points test, including state nomination points.
- Have completed a CRICOS-registered course of at least 46 CRICOS weeks with a South Australian education provider.
- Meet the minimum qualification level specified for the nominated occupation and have completed at least 75% of the qualification in South Australia.
- Have lived in South Australia for at least one year during their studies and for at least the last six months.
- Have completed at least six months of relevant skilled employment after completing their course and currently work full-time for at least 30 hours per week.

The employment must be related to the nominated occupation and generally fall within the same ANZSCO Sub Major Group, represented by the first two digits of the occupation code.

### Stream 2: Skilled Employment in South Australia

This stream is for skilled workers who live and work in South Australia and meet the required employment and residence conditions.

**Eligibility requirements:**

- Have an active SkillSelect EOI, with South Australia selected as the first preferred state or territory.
- Have an occupation eligible under the Skilled Employment in South Australia stream occupation list.
- Currently live and work in South Australia.
- Be under 45 years of age when state nomination is approved.
- Hold a valid and positive skills assessment.
- Have at least Competent English and a minimum of 65 points, including state nomination points.
- Have lived in South Australia for at least the last 12 months.
- Have worked for at least the last 12 months and currently hold full-time employment of at least 30 hours per week.

The employment must be related to and at the same skill level as the nominated occupation. For South Australian nomination, this is generally assessed by reference to the same ANZSCO Sub Major Group.

### Stream 3: Outer Regional Skilled Employment

This stream is for eligible skilled workers who live and work in Outer Regional South Australia.

**Eligibility requirements:**

- Have an active SkillSelect EOI, with South Australia selected as the first preferred state or territory.
- Have an occupation eligible under the Outer Regional Skilled Employment stream occupation list.
- Currently live and work in Outer Regional South Australia.
- Be under 45 years of age when state nomination is approved.
- Hold a valid and positive skills assessment.
- Have at least Competent English and a minimum of 65 points, including state nomination points.
- Have lived and worked in Outer Regional South Australia for at least the last nine months.
- Currently hold full-time employment of at least 30 hours per week.

The employment must be related to and at the same skill level as the nominated occupation, generally within the same ANZSCO Sub Major Group.

## State Nomination Eligibility Criteria for Offshore Applicants

### Stream 4: Offshore Skilled Migration

The state nomination process for offshore applicants is somewhat different from that for onshore applicants. Offshore applicants do not need to submit a Registration of Interest (ROI). Instead, they need to submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) through SkillSelect and ensure they meet the applicable eligibility criteria for South Australian state nomination.

Under the general selection criteria provided for the 2026–27 program, applicants must:

- Have at least three years of skilled work experience in their nominated or closely related occupation within the last five years.
- Meet the applicable Department of Home Affairs visa requirements.
- Demonstrate that they were eligible for the claims entered in their SkillSelect EOI at the time of invitation.

### Special Work Experience Concession for Construction and Trades Professionals

Eligible applicants whose nominated occupation is included in the applicable construction and trades occupation list may be considered with one year of skilled employment within the last three years, instead of the general three-year requirement within the last five years.
