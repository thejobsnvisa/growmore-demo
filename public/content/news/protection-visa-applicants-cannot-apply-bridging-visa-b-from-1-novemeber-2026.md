Australia has introduced changes to Bridging Visa B eligibility that will affect people with unresolved protection visa applications or related court proceedings. 
 
The changes apply to new Bridging Visa B applications lodged on or after 1 November 2026. People planning overseas travel should understand how these rules affect them before making arrangements. 

## What is a Bridging Visa B? 

A Bridging Visa B, also called a BVB or subclass 020 visa, allows an eligible person to leave Australia temporarily and return within an approved travel period while their visa matter continues. 
 
Holding a Bridging Visa A does not, by itself, give you permission to leave Australia and return. 
 
## What is changing? 

Under the new rules, a person cannot validly apply for a BVB if they have: 
 
An unresolved valid Protection (Class XA) visa application, including a subclass 866 application; or 
Ongoing court proceedings concerning that protection application, including a court appeal. 
 
This restriction can apply while the protection application is being considered by Home Affairs, during an ongoing valid Administrative Review Tribunal (ART) review, or while related judicial review proceedings continue. 

## Does this affect protection applications lodged before November 2026? 
 
Yes. The important date is when you apply for the BVB, rather than when you originally applied for protection. 

For example, someone who applied for protection in 2024 and applies for a BVB on 5 November 2026 will be subject to the new rules if their protection matter remains unresolved. 
 
An older protection application or an existing Bridging Visa A does not provide an exemption. 
 
## What if I also have a partner visa or another visa application? 

Having another visa application does not overcome the restriction while your protection matter remains unresolved. 

For example, a person with both a pending partner visa application and an unresolved Class XA protection application would still be caught by the new BVB exclusion. 
 
The new rules require an eligible non-protection visa matter and no unresolved Class XA protection application or related court proceedings. 
 
## Can I obtain a BVB for a family emergency? 

The new protection exclusion does not include an exception for compassionate or compelling circumstances. 

A serious family illness, funeral or another urgent reason for travel does not, by itself, overcome this restriction. Although substantial reasons for travel remain part of the ordinary BVB requirements, the applicant must also satisfy the new eligibility rules. 
 
## What if my protection application was refused? 

A refusal does not necessarily mean the protection matter has finished. 
 
If a valid ART review remains ongoing, or the matter is before a court, the restriction may still apply. 

However, having applied for protection in the past does not create a permanent BVB ban. If the protection matter has fully concluded and no related court proceedings remain ongoing, the historical application alone does not trigger this exclusion. The person must still have an eligible non-protection visa matter and meet every other BVB requirement. 
 
## What happens to BVB applications lodged before 1 November 2026? 
 
These amendments do not apply to a BVB application validly lodged before 1 November 2026, even if Home Affairs decides it after that date. 

However, an application lodged before commencement is not automatically approved. It must satisfy the requirements that apply to it. 

## What if I already hold a BVB? 

These changes do not automatically cancel an existing BVB or remove its approved travel facility. 
 
Before travelling, check that your visa remains in effect and that you can return within the travel period stated in your grant notice. 

If you need to make a fresh BVB application on or after 1 November 2026, the new rules will apply. 

## Are other visa applicants affected? 

People with eligible non-protection visa applications or related court proceedings may continue to qualify for a BVB, provided they have no unresolved Class XA protection matter and meet all other requirements. 

A BVB is not automatically available simply because a person has a pending visa application. 
 
## What should you do before travelling? 

Before booking flights or leaving Australia, have your current visa, application history, review proceedings and travel rights checked. 

Do not assume that holding a bridging visa guarantees that you can return to Australia. Also, do not withdraw a protection application or court case simply to pursue a BVB without advice about the consequences for your immigration status and remaining options.
