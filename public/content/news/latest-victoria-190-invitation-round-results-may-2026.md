## Key Insights from the Round
ICT occupations continue to require highly competitive profiles, especially candidates with strong salaries and relevant employment in Victoria. At the same time, invitations issued to occupations such as Primary School Teacher and Production Manager suggest that occupation demand is still influencing invitation outcomes. 

The offshore Hospital Pharmacist invitation also highlights that offshore applicants can still receive invitations if they have a strong combination of qualifications, English proficiency, and skilled experience.
