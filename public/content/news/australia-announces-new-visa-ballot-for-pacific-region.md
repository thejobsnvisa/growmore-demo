## Australia Opens 2026–27 Pacific Engagement Visa Ballot

*Date: June 17, 2026*

The Australian Government has announced that registrations for the Pacific Engagement Visa (PEV) ballot for the 2026–27 program year will open on July 1, 2026, offering a fresh opportunity for eligible citizens from Pacific nations to pursue permanent residency in Australia.

The Pacific Engagement Visa, also known as the [Subclass 192 visa](https://www.growmore.one/news/australia-opens-subclass-192-tuvalu-ballot-on-1-may-with-280-places/), is part of Australia’s broader strategy to strengthen economic, cultural, and people-to-people connections across the Pacific region. The program continues to attract attention as one of the most inclusive migration pathways, open to applicants from diverse professional and educational backgrounds.

## New Ballot Requires Fresh Applications

Officials have confirmed that the upcoming ballot is a new registration cycle, meaning applicants who participated in previous ballots will not be automatically included. All interested individuals must submit a new registration within the official application window.

The ballot will remain open until July 29, 2026, after which a random selection process will be conducted. Only candidates who are selected in the ballot will be invited to move forward with the visa application process.

## Two-Step Selection Process

**The visa follows a structured and transparent process designed to ensure fairness:**

* Applicants must first register for the PEV ballot  
* Selected individuals will then receive an invitation to apply for the visa  

The selection is conducted through a randomised system, ensuring equal access regardless of skill level, occupation, or gender.

## Eligible Countries

**Citizens from the following countries are eligible to participate in the 2026–27 ballot:**

* Federated States of Micronesia  
* Fiji  
* Kiribati  
* Palau  
* Papua New Guinea  
* Republic of the Marshall Islands  
* Samoa  
* Solomon Islands  
* Timor-Leste  
* Tonga  
* Tuvalu  

The program is designed to ensure balanced representation across participating nations, with allocations made for each country.

## Key Features of the Visa

The Pacific Engagement Visa provides successful applicants with a pathway to permanent residency in Australia, along with opportunities to build long-term careers and settle with their families.

**Key highlights include:**

* Ability to live and work in Australia permanently  
* Inclusion of partners and dependent children in applications  
* Access to employment and integration opportunities  

The Australian Government plans to offer up to 3,000 visa places annually, including accompanying family members.

## Eligibility Criteria

**To register for the ballot, applicants must meet several basic requirements:**

* Be aged between 18 and 45 years  
* Hold a valid passport from an eligible country  
* Be born in, or have a parent born in, a participating nation  
* Pay a registration fee of AUD 25  

Selection in the ballot does not guarantee a visa. Applicants who are invited to apply must meet additional conditions, including employment requirements, English proficiency, and health and character checks.

## Focus on Fair Access

The ballot system has been designed to promote transparency and equal opportunity, allowing individuals from all backgrounds to participate without restrictions related to profession or income level.

Authorities have also cautioned applicants to rely only on official government channels for registration and updates, warning against potential scams targeting visa applicants.

## Outlook

With registrations set to begin in early July, demand for the Pacific Engagement Visa is expected to remain high across eligible countries. The initiative continues to play a significant role in Australia’s regional engagement efforts, offering new migration opportunities while strengthening ties with Pacific communities.

Prospective applicants are encouraged to prepare their documentation in advance and ensure timely registration during the application window.
