Australia has introduced a new legislative instrument that will regulate how certain Child visa applications must be made.

The new **Migration (Arrangements for Child Visa Applications) Instrument 2026 (LIN 26/081)** has replaced the previous **Arrangements for Child Visa Applications 2016/051**. The new instrument was registered on **1 September 2026** and commenced the following day. It is made under **subregulation 2.07(5) of the Migration Regulations 1994**.

The change is primarily a replacement of the existing instrument rather than a major change to Child visa policy. The Department's Explanatory Statement describes the changes as minor and technical, with the existing arrangements continuing under the new instrument.

## Which Child Visa Applications Are Covered?

LIN 26/081 sets out the approved form, method and place for applications for three visa classes:

| Visa Class | Visa Type | Approved Form |
|---|---|---|
| **Class AH** | Child (Migrant) | **Form 47CH** |
| **Class BT** | Child (Residence) | **Form 47CH** |
| **Class TK** | Extended Temporary | **Form 918** |

The instrument sets out how these applications must be lodged, including which form applicants need to use and where and how the application should be submitted.

For **Child (Migrant)** and **Child (Residence)** visas, applicants must use **Form 47CH**. It covers different circumstances, including certain applications involving children of Australian citizens, permanent residents or eligible New Zealand citizens.

## Where Can You Lodge the Child Visa Application?

For the affected visa classes, the instrument specifies the required application arrangements.

Applications must be:

- **Posted** with sufficient prepaid postage to:

  **Department of Home Affairs**  
  **Child and Other Family Processing Centre**  
  **GPO Box 241**  
  **Port Melbourne VIC 3008**

- **Or delivered by courier** to:

  **Child and Other Family Processing Centre**  
  **Wellington Central**  
  **836 Wellington Street**  
  **West Perth WA 6005**

Applicants should ensure that the correct form, supporting documents and lodgement method are used before submitting the application.

## Restriction on Certain Pakistan Adoptions

One of the important provisions retained under **LIN 26/081** concerns overseas adoptions.

**Pakistan remains the only country specified under Schedule 2**, and the specified period is not limited to a particular date range.

In practical terms, an application for a **Child (Migrant) (Class AH)** or **Child (Residence) (Class BT)** visa will not be valid if the applicant seeks to satisfy the relevant visa requirements by claiming that the child was adopted in Pakistan.

This does **not** mean that every Child visa application involving a person from Pakistan is automatically invalid. The restriction specifically relates to using an adoption in the specified country as the basis for satisfying the relevant visa requirements.

## What Happens to Applications Lodged Under the Previous Instrument?

If an application was received at an address specified under the previous instrument **before the new instrument commenced**, it can still be treated as validly lodged under the new arrangements.

The application is also considered received on the date it was first received and stamped at that address.

This transitional provision helps ensure that applications lodged under the previous arrangements are not negatively affected simply because the old instrument has been replaced.

## Why Was the Child Visa Instrument Replaced?

The previous **Arrangements for Child Visa Applications 2016/051** was due to sunset on **1 October 2026**.

Rather than allowing the existing instrument to expire, the Government issued **LIN 26/081** to repeal and replace it.

The Explanatory Statement specifically states that the purpose is to continue the existing arrangements, with only **minor and technical changes**.

Therefore, the introduction of LIN 26/081 should primarily be understood as a continuation and formal replacement of the existing Child visa application arrangements rather than a major change to Child visa eligibility or policy.

## What Does This Mean for Child Visa Applicants?

For most applicants, the practical arrangements remain substantially the same. However, applicants should still check:

- The correct **visa class**
- The required **application form**
- The approved **lodgement method**
- The correct **Department of Home Affairs processing address**
- Any applicable **adoption-related restrictions**
- Transitional arrangements where an application was lodged under the previous instrument

Using the correct application form and following the required lodgement arrangements is important because an application that does not comply with the prescribed requirements may not be treated as a valid application.
