Tasmania is moving closer to the end of the current state nomination migration program year, and the state has released its latest weekly invitation round results on 28 May 2026.

In this round, a total of 64 ROIs were invited across both the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional visa (subclass 491) pathways.

With ROI submissions for the 2025-26 program year closing on 03 June 2026, the state is now entering the final stage. The next invitation round is expected on 04 June 2026, and it may potentially be the last round before this year’s program officially comes to an end. 

**Here are the details from today’s weekly invitation round:**

## Tasmania Invitation Round Results – 28 May 2026

| Visa Subclass | ROIs Invited | Lowest Score Invited |
|----------------|--------------|----------------------|
| Subclass 190 | 37 invitations | 314 points (Green Pass) |
| Subclass 491 | 27 invitations | 56 points (Orange-plus Pass) |

A total of 64 invitations were issued in this round, including 37 invitations for Subclass 190 and 27 invitations for Subclass 491 applicants.

## What This Means for Applicants

The latest round’s figures indicate that nomination pressure remains high, particularly within the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) pathway. With only 57 subclass 190 nomination places remaining and more than 530 ROIs still on hand, Tasmania is expected to maintain highly selective invitation activity in the final upcoming round on 04 June 2026.

The data also highlights strong ongoing demand across both visa categories, with more than 1,000 submitted ROIs still remaining in the system. This shows that Tasmania is continuing to prioritise applicants with stronger profiles, competitive ranking positions, and claims that closely match the state’s current nomination priorities.

So, applicants planning to apply for Australia PR through Tasmania state nomination should ensure that they submit their ROI before 3rd June and all ROI claims relating to points, skilled employment, English proficiency, residency status, and supporting documents are accurate, evidence-ready, and strategically presented before the final invitation round takes place.
