## What Should Applicants Do Next?

If you are waiting for a NSW Subclass 190 invitation, consider taking the following steps:

1. **Check your SkillSelect EOI:** Ensure your points score, English proficiency, qualifications and skilled work experience are accurately recorded. Also, check that your EOI details and NSW nomination preferences are correct.

2. **Review your occupation:** Confirm that your occupation meets the applicable NSW nomination requirements. For detailed information, read our guide on [NSW Skilled Migration: Check the Latest 190 Visa Eligibility](https://www.growmore.one/news/nsw-skilled-migration-check-the-latest-190-visa-eligibility/).

3. **Monitor official updates:** Check the NSW Government website for verified information about invitation rounds and nomination criteria.

4. **Assess other pathways:** Depending on your circumstances, you may also wish to explore other state nomination options, including Subclass 491.
