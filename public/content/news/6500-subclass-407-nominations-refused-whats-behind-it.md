A wave of Subclass 407 nomination refusals is putting applicants and sponsors on alert, as the Department of Home Affairs has revealed that around **6,500 nominations have been refused out of approximately 8,000 finalised since July**.

The Department has also clarified what happens after a Subclass 407 nomination is refused, giving affected applicants a clearer idea of what to expect next.

## Why Are Subclass 407 Nominations Being Refused?

The Department said the high number of refusals is mainly due to a sharp increase in Subclass 407 Training visa nominations and visa applications.

Most of the applications finalised by the Department did not meet the required eligibility criteria. The issue also affects related Subclass 407 visa applications, with many still being processed even after the nomination has been refused.

Currently, around:

* **2,500 visa applications** are linked to cases where applicants have received a section 57 Natural Justice letter after their nomination was refused.
* **4,000 visa applications** are linked to refused nominations but have not yet been progressed.

This means applicants with pending Subclass 407 applications could continue to receive decisions over the coming weeks and months.

## What Happens After a Subclass 407 Nomination Refusal?

The Department has confirmed that the previously understood process will continue.

When a nomination is refused, the Department generally intends to allow **14 days after the nomination refusal** before deciding the related visa application.

This gives applicants a short period to consider their options. However, what happens next depends on the reason the visa application is being considered for refusal.

## Section 57 Natural Justice Letter

If the nomination has been refused and the applicant no longer has an approved nomination, the Department may issue a **section 57 Natural Justice letter** if it is considering refusing the visa because there is no current nomination.

The standard response period for this letter is **28 days**.

Applicants who receive a section 57 letter should carefully read the concerns raised by the Department and respond with relevant information and evidence within the specified timeframe.

## Visa Refusal on GTE Grounds

A section 57 letter will not always be issued.

The Department has confirmed that if an applicant is found not to meet the **Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE)** requirement, the visa application may be refused on GTE grounds without first issuing a section 57 Natural Justice letter.

However, the Department still intends to allow the **14-day period after the nomination refusal** before making the visa decision.

This means applicants should not assume that they will always receive a section 57 letter before their visa application is refused.

## What Should You Do If Your 407 Nomination Is Refused?

If your Subclass 407 nomination has been refused, it is important to act rather than simply wait for the visa application to be decided.

Depending on your circumstances, you may need to consider options such as:

* **Arranging another nomination:** A new nomination may be possible in some cases. However, you should first understand why the original nomination was refused and address the issues involved.
* **Responding to a section 57 letter:** If you receive a section 57 notice, you should respond within the 28-day period and address the Department's concerns with relevant evidence.
* **Considering withdrawal:** In some cases, withdrawing the visa application may be an option. However, this can have consequences, so applicants should understand their position before making this decision.
* **Looking at other visa options:** Depending on your circumstances, another Australian visa pathway may be available.

## More Subclass 407 Refusals May Follow

The Department has warned that high numbers of refusals are expected to continue in the coming weeks and months. This is because a large number of applications currently on hand do not have a valid nomination.

With around **4,000 visa applications** still on hand where the nomination has been refused but the visa application has not yet been progressed, more decisions are expected.

Applicants with pending Subclass 407 applications should therefore keep a close eye on their nomination and visa application status and respond quickly to any Department correspondence.
