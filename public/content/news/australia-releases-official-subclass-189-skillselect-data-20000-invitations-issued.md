## What Changed in the Latest Invitation Round Compared to June 2026? 

**1.Total Invitations Increased:** One of the most significant developments in this round was the sharp increase in invitations issued. The 15 October 2026 round saw 20,000 invitations, double the 10,000 invitations issued in the June 2026 round, representing a 100% increase. 

**2.Minimum Invitation Scores Dropped for Many Occupations:** Compared with the previous round, several occupations across industries such as construction, manufacturing, engineering, telecommunications, automotive, economics and community services recorded significant reductions in minimum invitation scores. These include Airconditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic (80 to 65), Cabinetmaker (80 to 65), Telecommunications Engineer (95 to 85), Automotive Electrician (80 to 65), Economist (80 to 65) and Welfare Centre Manager (85 to 65). 

**3.Minimum Invitation Scores Increased for Some Occupations:** Compared with the previous round, some occupations across healthcare, medical and community services recorded increases in minimum invitation scores, indicating higher competition. These include Clinical Psychologist (75 to 90), Social Worker (75 to 80) and Surgeon (General) (85 to 90).
